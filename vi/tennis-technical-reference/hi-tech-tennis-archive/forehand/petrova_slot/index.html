The Pull and the Slot: Nadia Petrova

By Jeff Counts (Hi-Tech Tennis) · Hi-Tech Tennis 240 FPS Optical Archive · Original Lesson ↗

Jeff Counts (Hi-Tech Tennis) Analysis Note: Professional strokes maintain đầu vợt and eye stability for 2–3 frames (approx. 12ms) through impact, ensuring absolute repeatability without compensatory cổ tay manipulation.

One of my favorite forehands belongs to former WTA pro Nadia Petrova. She was one of the first pro players I ever filmed and she just blasted the ball with a powerful windshield wiper motion. It wasn't until later, when I filmed her from behind, that I could see how the key to her windshield wiper thuận tay was the way she PULLED the vợt through the slot very early in the cú đánh.

By rotating her hand backwards as she rotates her torso forwards, she is creating incredible trễ of the vai, arm, and vợt. In the still on the left, imagine someone is grabbing her vợt and pulling it backwards and to the left while she opens her torso towards the ball. This is a great exercise for you to try if you need to develop trễ in your thuận tay. As you start to tháo cuộn your vai towards the ball keep your vợt back near your right hông and twist the hand backwards. Then have your coach or someone else tug backwards on the tip of the vợt. Then pull the vợt, butt cap leading the way, through the slot. You should feel the vợt being pulled through the slot, creating tremendous trễ that can be released into the ball just before contact.

Once Petrova has pulled through the slot, and her khuỷu tay is in front of the plane of her body, she is ready to giải phóng the trễ and have the tip of the vợt travel inside out into the ball, generating a burst of tốc độ đầu vợt. As she releases the cổ tay, she continues to pull her arm forward to the ball. In fact, the giải phóng will also propel the arm forward into the ball as the vợt travels an inside out path. The vợt will "catch up" to the rest of the arm on contact, with the cổ tay going from a 90 degree layback to the cẳng tay to about a 30 degree góc.

In the animation on the right you can see how the tip of the vợt whips around into the ball. But the arm is still pulling forward as the vợt whips around. The vợt, which has lagged behind and has been pulled, is now going to "catch up" to the arm in an inside out motion that generates a burst of speed. Now that everything is back in sync, the arm can take over and lift and đường bóng the ball with the muscular windshield wiper motion.
