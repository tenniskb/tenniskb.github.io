Pendulums, Gravity, and Arcs

By Jeff Counts (Hi-Tech Tennis) · Hi-Tech Tennis 240 FPS Optical Archive · Original Lesson ↗

Gravity is the lực that lets you initiate your cú đánh effortlessly. By letting the double bend hitting structure "free fall", with the aid of gravity, you start the cú đánh off effortlessly and then use this "free" đà to help propel the rotational and pulling motions in the next stage of the cú đánh. When you do this properly, it's a wonderful feeling.

When we see top players cú đánh the ball, we always notice how "effortless" it all looks. One huge reason for this is that they use gravity to initate the downswing of the cú đánh, which gives them "effortless" đà. It's just like letting your bike "free fall" down a hill - picking up speed without having to pedal. Or when a roller coaster drops from the top of a hill and rapidly gains speed.

To take it further, we see that the entire đưa vợt ra sau and drop is actually one big arcing pendulum. Rather than "take the vợt back right away", the cú đánh actually starts with an easy upward and backward lift of the arm and vợt, As the vợt and arm lift upward and backward, the tip of the vợt points straight up in the air. And then the entire hitting structure begins to fall, just as a pendulum starts it effortless path downward after reaching its apex.

Watch how the tip of Nalbandian, Arazi, Safin and Falla's vợt point straight up in the air at the top of the đưa vợt ra sau before "free falling" downward.

This wonderful, effortless pendulum đưa vợt ra sau is, unfortunately, never achieved by the vast majority of players due to tennis teaching's obsession with "early chuẩn bị". "Get the vợt back sooner!!" is a command I hear other instructors over and over, and it prevents people from ever feeling the wonderful, smooth, effortless feeling of turning the arm and vợt into a pendulum, and tapping into the "free fall" that gravity provides to initate the downswing.

To get the pendulum effect, start the motion by beginning with the tip of the vợt pointing forward, in the chicken wing vị trí. Then smoothly and slowly begin the ascent up and back with the vợt. DON'T do this quickly. Think slow and smooth. Like a roller coasting chugging up to the top, before it free falls down, you want to be smooth and slow with this arcing pendulum like motion.
