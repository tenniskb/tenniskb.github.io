Sam Stosur: The Ultimate Heavy Kick Delivery

By Jeff Counts (Hi-Tech Tennis) · Hi-Tech Tennis 240 FPS Optical Archive · Original Lesson ↗

One of the weakest strokes I see on the pro tour is the female giao bóng. All sorts of technical problems have plagued female players' serving motions and for some, such as Elena Dementieva, these problems most certainly cost them grand slam titles. The range of technical problems run the gamut - from distorted rhythm to severe technical limitations - and constitue a surprising chink in an otherwise technically superior sport.

When a female player comes along with a technically superior giao bóng, it wields an enormous lợi thế. Venus and Serena, two of the best servers in the history of the game, have certainly reaped the rewards of having technically superior serving motions. Their lợi thế, of course, comes from sheer power. Venus holds the fastest female giao bóng ever recorded at 129mph while Serena holds the second at 128mph, while also holding the record for the most aces served by a female player at a Grand Slam event.

And that's why I find Samantha Stosur to be so intriguing. Not only does Sam have the powerful first giao bóng bomb, but she combines power and spin in a way that no other female player can. Her powerful first giao bóng (third fastest ever recorded) is matched by a ferocious kick giao bóng that literally pulls her opponent way off to the side of the sân. It's a shot you just don't see on the women's tour.

"Due to its power, kick and variety, Stosur's giao bóng is widely considered as one of the best on the women's tour. Her first giao bóng, however erratic, reaches more than 118 mph (190 km/h) on a regular basis. Her second, a high bouncing kick giao bóng, is highly rated as being the best second giao bóng in the women's game by media and players alike, and was thought to play a key role in her French Open success in 2009 and 2010. One thing that Stosur has changed in her game, thanks to coach David Taylor is the fact that her giao bóng has been less predictable with Stosur sometimes even using her kick giao bóng as first serves. Her good serving motion has helped her bóng trên cao smashes and she rarely misses those shots."
