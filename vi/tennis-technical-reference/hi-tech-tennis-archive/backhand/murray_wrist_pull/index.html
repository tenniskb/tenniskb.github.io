Both cổ tay Collapse and Pull Through Contact

Jeff Counts (Hi-Tech Tennis) Analysis Note: Professional strokes maintain đầu vợt and eye stability for 2–3 frames (approx. 12ms) through impact, ensuring absolute repeatability without compensatory cổ tay manipulation.

Things get quite interesting in this next stage. What we see with Andy, which we really see with all great two handers, is some fascinating cổ tay action that allows a pulling of the vợt forward into the slot.

So here is what is happening. First, like with the thuận tay, the tip of the vợt points "outside". In the animation to the right, note how the tip of Murray's vợt is pointing at the "J" in JP. As he starts to take his arms back, his cổ tay will fall backwards and downwards and then he will start to pull the butt cap forward through a mini "slot." This starts to bring the butt cap of the vợt forward toward the ball at góc. This butt cap is now an axis of xoay around which the hands and vợt can roi vung around, inside out, into contact.

Here the cổ tay of the right hand is just as interesting as the cổ tay on the left hand. In the picture below, you can see Andy's right cổ tay from three different angles. You can see how his right cổ tay is angled, or curved downwards. This cổ tay vị trí not only allows him to "pull the vợt through a slot", but it will giao lại him rotate his cổ tay outward into the ball.

As the right cổ tay collapses downward, the left cổ tay will cock back and downward. Together these cổ tay positions allow you to pull the vợt forward, butt cap first, "through a slot".

Here we can see what it looks like to be "in the slot" for the two handed trái tay. Andy's right cổ tay is curved backwards. His left cổ tay is laid back. The vợt is completely parallel to the side fence. Notice as well that his vợt face is slightly closed.

Being "in the slot" is a key vị trí for all ground strokes. It lets the vợt come "inside out" into the ball in a burst of angular moment that generates tremendous vợt speed coming into the ball.

In the next stage, the left arm will lift to contact while the hands rotate the vợt inside out into the ball in a burst of tốc độ đầu vợt.
