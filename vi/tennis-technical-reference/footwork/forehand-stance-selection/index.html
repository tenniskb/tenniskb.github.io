thuận tay tư thế Selection

thuận tay tư thế Selection is the decision — made within the split-second after recognising the incoming ball's speed, height, and landing zone — of which of the three thuận tay stances (open, semi-open, neutral) to adopt, each producing a different thăng bằng of linear and rotational đà through the contact zone.

The Three Stances

tư thế mở

Feet parallel to the đường cuối sân, wider than vai, đầu gối bent.

Weight đang nạp: The right leg (for right-handers) loads the majority of the năng lượng. The coiling of the hông and vai against each other — the X-Factor — generates the primary torque. The push-off is from the right leg upward and forward.

Best used for: - rộng, fast, or high balls where there is insufficient time to step through - Balls hit to the giữa of the sân at waist height or above - Any situation where immediate phục hồi vị trí is needed (the tư thế mở's rotational nạp-and-push-back is inherently faster to reset)

Power source: Almost entirely moment động lượng góc — rotational torque from the X-Factor stretch and corkscrew hông swivel.

phục hồi vị trí lợi thế: Because the outside leg loads and pushes back, phục hồi vị trí toward the centre requires no "untangling" steps. The legs remain in a vị trí ready to push in either direction.

tư thế bán mở

Feet staggered at approximately 45 degrees; left foot in front of right.

Weight đang nạp: More even between both legs than the tư thế mở, with the push coming primarily from the left (lead) leg upward and forward into the shot.

Best used for: - trong-ra ngoài and trong-vào trong forehands - Balls above the waist hit toward the giữa of the sân - When there is sufficient chuẩn bị time and maximum power is the priority

Power source: A combination of moment động lượng góc (rotational X-Factor) and linear đà — the staggered foot vị trí provides more forward weight distribution than the tư thế mở.

The hierarchy: If time permits, use semi-open rather than open — the lead foot vị trí produces greater forward weight distribution and therefore heavier contact.

Neutral (Closed) tư thế

Step directly forward with the front foot toward the lưới. Less xoay hông available.

Weight đang nạp: Clear forward chuyển trọng lượng — mass moves linearly through the điểm tiếp xúc.

Best used for: - ngắn balls requiring a "đường bóng through" the sân - Approach shots where forward đà into the lưới is intentional - Low balls in the giữa of the sân (Berdych / classic model)

Power source: Primarily linear đà — the step-through transfers body mass forward.

Trade-off: Slowest phục hồi vị trí of the three. The cross-body step requires untangling before lateral di chuyển can begin. Reserve for balls where forward approach follows.

The Foot Pivot Rule

On all open and semi-open forehands, the feet must not be "locked down." As the vai rotate aggressively through the shot, the feet pivot and elevate to allow full body xoay: - The left foot finishes pointing toward the target - The right foot ends resting lightly on the toe, heel off the ground

Locking the feet interrupts the rotational flow and places torsional stress on the đầu gối joints as the vai counter-rotate against fixed hông.

tư thế and Ball Height

Ball Height Preferred tư thế Reason
Above the vai Open or semi-open Rotational torque generates lift; rộng base handles bóng cao
Waist height Semi-open thăng bằng of power and control
Below the waist Neutral or semi-open Forward chuyển trọng lượng keeps the điểm tiếp xúc in front
Very low (phòng thủ) Open — wider base Centre of gravity lowered; wider base prevents collapsing
