# Bài viết 145: Tối Ưu Căng Dây - Sức Mạnh, Kiểm Soát, Spin & Độ Bền

> **CUE CHUYÊN GIA:** Căng dây (String Tension) = **Biến số quan trọng nhất ảnh hưởng cảm giác & hiệu suất** — Thay đổi 2-3 lbs = Thay đổi rõ rệt Power/Control/Spin/Comfort. Không có "Căng dây đúng" chung — Chỉ có "Căng dây đúng CHO BẠN, CHO HÔM NAY, CHO ĐIỀU KIỆN NÀY". Train "Tension Mastery" = Thạo biến số quan trọng nhất.

## 1. Tóm Tắt Điều Hành & Ý Định Thể Thao

Căng dây (String Tension) là **điều khiển trung tâm của hiệu suất vợt** — Nó quyết định: Bóng bay bao xa (Power), Bóng rơi đâu (Control), Bóng xoay bao nhiêu (Spin), Cảm giác tay như thế nào (Feel), Cổ tay/Chân tay có đau không (Comfort). Nhiều VĐV: Căng một mức cả năm, Copy pro, Không adjust theo thời tiết, Không track tension loss. Bài viết định nghĩa **String Tension Model**: (1) **Physics of Tension** (Vật lý căng dây) — Ball dwell time, String bed stiffness, Trampoline effect, Launch angle. (2) **Tension-Performance Curves** (Đường cong Căng-Hiệu suất) — Power ↓, Control ↑, Spin ↑ (to a point), Comfort ↓ as tension ↑. (3) **Individual Optimization** (Tối ưu cá nhân) — Testing protocol, Sweet spot finding, Preference mapping. (4) **Environmental & Situational Adjustment** (Điều chỉnh môi trường & tình huống) — Temp, Humidity, Altitude, Surface, Ball, Tournament vs Practice. Giao thức **"Tension Mastery Protocol"** biến căng dây từ đoán mò thành khoa học chính xác.

Ý định thể thao ba khía cạnh: thứ nhất, định nghĩa **Tension = Dynamic, Not Static** — Mất 10-25% trong 24h đầu, Tiếp tục mất dần, "Dead" khi >20% loss. Thứ hai, chỉ ra **Lower Tension Revolution** — Pro trend: 40-50 lbs (Thậm chí 35 lbs) với Poly cho Spin + Control + Comfort. "Căng cao = Control" là outdated. Thứ ba, cung cấp **bộ bài tập "Tension Mastery"** từ physics đến testing đến tournament protocol.

## 2. Nền Tảng Sinh Học Cơ - Vật Lý Học Căng Dây

### 2.1 String Bed Physics (Vật Học Mặt Dây)

```
STRING BED MECHANICS:
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  DWELL TIME (Thời Gian Cầu Ở Trên Dây):                                    │
│  ├── **Definition**: Thời gian bóng 접촉 (contact) với mặt dây (~3-5ms)    │
│  ├── **Lower Tension**: Dwell time ↑ (Softer bed, Deeper pocketing)      │
│  ├── **Higher Tension**: Dwell time ↓ (Stiffer bed, Shorter contact)     │
│  └── **Impact**: Dwell time ↑ → Spin potential ↑, Control ↑, Feel ↑      │
│                                                                             │
│  TRAMPOLINE EFFECT (Hiệu Ứng Nhảy Mùi):                                    │
│  ├── **Lower Tension**: String bed deflects more → Energy return ↑       │
│  │   → Ball speed ↑ (Power ↑), Launch angle ↑                             │
│  ├── **Higher Tension**: String bed deflects less → Energy return ↓      │
│  │   → Ball speed ↓ (Power ↓), Launch angle ↓ (Flatter)                 │
│  └── **Sweet Spot**: Optimal deflection for max speed + control          │
│                                                                             │
│  STRING BED STIFFNESS (Độ Cứng Mặt Dây - SBS):                            │
│  ├── **Primary Determinants**: Tension, String Material, Gauge, Pattern │
│  ├── **SBS ∝ Tension × Material Stiffness × (1/Gauge) × Pattern Density**│
│  ├── **Measurement**: Stringway ER300, Babolat RDC, Apps (Relative)      │
│  └── **Target SBS**: Individual (Based on swing speed, Style, Preference)│
│                                                                             │
│  LAUNCH ANGLE (Góc Khởi Hành):                                             │
│  ├── **Lower Tension**: Higher launch (Ball pockets deeper, Exits higher)│
│  ├── **Higher Tension**: Lower launch (Ball slides, Exits flatter)       │
│  ├── **Spin Interaction**: Topspin + High launch = Heavy ball, Kicks up  │
│  └── **Control**: Consistent launch angle = Predictable trajectory       │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 2.2 Tension-Performance Relationships (Mối Quan Hệ Căng-Hiệu Suất)

```
TENSION vs PERFORMANCE (General Trends):
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  POWER (Sức Mạnh):                                                          │
│  ├── **Trend**: Tension ↑ → Power ↓ (Linear-ish)                          │
│  ├── **Magnitude**: ~1-2% power change per 5 lbs tension change          │
│  ├── **Mechanism**: Trampoline effect ↓, Dwell time ↓, Energy return ↓   │
│  └── **Exception**: Very low tension (<40 lbs) → Power ↓ (Bottom out)    │
│                                                                             │
│  CONTROL (Kiểm Soát):                                                       │
│  ├── **Trend**: Tension ↑ → Control ↑ (To a point)                       │
│  ├── **Mechanism**: Lower launch angle, Less trampoline variability,     │
│  │   Shorter dwell = Less time for racket face change                     │
│  ├── **Diminishing Returns**: >60 lbs → Control plateaus, Feel harsh     │
│  └── **True Control**: **Consistency** (Low variance) > Absolute accuracy│
│                                                                             │
│  SPIN (Xoay):                                                               │
│  ├── **Complex**: Inverted U-curve                                        │
│  ├── **Low Tension**: Deep pocketing → String snapback ↑ → Spin ↑        │
│  ├── **Optimal**: ~45-55 lbs (Poly) → Max snapback + Dwell time          │
│  ├── **High Tension**: Stiff bed → Less snapback, Ball slides → Spin ↓   │
│  └── **Pattern**: Open pattern (16×19) shifts curve left (Lower optimal) │
│                                                                             │
│  COMFORT/FEEL (Cảm Giác/Thoải Mái):                                        │
│  ├── **Trend**: Tension ↑ → Comfort ↓, Feel ↑ (Crisp) → Then Feel ↓ (Boardy)│
│  ├── **Shock Transmission**: Tension ↑ → Impact shock ↑ → Arm stress ↑   │
│  ├── **Vibration**: Higher frequency, Higher amplitude at high tension   │
│  └── **Sweet Spot Feel**: Optimal tension = "Pocketing" sensation        │
│                                                                             │
│  DURABILITY (Độ Bền):                                                       │
│  ├── **Trend**: Tension ↑ → Durability ↓ (Higher stress on strings)      │
│  ├── **Notching**: Higher tension → Faster notching at crosses           │
│  ├── **Breakage**: Higher tension → Higher peak stress → Earlier break   │
│  └── **Tension Loss**: Higher initial → Faster absolute loss (But % similar)│
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 2.3 Material-Specific Tension Behavior (Hành Vi Căng Theo Chất Liệu)

```
MATERIAL-SPECIFIC TENSION CHARACTERISTICS:
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  NATURAL GUT:                                                               │
│  ├── **Optimal Range**: 52-60 lbs (Full), 50-56 lbs (Hybrid mains)       │
│  ├── **Tension Loss**: Low (5-10% first 24h, Then stable)                │
│  ├── **Feel**: Best pocketing, Plushe, Arm-friendly                      │
│  ├── **Power**: Highest at any tension                                    │
│  └── **Cost/Durability**: Low durability, High cost → Hybrid crosses     │
│                                                                             │
│  MULTIFILAMENT:                                                             │
│  ├── **Optimal Range**: 50-56 lbs (Full), 48-54 lbs (Hybrid crosses)     │
│  ├── **Tension Loss**: Moderate (10-15% first 24h, Continues slowly)     │
│  ├── **Feel**: Gut-like, Good comfort, Less crisp                        │
│  └── **Best For**: Arm issues, Comfort seekers, Hybrid crosses           │
│                                                                             │
│  POLYESTER (Poly):                                                          │
│  ├── **Optimal Range**: **40-52 lbs** (Revolution: Much lower than old)  │
│  ├── **Tension Loss**: **High** (15-25% first 24h, Continues → "Dead")   │
│  ├── **Feel**: Crisp → Boardy (When dead), Low power at high tension     │
│  ├── **Spin**: **Max at 45-50 lbs** (Snapback optimal)                   │
│  ├── **Control**: Excellent at low tension (Due to stiffness, Not tension)│
│  └── **Hybrid Mains**: 44-50 lbs (With Gut/Multi crosses 2-4 lbs higher) │
│                                                                             │
│  NYLON / SYNTHETIC GUT:                                                     │
│  ├── **Optimal Range**: 52-58 lbs                                         │
│  ├── **Tension Loss**: Moderate-High (10-20%)                            │
│  ├── **Feel**: Average, Crisp initially, Then mushy                      │
│  └── **Best For**: Recreational, Budget, Hybrid crosses (Budget)         │
│                                                                             │
│  KEVLAR / ARAMID:                                                           │
│  ├── **Optimal Range**: 50-60 lbs (Very stiff, Low tension feels boardy) │
│  ├── **Tension Loss**: Very low (But feels dead fast)                    │
│  └── **Use**: Extreme durability only, Hybrid crosses, Arm risk HIGH     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 3. Thực Hành Kỹ Thuật Từng Bước

### Giai Đoạn 1: Baseline Establishment (Thiết Lập Cơ Sở)

**Bài tập 1: "Current Tension Audit - Measure, Not Guess" (Kiểm Toán Căng Dây Hiện Tại - Đo, Không Đoán)"
- **Measurements**:
  1. **Reference Tension**: What you asked stringer for (Target).
  2. **Actual Tension** (Stringmeter/ER300/App): 
     - **Fresh** (Within 2h of stringing): Should be ~90-95% of reference (Stringer machine variance).
     - **24h Later**: ~85-90% of reference (Initial relaxation).
     - **Weekly**: Track decay curve.
  3. **String Bed Stiffness (SBS)**: ER300 or Babolat RDC (If available) — Absolute reference.
  4. **String Specs**: Material, Gauge, Pattern, Date strung, Hours played.
- **Subjective Baseline**: Rate (1-10) Power, Control, Spin, Comfort, Feel, Launch angle, Overall.
- **Document**: Photo of string bed, Stringer receipt, All measurements.
- 1 audit. KPI: Baseline documented, Actual vs Target known, Decay curve started.

**Bài tập 2: "String Decay Curve Mapping - Your Strings, Your Timeline" (Bản Đồ Đường Cong Suy Giảm Dây - Dây Của Bạn, Thời Gian Của Bạn)"
- **Protocol**:
  1. **Fresh string job** (Your current optimal setup).
  2. **Daily Stringmeter reading** (Same time, Same method, Same 3 spots: Center, 3&9, Throat).
  3. **Log**: Date, Hours played, Reading (Avg), Subjective feel (1-10), Performance notes.
  4. **Duration**: Until you cut it (Break or "Dead" feel).
- **Analysis**:
  - **Initial Drop** (0-24h): % loss (Typical: Poly 15-25%, Multi 10-15%, Gut 5-10%).
  - **Linear Phase**: % loss per hour (Poly ~0.5-1%/h, Multi ~0.3-0.5%/h, Gut ~0.1-0.2%/h).
  - **"Dead" Threshold**: When feel/performance unacceptable (Typically >20% total loss).
  - **Total Life**: Hours to threshold.
- **Result**: **Your Personal Decay Model** → Predicts optimal restring window.
- Per string setup (3+ setups). KPI: Decay curves mapped, Restring schedule personalized, No surprises.

**Bài tập 3: "Stringmeter Calibration & Consistency" (Hiệu Chuẩn Stringmeter & Nhất Quán)"
- **Tool Selection**: Stringway ER300 (Gold standard), Babolat RDC (Pro), Gamma Stringmeter (Budget), Apps (Relative only).
- **Calibration**:
  1. **Reference Racket**: Known tension (Freshly strung on calibrated machine).
  2. **Measure 10x**: Same spots, Same technique → Calculate mean, SD.
  3. **Consistency Check**: SD < 0.5 lbs = Good, < 1.0 lbs = Acceptable.
  4. **Technique Standardization**: 
     - Same 3 measurement points.
     - Same force application (Tool dependent).
     - Same temperature (Strings expand/contract).
     - Same person (Inter-rater reliability low).
- **Weekly Protocol**: Same day, Same time, Same conditions, Same tool, Same person.
- Ongoing. KPI: Tool calibrated, Technique consistent, Data reliable, Decisions confident.

### Giai Đoạn 2: Tension Testing Protocol (Giao Thức Thử Nghiệm Căng Dây)

**Bài tập 4: "Scientific Tension Testing - The 3-Point Protocol" (Thử Nghiệm Căng Dây Khoa Học - Giao Thức 3 Điểm)"
- **Setup**: 
  - **Same Racket** (Or identical rackets).
  - **Same String** (Fresh each test).
  - **Same Stringer** (Consistency).
  - **Same Conditions** (Indoor, Same balls, Same time of day).
- **Test Tensions**: **Current - 4 lbs, Current, Current + 4 lbs** (e.g., 44, 48, 52 lbs).
  - If unsure of current: **40, 48, 56 lbs** (Wide range for Poly).
- **Test Session** (Each tension, 30-45min, Fresh string job):
  1. **Warm-up** (5min): Feel, Comfort, Rhythm.
  2. **Groundstrokes** (15min): 
     - Cross-court depth control (Target zones).
     - Down-line accuracy.
     - Spin production (High, Medium, Flat).
     - **Metrics**: Radar speed, Ball tracking (Spin RPM, Launch angle), Target %.
  3. **Serve** (10min): 
     - 1st serve: 10 flat, 10 slice, 10 kick → Speed, Placement %, Spin.
     - 2nd serve: 10 kick/slice → Consistency, Kick height.
  4. **Volleys/Transition** (5min): Touch, Stability, Control.
  5. **Match Play** (10min): Points, Pressure, Decision quality.
  6. **Comfort Check**: Arm feel (During/After 30min), Vibration perception.
- **Subjective Ratings** (1-10 each): Power, Control, Spin, Comfort, Feel, Launch, Overall.
- **Decision Matrix**: Weighted scoring (Based on player priorities) → Winner.
- Per string material (3+ materials). KPI: Protocol executed, Data rich, Clear winner, Confidence high.

**Bài tập 5: "Spin-Specific Tension Optimization - Snapback Focus" (Tối Ưu Căng Dây Cho Spin - Tập Trung Snapback)"
- **Concept**: **Snapback** (Mains deflect sideways → Snap back → Spin) is tension-dependent.
- **Test** (Poly strings, Open pattern 16×19/18×20):
  - **Tensions**: 38, 42, 46, 50, 54 lbs (5 points, 4 lbs increments).
  - **Metric**: **Spin RPM** (Radar/TrackMan/High-speed video) on standardized topspin FH.
  - **Control Metric**: Depth consistency (Target zone %).
  - **Comfort Metric**: Arm feel (1-10).
- **Analysis**: Plot Spin vs Tension → **Inverted U** → Peak = Optimal spin tension.
- **Verify**: At peak spin tension, is Control acceptable? Comfort acceptable?
- **Result**: **Personal Spin-Optimal Tension** (Usually 44-50 lbs for Poly).
- Per poly string (3+ polys). KPI: Spin curve mapped, Peak identified, Trade-offs clear.

**Bài tập 6: "Hybrid Tension Differential - Mains vs Crosses" (Chênh Lệch Căng Dây Hybrid - Dây Dọc vs Dây Ngang)"
- **Principle**: **Crosses lower than mains** → Softer pocketing, More mains deflection → More snapback/spin.
- **Standard Differentials**:
  - **Gut Mains / Poly Crosses**: Mains 2-4 lbs **HIGHER** than crosses (Gut holds tension, Poly loses fast).
  - **Poly Mains / Gut Crosses**: Mains 2-4 lbs **LOWER** than crosses (Poly stiff, Gut soft).
  - **Poly Mains / Multi Crosses**: Mains 2-4 lbs LOWER (Multi softer).
  - **Same Material**: Usually same tension (Or crosses 1-2 lbs lower for pocketing).
- **Testing Protocol**:
  - **Base**: Optimal single tension found (e.g., 48 lbs Poly).
  - **Test**: Mains 46 / Crosses 50, Mains 48 / Crosses 48, Mains 50 / Crosses 46.
  - **Metrics**: Spin, Pocketing feel, Control, Comfort, Tension stability (Crosses lose faster).
- **Pro Trend**: **Poly mains LOWER than Gut crosses** (e.g., 44/48, 46/50) for max spin + feel.
- Per hybrid combo. KPI: Differential optimized, Tension stability managed, Performance peak.

### Giai Đoạn 3: Environmental & Situational Adjustment (Điều Chỉnh Môi Trường & Tình Huống)

**Bài tập 7: "Temperature & Humidity Adjustment Calculator" (Máy Tính Điều Chỉnh Nhiệt Độ & Độ Ẩm)"
- **Physics**: 
  - **Hot**: Strings expand → Tension drops → Ball flies more → **Lower target tension**.
  - **Cold**: Strings contract → Tension rises → Ball heavier → **Higher target tension**.
  - **Humid**: Nylon/Multi absorb moisture → Stretch → Tension drops. Poly hydrophobic.
- **Adjustment Rules** (Per 10°F / 5°C change from baseline 70°F/21°C):
  - **Temperature**: **±2 lbs per 10°F** (Hot = Lower, Cold = Higher).
  - **Humidity**: **+1-2 lbs if >70% RH** (For Nylon/Multi), Poly = No change.
  - **Combined**: Hot + Humid = -3 to -4 lbs. Cold + Dry = +3 to +4 lbs.
- **Calculator** (Simple card/App):
  - Input: Baseline tension, Current temp, Current humidity, String material.
  - Output: Adjusted target tension.
- **Validation**: Test at extremes (Hot vs Cold day) → Confirm feel/performance.
- Ongoing. KPI: Calculator accurate, Adjustments automatic, Performance consistent across conditions.

**Bài tập 8: "Altitude & Ball Adjustment" (Điều Chỉnh Cao Độ & Quả Bóng)"
- **Altitude** (Every 1000m / 3000ft above sea level):
  - **Air density ↓** → Ball flies further, Less drag → **Lower tension 4-6 lbs**.
  - **Ball**: High-altitude balls (Pressurized less) → Different feel → Test.
  - **Example**: Sea level 48 lbs → Denver (1600m) 42-44 lbs → Mexico City (2200m) 40-42 lbs.
- **Ball Type** (ITF Approved, But different):
  - **Regular Duty** (Clay/Indoor): Softer felt, Fluffs up → **Lower tension 2 lbs**.
  - **Extra Duty** (Hard): Harder felt, Lasts longer → **Standard tension**.
  - **High Altitude**: Less pressure → **Lower tension 2-4 lbs**.
  - **Brand Differences**: Wilson (Lively), Dunlop (Heavy), Babolot (Spinny), Penn (Durable) → Micro-adjust ±1-2 lbs.
- **Tournament Protocol**: 
  - **Practice day**: Test tournament balls at tournament altitude → Fine-tune.
  - **Stringer brief**: "Tournament balls, Altitude X, Target Y lbs."
- Per tournament/travel. KPI: Adjustments correct, First match feel dialed, No mid-tournament restring.

**Bài tập 9: "Surface-Specific Tension Strategy" (Chiến Lược Căng Dây Theo Bề Mặt)"
- **Surface Physics**:
  - **Clay**: Ball slows, Bounces higher, More spin → **Lower tension 2-4 lbs** (More pocketing, Spin).
  - **Grass**: Ball fast, Low bounce, Skids → **Higher tension 2-4 lbs** (Control, Flatter launch).
  - **Hard**: Medium, Consistent → **Baseline tension**.
  - **Indoor**: Fast, Consistent, No wind → **Baseline +2 lbs** (Control premium).
- **Transition Protocol** (2 weeks before surface change):
  - **Week 1**: Practice at new surface tension (50% sessions).
  - **Week 2**: 100% at new surface tension.
  - **Tournament**: Arrive early, Confirm with tournament balls.
- **Multi-Surface Tournament** (Rare but happens): 
  - **Separate rackets** per surface (Strung differently).
  - **Quick adjust**: If same racket, Stringer on-site, 30min turnaround.
- Per surface season. KPI: Tension matched to surface, Transition smooth, Performance consistent.

### Giai Đoạn 4: Tournament & Maintenance Protocol (Giao Thức Giải Đấu & Bảo Trì)

**Bài tập 10: "Tournament Stringing Protocol - Zero Surprises" (Giao Thức Cài Dây Giải Đấu - Không Bất Ngờ)"
- **Pre-Tournament** (1 week before):
  - **Confirm**: Optimal tension for tournament conditions (Temp, Humidity, Altitude, Balls, Surface).
  - **Stringer**: Book trusted stringer (On-site or local), Communicate specs clearly.
  - **Rackets**: 4-6 identical (Same model, Specs, Customization).
- **Stringing Schedule**:
  - **Match Day -1 (Evening)**: 2 rackets at **Match Tension**.
  - **Match Day Morning**: 2 rackets at **Match Tension** (Freshest feel).
  - **Backup Tensions**: 1 racket at -2 lbs, 1 at +2 lbs (Conditions change).
  - **Total**: 4-6 fresh string jobs per match day (Pro), 2-3 (Advanced).
- **Stringer Communication Sheet**:
  ```
  Player: [Name] | Racket: [Model] | Specs: [Weight/Balance/SW]
  String: [Mains Material/Gauge] / [Crosses Material/Gauge]
  Tensions: Match: [X] lbs | Backup -2: [X-2] | Backup +2: [X+2]
  Pattern: [16×19] | Knots: [Standard/ATW] | Prestretch: [Yes/No/%]
  Notes: [Cut old / Save grommets / Specific stringer]
  ```
- **Post-Match**: 
  - Used rackets → Stringer immediately (Next day fresh).
  - Tension reading (Stringmeter) → Log decay.
- Per tournament. KPI: Zero string issues, Fresh feel every match, Backups ready, Log complete.

**Bài tập 11: "In-Match Tension Awareness - Feel the Change" (Nhận Biết Căng Dây Trong Trận - Cảm Nhận Thay Đổi)"
- **Signs of Tension Loss During Match**:
  - **Power ↑ unexpectedly**: Ball flying long (Trampoline ↑ as tension drops).
  - **Control ↓**: Unforced errors ↑, Depth variability ↑.
  - **Spin ↓**: Ball not kicking, Flatter trajectory.
  - **Feel change**: "Mushy", "Boardy", "Dead", "No pocketing".
  - **Launch angle ↑**: Ball flying higher over net.
- **In-Match Adjustments** (Mental + Tactical, Not physical):
  - **Swing adjustment**: More spin, Lower target, Shorter swing.
  - **Tactical**: More margin, Less risk, Play percentages.
  - **Racket switch**: At changeover (If backup racket fresh).
- **Training for Awareness**: 
  - **Practice with "Dead" strings** (Old racket) → Learn to adapt.
  - **Blind tension test**: Play with unknown tension → Guess / Adapt.
- Ongoing. KPI: Awareness high, Adaptation effective, Racket switches timely, Mental stable.

**Bài tập 12: "Restring Decision Framework - Data-Driven" (Khung Quyết Định Cài Lại Dây - Dựa Trên Dữ Liệu)"
- **Decision Triggers** (Any ONE triggers restring):
  1. **Tension Loss >20%** from fresh reference (Stringmeter).
  2. **Hours Played**: Poly 20-30h, Multi/Gut 30-40h, Syn Gut 15-20h.
  3. **Feel Threshold**: "Dead", "Boardy", "Unpredictable", "Arm discomfort".
  4. **Visual**: Notching >50% depth, Fraying, Sawing at crosses.
  5. **Performance**: Consistent drop in spin/control/comfort over 3+ sessions.
  6. **Calendar**: Before tournament (Fresh), After tournament (If heavy play).
- **Proactive Schedule** (Preventive > Reactive):
  - **Weekly Stringmeter** → Predict "Dead" date → Schedule 1-2 days before.
  - **Tournament Calendar** → Work backward → Fresh for each event.
  - **Heavy Weeks** → Mid-week restring if >10h play.
- **Stringer Partnership**: 
  - **Standing order**: "Restring every Monday/Thursday" or "Call when needed".
  - **Consistency**: Same stringer, Same machine, Same technique = Consistent tension.
- Ongoing. KPI: Zero reactive restrings, Performance consistent, Stringer partnership strong.
