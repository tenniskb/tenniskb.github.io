Bài viết 139: Phòng Chấn Thương - Prehab, Quản Trị Tải & Sàng Lọc Chuyển Động

CUE CHUYÊN GIA: Phòng chấn thương (Injury Prevention) = Không phải "Không bị thương" — Là Giảm nguy cơ, Giảm mức độ, Giảm thời gian vắng mặt. Prehab = Rehab trước khi cần. Load Management = Bảo vệ qua lập trình. Screening = Sớm phát hiện. Train "Durability" = Sự bền bỉ.

1. Tóm Tắt Điều Hành & Ý Định Thể Thao

Phòng chấn thương (Injury Prevention) là đầu tư an toàn hiệu suất cao nhất trong tennis. Chấn thương khiến VĐV vắng mặt trung bình 2-6 tuần/năm, Chi phí y tế cao, Hiệu suất giảm, Career rút ngắn. Tennis có tỷ lệ chấn thương cao do: Bản chất đơn phương, Lặp lại hàng triệu lần, Cường độ cao, Multi-surface, Du lịch liên tục. Bài viết định nghĩa Tennis Injury Prevention Model: (1) Epidemiology & Risk Factors (Dịch tễ học & Yếu tố nguy cơ) — Where, When, Why, Who. (2) Prehab Programming (Lập trình Prehab) — Daily, Weekly, Periodized, Individualized. (3) Load Management (Quản trị tải) — Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio (ACWR), Spikes, Floor/Ceiling. (4) Movement Screening & Monitoring (Sàng lọc & Giám sát chuyển động) — Quarterly screens, Daily checks, Red flags. Giao thức "Durability Protocol" xây dựng vận động viên tennis khỏe, bền, ít chấn thương.

Ý định thể thao ba khía cạnh: thứ nhất, định nghĩa Prehab ≠ Rehab — Prehab = Proactive, Daily, Integrated — Không đợi đau mới làm. Thứ hai, chỉ ra Load Management = Primary Prevention — Training load errors = #1 injury cause. ACWR, Spike detection, Gradual progression. Thứ ba, cung cấp bộ bài tập "Durability Mastery" từ screening đến prehab đến load management.

2. Nền Tảng Sinh Học Cơ - Dịch Tế Học & Cơ Chế

2.1 Tennis Injury Epidemiology (Dịch Tế Học Chấn Thương Tennis)

```

TENNIS INJURY EPIDEMIOLOGY:

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

│ INJURY RATES: │

│ ├── Incidence: 1.5-3.0 injuries/1000h play (Higher in competition) │

│ ├── Prevalence: 20-30% athletes injured at any time │

│ ├── Time Loss: Median 7-14 days, Severe >28 days (15-20%) │

│ ├── Recurrence: 15-30% (Same injury within 12 months) │

│ └── Career Impact: 10-15% early retirement due to injury │

│ │

│ BODY REGION DISTRIBUTION: │

│ ├── Lower Extremity: 40-50% (Ankle, Knee, Hip/Thigh, Foot) │

│ │ ├── Ankle sprain (Lateral) — #1 acute injury │

│ │ ├── Patellar tendinopathy (Jumper's knee) — #1 overuse │

│ │ ├── Hip/Groin (Adductor, Labral, FAI) — High in elite │

│ │ └── Foot (Plantar fasciitis, Stress fracture, Turf toe) │

│ ├── Trunk/Spine: 20-25% (Lumbar, Thoracic, Abdominal) │

│ │ ├── Lumbar stress fracture (Pars) — Youth/High volume │

│ │ ├── Lumbar disc/facet — Rotation + Extension load │

│ │ └── Abdominal strain (Oblique) — Serve/rotation │

│ ├── Upper Extremity: 25-35% (Shoulder, Elbow, Wrist, Hand) │

│ │ ├── Shoulder (Impingement, Rotator cuff, Labral, Instability) │

│ │ ├── Elbow (Lateral epicondylitis - Tennis elbow, Medial - Golfer's)│

│ │ ├── Wrist (TFCC, Tendinopathy, Stress fracture) │

│ │ └── Hand (Blisters, Fractures) │

│ └── Head/Neck: <5% (Concussion rare, Neck strain) │

│ │

│ MECHANISM: │

│ ├── Acute/Traumatic: 30-40% (Landing, COD, Collision, Slip) │

│ ├── Overuse/Gradual: 60-70% (Repetitive load, Insufficient recovery)│

│ ├── Contact: <5% (Ball, Racquet, Net, Opponent) │

│ └── Non-contact: 95% (Preventable via load/movement management) │

│ │

│ RISK FACTORS (Yếu Tố Nguy Cơ): │

│ ├── Intrinsic: Age, Sex, Previous injury, Anatomy, Mobility deficits,│

│ │ Strength asymmetry, Movement patterns, Maturation (Youth) │

│ ├── Extrinsic: Training load (Volume, Intensity, Frequency), │

│ │ Surface change, Equipment (Shoes, Racquet, Strings), Schedule, │

│ │ Travel, Recovery (Sleep, Nutrition), Coaching │

│ └── Modifiable vs Non-modifiable: Focus on MODIFIABLE │

└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

```

2.2 Load Management - ACWR (Tỷ Lệ Tải Cấp Tính:Mãn Tính)

```

ACUTE:CHRONIC WORKLOAD RATIO (ACWR):

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

│ DEFINITION: ACWR = Acute Load (1 week) / Chronic Load (4-6 week avg) │

│ ├── Acute: Current week training load (sRPE × Duration, or GPS) │

│ ├── Chronic: Rolling 4-6 week average (Fitness/Fatigue baseline) │

│ └── Ratio: 1.0 = Maintenance, >1.0 = Loading, <1.0 = Deloading │

│ │

│ INJURY RISK ZONES (Gabbett et al.): │

│ ├── Sweet Spot: 0.8 - 1.3 (Lowest injury risk) │

│ ├── Danger Zone: >1.5 (High injury risk — Spike) │

│ ├── Under-preparation: <0.8 (Detraining, Low resilience) │

│ └── Optimal Range: 1.0 - 1.2 (Progressive loading) │

│ │

│ CALCULATION METHODS: │

│ ├── Session-RPE: sRPE (1-10) × Duration (min) = AU (Arbitrary Units)│

│ ├── GPS/Tracking: High-speed distance, Accel/Decel, PlayerLoad │

│ ├── Tennis-Specific: Court hours × Intensity factor (1-3) │

│ └── Coupled: Internal (sRPE, HR) + External (GPS, Court time) │

│ │

│ LOAD MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLES: │

│ ├── Weekly Progression: ≤10-15% increase (Chronic load) │

│ ├── Spike Avoidance: No single week >1.5 ACWR │

│ ├── Floor & Ceiling: Minimum chronic load (Floor), Maximum safe │

│ │ acute load (Ceiling) — Individualized │

│ ├── Deload Weeks: Every 3-4 weeks (ACWR 0.6-0.8) │

│ ├── Return-to-Play: Gradual ACWR build (0.8 → 1.0 → 1.2) │

│ └── Tournament Weeks: Match load counted, Training adjusted │

└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

```

2.3 Prehab Philosophy (Triết Lý Prehab)

```

PREHAB FRAMEWORK:

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

│ PREHAB = PROACTIVE REHAB: │

│ ├── Daily (5-10min): Tissue prep, Activation, Motor control │

│ ├── Weekly (2-3x 20-30min): Targeted strength, Mobility, Stability │

│ ├── Periodized: Off-season build → In-season maintain → Peak sharpen│

│ ├── Individualized: Based on Screen, History, Demands, Deficits │

│ └── Integrated: Part of warm-up, Cool-down, Strength, On-court │

│ │

│ TENNIS PREHAB PRIORITIES (Tissue-Specific): │

│ ├── Shoulder: Rotator cuff (ER/IR), Scapular control, Posterior capsule│

│ ├── Elbow: Wrist extensor/flexor eccentric, Pronation/supination │

│ ├── Wrist/Hand: Flexor/extensor strength, Grip, Proprioception │

│ ├── Trunk: Anti-extension/rotation/lateral flexion, Oblique strength│

│ ├── Hip/Groin: Adductor strength (Copenhagen), Glute med/max, Hip IR│

│ ├── Knee: Quad/HS eccentric, Patellar tendon (Heavy slow resistance)│

│ ├── Ankle/Foot: Calf complex (Gastroc/Soleus), Intrinsic foot, Proprioception│

│ └── Posterior Chain: Hamstring (Nordic), Glute, Calf, Thoracic │

│ │

│ DOSE-RESPONSE: │

│ ├── Frequency: Daily (Micro-dose) > Weekly (Large dose) │

│ ├── Intensity: Low-moderate (Quality, Control, End-range) │

│ ├── Volume: 5-15min daily, 20-30min 2-3x/week targeted │

│ ├── Progression: Range → Control → Load → Speed → Complexity │

│ └── Consistency: 365 days/year — Non-negotiable habit │

└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

```

3. Thực Hành Kỹ Thuật Từng Bước

Giai Đoạn 1: Screening & Risk Profiling (Sàng Lọc & Hồ Sơ Nguy Cơ)

**Bài tập 1: "Comprehensive Movement Screen - Quarterly" (Sàng Lọc Chuyển Động Toàn Diện - Hàng Quý)"

  • Tests (Video + Measurements):
  • 1. Mobility: Ankle DF, Hip Flex/IR/ER/Abd, Thoracic Rot/Ext, Shoulder Flex/ER/IR, Wrist Flex/Ext, Cervical Rot.
  • 2. Stability/Motor Control: SL Balance (EO/EC), SL Squat, Bird-Dog, Push-up, Core endurance (Plank/Side/Back).
  • 3. Strength/Asymmetry: SL Hop, SL RDL, CMJ, Push-up, Pull-up, 1-Arm Press/Row, Farmer Carry.
  • 4. Tennis-Specific: Split step landing, Serve motion (Video), FH/BH motion (Video), COD 5-0-5.
  • 5. Tissue Health: Palpation (Tendons, Muscle bellies), Joint play, Neural tension (SLUMP, ULNT).
  • Scoring: Green/Yellow/Red per test. Red = Immediate intervention.
  • Risk Profile: Combine Screen + History + Load + Demographics = Individual Risk Score.
  • Quarterly. KPI: Screen complete, Risk profile updated, Intervention plan current.

**Bài tập 2: "Daily Readiness Check - Red Flags" (Kiểm Tra Sẵn Sàng Hàng Ngày - Cờ Đỏ)"

  • Morning (2min, Part of monitoring):
  • Pain: Any joint/muscle pain >2/10? Location, Type, Since when?
  • Stiffness: Morning stiffness >30min? Which joints?
  • Swelling: Visible swelling? Effusion?
  • Giving way: Instability episodes? Buckling?
  • Night pain: Pain disturbing sleep?
  • Functional: Unable to perform usual activities?
  • Decision Tree:
  • No flags: Green → Full training.
  • 1-2 mild flags: Yellow → Modify (Reduce load, Add prehab, Monitor).
  • Any red flag: Red → Medical referral, No training until cleared.
  • Daily. KPI: Check completed, Flags caught early, Appropriate action taken.

**Bài tập 3: "Load Tracking - ACWR Daily/Weekly" (Theo Dõi Tải - ACWR Hàng Ngày/Tuần)"

  • Daily: Session sRPE × Duration = Daily Load.
  • Weekly: Sum Daily Loads = Weekly Load (Acute).
  • Chronic: Rolling 4-week average of Weekly Loads.
  • ACWR Calculation: Weekly Load / Chronic Load.
  • Dashboard: Plot Acute, Chronic, ACWR weekly. Flag >1.5 or <0.8.
  • Spike Detection: Single session >2x daily average? Week >1.5 ACWR?
  • Adjustment Rules:
  • ACWR >1.5 → Next week load = Chronic × 1.0 (Stabilize).
  • ACWR <0.8 × 2 weeks → Progressive reload (10%/week).
  • Spike session → Next session -30% volume.
  • Daily/Weekly. KPI: ACWR tracked, Spikes caught, Adjustments made, Injury risk minimized.

Giai Đoạn 2: Daily Prehab Integration (Tích Hợp Prehab Hàng Ngày)

**Bài tập 4: "Daily Prehab Micro-Dose - 5-10min" (Prehab Vi Liều Hàng Ngày - 5-10phút)"

  • Morning (Integrated in Mobility Routine - Bài 137):
  • Feet/Ankle: Toe yoga 1min, Calf raise 2×15, SL balance 30s/side.
  • Hip/Groin: Copenhagen 2×15s/side, Clamshell 2×15, Hip CARs.
  • Core: Dead Bug 2×10, Side Plank 2×20s, Bird-Dog 2×10.
  • Scapular/Shoulder: Scap Push-up 2×15, Band Pull-apart 2×20, ER/IR 2×15.
  • Wrist: Flex/Ext 2×15, Pron/Sup 2×15, Grip squeeze 2×10.
  • Pre-Tennis (Integrated in Warm-up):
  • Activation: Glute med (Band walk 2×10), Core (Plank 30s), Scap (Y-T-W-L 1×8).
  • Tendon Prep: Light hops 2×10, Shadow swings progressive.
  • Post-Tennis (Integrated in Cool-down):
  • Eccentric: Nordic 2×5, SL RDL 2×8, Wrist eccentric 2×10.
  • Mobility: Priority joints (From screen) 30-60s hold.
  • Daily. KPI: Compliance >90%, Tissue prep consistent, No accumulated stiffness.

**Bài tập 5: "Weekly Targeted Prehab Sessions - 2-3x/week" (Phiên Prehab Nhắm Mục Tiêu Tuần - 2-3x/tuần)"

  • Session A (Lower Body Focus - 25min):
  • 1. Ankle/Foot: SL Calf Raise 3×12 (Straight/Bent knee), Toe Yoga, Short Foot 3×30s.
  • 2. Knee: Spanish Squat 3×30s (Patellar tendon), SL RDL 3×8, Step-down 3×8/side.
  • 3. Hip/Groin: Copenhagen 3×20s/side, SL Hip Thrust 3×10, Lateral Lunge 3×8/side.
  • 4. Hamstring: Nordic 3×5 (Eccentric focus), Slider Curl 3×8.
  • Session B (Upper Body Focus - 25min):
  • 1. Scapular: Scap Push-up 3×15, Serratus Wall Slide 3×10, Y-T-W-L 2×8.
  • 2. Rotator Cuff: ER/IR 3×15 (Band/Cable), 90/90 ER 3×12, IR 3×12.
  • 3. Elbow/Wrist: Wrist Ext/ Flex Eccentric 3×10, Pron/Sup 3×12, Grip 3×30s.
  • 4. Posterior Cuff/Scap: Face Pull 3×15, External Rotation Press 3×10.
  • Session C (Trunk/Integration - 20min):
  • 1. Anti-Extension: Dead Bug 3×10, Plank 3×45s, Ab Wheel 3×8.
  • 2. Anti-Rotation: Pallof Press 3×10/side, Bird-Dog 3×10/side.
  • 3. Anti-Lateral: Side Plank 3×30s/side, Suitcase Carry 3×20m/side.
  • 4. Integration: Turkish Get-up 2×3/side, Med Ball Rotational 3×6/side.
  • Progression: Monthly increase difficulty (Range → Load → Speed → Unstable).
  • Ongoing. KPI: Sessions completed, Progression tracked, Deficits improving.

Giai Đoạn 3: Load Management Systems (Hệ Thống Quản Trị Tải)

**Bài tập 6: "Individual Load Profile - Floor, Ceiling, Sweet Spot" (Hồ Sơ Tải Cá Nhân - Sàn, Trần, Điểm Ngọt)"

  • Establish Baseline (4-6 weeks consistent training):
  • Chronic Load: Rolling 4-week avg (sRPE × Duration).
  • Session Types: High (>600 AU), Moderate (300-600), Low (<300).
  • Weekly Pattern: High/Low/Mod distribution.
  • Determine Individual Zones:
  • Floor: Minimum chronic load for resilience (Typically 60-70% avg chronic).
  • Ceiling: Maximum safe acute load (Typically Chronic × 1.3-1.5).
  • Sweet Spot: ACWR 1.0-1.2, Weekly progression 5-10%.
  • Document: Individual Load Profile Card (Updated monthly).
  • Quarterly. KPI: Profile established, Zones respected, Load decisions data-driven.

**Bài tập 7: "Surface Transition Load Management" (Quản Trị Tải Chuyển Đổi Bề Mặt)"

  • Surface Change Protocol (Clay ↔ Grass ↔ Hard ↔ Indoor):
  • 2-3 Weeks Before: Gradual exposure (1 session → 2 → 3 → Full).
  • Load Adjustment: Reduce overall load 20% during transition (Novel stimulus).
  • Footwear: Break in new shoes 1-2 weeks before.
  • Movement: Surface-specific patterns (Slide on clay, Low bounce on grass).
  • Monitoring: Daily soreness (Achilles, Patellar, Hip, Back), ACWR conservative.
  • Tournament Surface Prep:
  • Arrive 5-7 days early (Grass/Clay), 3-5 days (Hard).
  • Practice on tournament courts, Same balls.
  • Per transition. KPI: Transition smooth, No spike injuries, Performance maintained.

**Bài tập 8: "Tournament Load Accounting - Match + Training" (Kế Toán Tải Giải Đấu - Trận + Tập)"

  • Match Load Quantification:
  • Duration-based: Minutes × Intensity factor (1.5-3.0 based on level).
  • GPS-based: Distance, HSD, Accel/Decel, PlayerLoad.
  • sRPE-based: Post-match RPE × Minutes.
  • Training Adjustment During Tournament:
  • Match Day: Training = Warm-up + Recovery only (Low load).
  • Off Day: Light training (Moderate load) OR Full recovery (Low load).
  • Weekly Total: Match load + Training load = Weekly Acute Load.
  • ACWR Calculation: Include match load in acute, Chronic = Pre-tournament avg.
  • Decision: If ACWR >1.3 → Next off day = Recovery only.
  • Per tournament. KPI: Load accounted, ACWR controlled, No tournament-week spikes.

Giai Đoạn 4: Return-to-Play & Advanced Prevention (Trở Lại Thi Đấu & Phòng Ngừa Nâng Cao)

**Bài tập 9: "Return-to-Play (RTP) Framework - Criteria-Based" (Khung Trở Lại Thi Đấu - Dựa Trên Tiêu Chí)"

  • Phase 1: Clinical Clearance (Medical):
  • Pain-free ADL, Full ROM, Strength >90% contralateral, No swelling/effusion.
  • Phase 2: Physical Performance (Performance):
  • Strength: SL Hop >90%, SL RDL >90%, CMJ >90%, Push/Pull >90%.
  • Mobility: All joints Green on screen.
  • Running: Linear sprint times >90%, COD times >90%.
  • RSA: Fatigue Index <10%.
  • Phase 3: Tennis-Specific (Sport):
  • Shadow patterns: Full speed, No compensation, 20min continuous.
  • Live ball: 30min controlled, Progressive intensity.
  • Practice sets: 2-3 sets, Full rules, No pain/swelling next day.
  • Tournament sim: Back-to-back days, Match intensity.
  • Phase 4: Full Return (Competition):
  • Gradual tournament re-entry (Lower level → Target level).
  • Load monitoring: ACWR conservative (0.8-1.0) first 4 weeks.
  • Ongoing prehab: Enhanced for previously injured area.
  • Per injury. KPI: Criteria met at each phase, No re-injury 12 months, Performance restored.

**Bài tập 10: "Recurrence Prevention - Previously Injured Area" (Phòng Tái Phát - Vùng Đã Chấn Thương)"

  • Enhanced Prehab (Daily + Weekly, Lifetime):
  • 2x Daily Micro-dose for previously injured area (Morning + Pre-tennis).
  • Weekly Targeted Session (1x/week additional for that region).
  • Monthly Re-screen (Specific tests for that injury).
  • Load Management Special Rules:
  • Lower Floor: Chronic load never <80% baseline (Resilience maintenance).
  • Lower Ceiling: ACWR >1.3 = Immediate deload (Lower tolerance).
  • Spike Sensitivity: Single session >1.5x daily avg = Next session -50%.
  • Psychological: Confidence building, Fear-avoidance addressing, Imagery.
  • Lifetime. KPI: No recurrence 2+ years, Confidence high, Load tolerance maintained.

**Bài tập 11: "Youth Injury Prevention - Growth & Maturation" (Phòng Chấn Thương Thanh Niên - Sự Phát Triển & Trưởng Thành)"

  • Growth-Related Risks:
  • Peak Height Velocity (PHV): Coordination loss, Bone vulnerability, Tendon strain.
  • Apophysitis: Osgood-Schlatter (Knee), Sever's (Heel), ISIS (Elbow).
  • Stress Fractures: Lumbar pars, Metatarsals, Tibia.
  • Screening (Quarterly during PHV):
  • Height velocity (cm/3mo), Maturation offset (Mirwald), Mobility screen.
  • Red Flags: Rapid growth >2cm/mo, Pain at apophysis, Night pain.
  • Training Adjustments During PHV:
  • Reduce volume 20-30%, Reduce high-impact (Jumping, Sprinting).
  • Emphasize: Mobility, Motor control, Low-load strength, Coordination.
  • NO: Heavy loading, Max sprinting, Max serving, Tournament stacking.
  • Education: Athlete, Parents, Coaches — "Growing pains ≠ Normal, Report early".
  • Ongoing. KPI: Growth tracked, Adjustments made, Zero growth-plate injuries.

**Bài tập 12: "Injury Surveillance System - Team/Club Level" (Hệ Thống Theo Dõi Chấn Thương - Cấp Đội/Câu Lạc Bộ)"

  • Data Collection (Every injury):
  • Diagnosis: OSICS-10 / ICD-10 code, Body region, Tissue type, Mechanism.
  • Severity: Time loss (Days), Medical attention (Y/N), Surgery (Y/N).
  • Context: Training/Match, Surface, Session type, Load (ACWR prior 4 weeks).
  • Outcome: RTP date, Recurrence (Y/N), Performance impact.
  • Analysis (Quarterly):
  • Incidence Rates: Per 1000h (Training vs Match).
  • Burden: Days lost per 1000h.
  • Patterns: Body region, Mechanism, Month, Surface, Age group.
  • Risk Factors: ACWR spikes, Previous injury, Screen deficits.
  • Action: Report → Intervention → Monitor effectiveness.
  • Ongoing. KPI: Database complete, Trends identified, Interventions effective, Rates declining.

6. Hiệp Đồng Liên Miền

Với Physical (Bài 131-138): Strength, Mobility, Stability, Recovery = Injury prevention pillars.

V với Periodization (Bài 130): Load management = Periodization execution. Deloads, Progressions, Taper.

V với Tournament Prep (Bài 129): Travel, Surface, Match load = Injury risk modifiers.
