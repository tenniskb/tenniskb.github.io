# Bài viết 136: Sức Mạnh Anaerobic - Repeat Sprint Ability & Dung Nạp Lactate

> **CUE CHUYÊN GIA:** Anaerobic capacity (Sức mạnh Anaerobic) = **Kem ấn trên nền tảng Aerobic** — Cho phép duy trì chất lượng điểm CAO khi mỏi. RSA (Repeat Sprint Ability) = **Lặp lại nỗ lực tối đa**. Lactate tolerance = **Chơi tốt khi đau**. Train "Anaerobic Engine" = Sức bền cường độ cao.

## 1. Tóm Tắt Điều Hành & Ý Định Thể Thao

Sức mạnh Anaerobic (Anaerobic Capacity) là **khả năng sản xuất và duy trì công suất cao khi hệ oxy không đáp ứng kịp** — Quan trọng cho tennis vì: Mỗi điểm = 3-10s nỗ lực gần tối đa, Nghỉ 20-25s, Lặp lại 100-300 lần/trận. Bài viết định nghĩa **Tennis Anaerobic Model**: (1) **Glycolytic Power** (Sức mạnh Glycolytic) — Peak output 10-60s, Lactate production. (2) **Repeat Sprint Ability (RSA)** (Khả Năng Lặp Sprint) — 6-10 × max effort, Incomplete recovery, Fatigue resistance. (3) **Lactate Tolerance & Clearance** (Dung Nạp & Thanh Trừ Lactate) — Buffer H+, Maintain pH, Oxidize lactate during recovery. (4) **On-Court Anaerobic Conditioning** (Điều Hành Anaerobic Trên Sân) — Drills mô phỏng match demands. Giao thức **"Anaerobic Engine Protocol"** xây dựng động cơ anaerobic tennis từ foundation đến peak.

Ý định thể thao ba khía cạnh: thứ nhất, định nghĩa **Anaerobic = Dependent on Aerobic** — Không aerobic base tốt = Không recover giữa sprints = Anaerobic thất bại. Thứ hai, chỉ ra **RSA ≠ Sprint training** — RSA = Lặp lại VỚI phục hồi không hoàn toàn, Quality maintenance. Thứ ba, cung cấp **bộ bài tập "Anaerobic Mastery"** từ off-court intervals đến on-court match simulation.

## 2. Nền Tảng Sinh Học Cơ - Khoa Học Anaerobic

### 2.1 Anaerobic Physiology for Tennis (Sinh Lý Học Anaerobic Cho Tennis)

```
ANAEROBIC ENERGY SYSTEM IN TENNIS:
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  GLYCOLYTIC SYSTEM (Hệ Glycolytic - 10-90s):                               │
│  ├── **Fuel**: Muscle Glycogen + Blood Glucose                             │
│  ├── **Rate**: Fast ATP production (But limited total yield)              │
│  ├── **Byproducts**: Lactate + H+ (Proton) → **Acidosis** (pH ↓)         │
│  ├── **Fatigue Mechanisms**:                                              │
│  │   ├── H+ inhibits PFK (Glycolysis enzyme)                              │
│  │   ├── H+ competes with Ca2+ → Cross-bridge cycling impaired           │
│  │   ├── Pi (Inorganic phosphate) ↑ → Force production ↓                 │
│  │   └── K+ accumulation → Membrane excitability ↓                        │
│  ├── **Recovery**: Oxidative metabolism (Aerobic) clears lactate/H+      │
│  └── **Tennis**: Long rallies (15-30s), Extended points                  │
│                                                                             │
│  ATP-PCR SYSTEM (Hệ Phosphagen - 0-10s):                                  │
│  ├── **Fuel**: Stored ATP + Phosphocreatine (PCr)                         │
│  ├── **Rate**: Immediate, Highest power                                   │
│  ├── **Depletion**: 50% in 5-10s max effort                              │
│  ├── **Resynthesis**: Aerobic dependent (50% in 30s, 95% in 3-5min)      │
│  └── **Tennis**: Every sprint, Jump, Swing, First step, COD              │
│                                                                             │
│  INTERPLAY (Tương Tác):                                                    │
│  ├── **Point (5-10s)**: ATP-PCr (70%) + Glycolytic (20%) + Oxidative (10%)│
│  ├── **Rest (20-25s)**: PCr resynthesis (Aerobic) + Lactate clearance    │
│  ├── **Next Point**: Quality = f(Recovery completeness)                  │
│  └── **Match**: Cumulative fatigue → Glycogen depletion → Output drop    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 2.2 Repeat Sprint Ability (RSA) - Khả Năng Lặp Sprint

```
RSA FOR TENNIS:
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  DEFINITION: **Khả năng lặp lại nỗ lực sprint tối đa (hoặc gần tối đa)    │
│  với phục hồi không hoàn toàn** — Mirror tennis point-to-point demands.   │
│                                                                             │
│  RSA PROTOCOLS:                                                            │
│  ├── **Standard RSA**: 6-10 × 20-30m sprint, 20-30s rest                  │
│  ├── **Tennis RSA**: 6-10 × Tennis pattern (Split→Sprint→Plant→Hit), 25s rest│
│  ├── **On-Court RSA**: 6-10 × Point simulation (Max intensity), 25s rest │
│  └── **Metrics**:                                                          │
│      ├── **Best Time**: Single best effort                                │
│      ├── **Mean Time**: Average of all sprints                            │
│      ├── **Total Time**: Sum of all sprints                               │
│      ├── **Fatigue Index (FI)**: (Slowest - Fastest) / Fastest × 100     │
│      ├── **Decrement %**: (Mean - Best) / Best × 100                      │
│      └── **Performance Decrement**: % drop from 1st to last sprint        │
│                                                                             │
│  RSA FATIGUE PROFILE:                                                      │
│  ├── **Sprint 1-2**: Near max, PCr dominant                              │
│  ├── **Sprint 3-5**: PCr depletion, Glycolytic ↑, H+ accumulation        │
│  ├── **Sprint 6-10**: Glycolytic dominant, Acidosis severe, Power ↓      │
│  └── **Recovery between**: Aerobic capacity determines PCr resynthesis   │
│                                                                             │
│  TENNIS RSA TARGETS:                                                       │
│  ├── **FI <10%** = Elite (Pro)                                            │
│  ├── **FI 10-15%** = Good (College/High Performance)                     │
│  ├── **FI 15-20%** = Average (Club/Junior)                                │
│  └── **FI >20%** = Poor (Needs anaerobic development)                    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 2.3 Lactate Tolerance & Clearance (Dung Nạp & Thanh Trừ Lactate)

```
LACTATE DYNAMICS IN TENNIS:
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  LACTATE PRODUCTION:                                                       │
│  ├── **During high-intensity**: Glycolysis → Pyruvate → Lactate + H+      │
│  ├── **Blood Lactate**: Rest 1-2 mmol/L → Match 4-12 mmol/L (Peaks)      │
│  ├── **Muscle Lactate**: Higher than blood (15-30 mmol/kg)               │
│  └── **Rate of rise**: Determines early fatigue                          │
│                                                                             │
│  LACTATE CLEARANCE (Thanh Trừ):                                           │
│  ├── **Oxidation**: Heart, Slow-twitch fibers, Liver (Cori cycle)        │
│  ├── **Transport**: MCT1 (Import to oxidative fibers), MCT4 (Export)     │
│  ├── **During Recovery**: 25s between points = Partial clearance         │
│  ├── **During Changeover**: 90s = Significant clearance                  │
│  └── **Aerobic Fitness**: VO2max ↑, Mitochondria ↑ → Clearance ↑↑        │
│                                                                             │
│  LACTATE TOLERANCE (Dung Nạp):                                            │
│  ├── **Buffering Capacity**: Carnosine, Bicarbonate, Phosphate, Proteins │
│  ├── **pH Maintenance**: Intracellular pH >6.8 = Function maintained     │
│  ├── **Training Effect**: Repeated acidosis exposure → Buffering ↑       │
│  ├── **Supplements**: Beta-alanine (Carnosine ↑), Sodium bicarbonate    │
│  └── **Mental**: Discomfort tolerance → Decision quality under duress    │
│                                                                             │
│  LACTATE THRESHOLDS:                                                       │
│  ├── **LT1 (Aerobic Threshold)**: ~2 mmol/L — Zone 2 upper limit        │
│  ├── **LT2 (Anaerobic Threshold/MLSS)**: ~4 mmol/L — Max steady state   │
│  └── **Tennis**: Points oscillate ABOVE LT2, Recovery BELOW LT1          │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 3. Thực Hành Kỹ Thuật Từng Bước

### Giai Đoạn 1: Off-Court Anaerobic Foundation (Nền Tảng Anaerobic Ngoài Sân)

**Bài tập 1: "Short Intervals - Glycolytic Power" (Khoảng Cách Ngắn - Sức Mạnh Glycolytic)"
- **Protocol**: 
  - **Work**: 20-30s MAX EFFORT (Bike/Row/Run/SkiErg).
  - **Rest**: 60-90s (Passive or Active recovery).
  - **Sets**: 6-10 intervals.
  - **Frequency**: 1x/week (Specific Prep), 2x/week (Pre-Comp).
- **Intensity**: **RPE 9-10/10**, HR >90% max, **Cannot hold conversation**.
- **Progression**: 
  - Week 1-2: 6×20s / 90s rest
  - Week 3-4: 8×20s / 75s rest
  - Week 5-6: 8×30s / 60s rest
  - Week 7-8: 10×30s / 60s rest
- **Monitoring**: Power/Watts each interval, Fatigue Index, Lactate (if available).
- 8 weeks. KPI: Mean power maintained, FI <15%, Lactate peak ↑, Clearance ↑.

**Bài tập 2: "Long Intervals - Lactate Tolerance" (Khoảng Cách Dài - Dung Nạp Lactate)"
- **Protocol**:
  - **Work**: 60-120s @ 90-95% max (Hard but sustainable).
  - **Rest**: 2-3min (Active recovery jog/walk).
  - **Sets**: 4-6 intervals.
  - **Frequency**: 1x/week (Alternate with Short Intervals).
- **Intensity**: **RPE 8-9/10**, "Comfortably hard" → "Hard" by end.
- **Progression**:
  - Week 1-2: 4×60s / 3min rest
  - Week 3-4: 5×75s / 2:30 rest
  - Week 5-6: 6×90s / 2min rest
  - Week 7-8: 6×120s / 2min rest
- **Focus**: **Mental tolerance, Technique maintenance, Breathing control**.
- 8 weeks. KPI: Pace maintained, RPE managed, Lactate tolerance ↑.

**Bài tập 3: "RSA Training - Standard Protocol" (Huấn Luyện RSA - Giao Thức Chuẩn)"
- **Protocol**:
  - **Sprint**: 20m (Linear) HOẶC Tennis pattern (Split→Sprint 5m→Plant→Shadow).
  - **Rest**: 20s (Strict timing).
  - **Reps**: 8-10 sprints.
  - **Frequency**: 1x/week (Pre-Comp), Replace 1 interval session.
- **Metrics**: Best, Mean, Total, FI, Decrement %.
- **Progression**:
  - Week 1-2: 8×20m / 25s rest (Linear)
  - Week 3-4: 10×20m / 25s rest (Linear)
  - Week 5-6: 8×Tennis pattern / 25s rest
  - Week 7-8: 10×Tennis pattern / 25s rest
- **Quality Control**: If sprint time >10% slower than best → **Stop set**.
- 8 weeks. KPI: FI <10%, Mean time consistent, Tennis pattern quality.

### Giai Đoạn 2: On-Court Anaerobic Conditioning (Điều Hành Anaerobic Trên Sân)

**Bài tập 4: "On-Court Intervals - Point Simulation" (Khoảng Cách Trên Sân - Mô Phỏng Điểm)"
- **Drill**: **Coach feeds / Partner rally → Max intensity point (5-15s) → 25s rest**.
- **Pattern**: 
  - Serve + 1 (Serve → Return → 1st ball attack)
  - Return + 1 (Return → Serve + 1 attack)
  - Baseline pattern (Cross-court → Down-line → Short → Approach)
  - Net play (Approach → Volley → Overhead)
- **Sets**: 8-12 points per pattern.
- **Rest**: **Strict 25s** (Walk to back, Towel, Breathe, Reset).
- **Frequency**: 2x/week (Specific Prep/Pre-Comp).
- 8 weeks. KPI: Point quality maintained, HR recovery 25s ↑, Decision quality.

**Bài tập 5: "Suicide/Line Drills - Tennis Specific RSA" (Drill Đường Dây - RSA Đặc Thù Tennis)"
- **Drill**: **Tennis Court Suicide** (Baseline → Service line → Baseline → Net → Baseline).
- **Protocol**:
  - 1 Rep = Full suicide (Touch lines with racquet).
  - **Work**: 1 Rep max effort (~25-30s).
  - **Rest**: 30s.
  - **Reps**: 6-8.
- **Variations**:
  - **Lateral Suicide**: Sideline → Center → Sideline → Center.
  - **Pattern Suicide**: Split step at each line → Shadow hit.
  - **Reactive Suicide**: Coach calls line → Athlete moves.
- **Frequency**: 1x/week (Pre-Comp).
- 6 weeks. KPI: Rep time consistent, FI <10%, Movement quality maintained.

**Bài tập 6: "Match Simulation Sets - Anaerobic Endurance" (Mô Phỏng Set Trận - Sức Bền Anaerobic)"
- **Protocol**: **Play practice sets với modified rules để tăng cường độ**:
  - **No-Ad scoring** (Faster points, More pressure).
  - **Second serve only** (More returns, Longer rallies).
  - **Winner = 2 points** (Encourage aggression).
  - **Time limit per point** (Max 15s, Enforce pace).
- **Duration**: 2-3 sets (Simulate match anaerobic load).
- **Monitoring**: HR, RPE, Lactate (if available), Point quality.
- **Frequency**: 1x/week (Pre-Comp), Replace 1 interval session.
- 6 weeks. KPI: Set quality maintained, Late-set performance ↑, Mental toughness.

### Giai Đoạn 3: Advanced Anaerobic Methods (Phương Pháp Anaerobic Nâng Cao)

**Bài tập 7: "Lactate Stacking - Accumulation Tolerance" (Chồng Chất Lactate - Dung Nạp Tích Lũy)"
- **Protocol** (Advanced, 1x/2 weeks, Pre-Comp only):
  - **Block 1**: 4×30s max / 30s rest (High lactate production).
  - **Rest**: 3min (Partial clearance).
  - **Block 2**: 4×30s max / 30s rest (Stack on residual lactate).
  - **Rest**: 3min.
  - **Block 3**: 4×30s max / 30s rest (Maximal acidosis exposure).
- **Total**: 12 intervals, ~25min work.
- **Purpose**: **Expose to extreme acidosis → Buffering adaptation + Mental tolerance**.
- **Safety**: HRV green, No illness, Experienced athlete only.
- 4-6 sessions. KPI: Tolerance ↑, Mental resilience, Power maintenance in Block 3.

**Bài tập 8: "Beta-Alanine / Bicarbonate Loading - Supplement Protocol" (Bổ Sung Beta-Alanine / Bicarbonate - Giao Thức)"
- **Beta-Alanine** (Chronic loading):
  - **Dose**: 3.2-6.4g/day (Split doses 0.8-1.6g để giảm paresthesia).
  - **Duration**: 4-12 weeks → Muscle Carnosine ↑ 40-80%.
  - **Effect**: Intracellular buffering ↑, High-intensity performance ↑.
- **Sodium Bicarbonate** (Acute loading):
  - **Dose**: 0.3g/kg BW (e.g., 70kg = 21g) trong 500ml nước.
  - **Timing**: 60-90min trước competition/hard training.
  - **Effect**: Extracellular buffering ↑, Blood pH maintained.
  - **Side effects**: GI distress (Test in training FIRST).
- **Monitoring**: Performance with/without, GI tolerance.
- Ongoing. KPI: Performance improvement, Tolerance established.

### Giai Đoạn 4: Integration & Monitoring (Tích Hợp & Giám Sát)

**Bài tập 9: "Anaerobic Testing Battery - Monthly" (Bộ Kiểm Tra Anaerobic - Hàng Tháng)"
- **Test 1**: **RSA Test** — 10×20m / 25s rest → Best, Mean, FI, Decrement.
- **Test 2**: **300m Shuttle** — 6×50m (25m out/back) continuous → Time, Fatigue.
- **Test 3**: **On-Court Anaerobic** — 8×Point sim / 25s rest → Quality, HR recovery.
- **Test 4**: **Lactate Profile** (If lab) — Staircase test → LT1, LT2, Max lactate.
- **Analysis**: Compare to baseline, Track trends, Adjust training.
- Monthly. KPI: All metrics trending positive, FI decreasing, Quality increasing.

**Bài tập 10: "Recovery Between Intervals - Aerobic Dependency" (Phục Hồi Giữa Khoảng Cách - Phụ Thuộc Aerobic)"
- **Experiment**: 
  - Day 1: RSA test **FRESH** (After aerobic base week).
  - Day 2: RSA test **FATIGUED** (After hard tennis session).
  - Compare: FI, Mean time, HR recovery, Subjective quality.
- **Insight**: **Aerobic fitness = RSA recovery capacity**.
- **Application**: If RSA degrades significantly when fatigued → **More aerobic volume**.
- Monthly. KPI: Aerobic-anaerobic relationship quantified, Training adjusted.

**Bài tập 11: "In-Season Anaerobic Maintenance - Micro-dose" (Duy Trì Anaerobic Mùa Giải - Liều Vi)"
- **Protocol** (Match week):
  - **1 session**: 4-6 × 15s max / 45s rest (Bike/Row) OR 4×Point sim / 25s rest.
  - **Timing**: 48h before first match (Not day before).
  - **Volume**: **Micro-dose** — Stimulus without fatigue.
  - **Intent**: Maintain glycolytic enzyme activity, Buffering, Mental sharpness.
- **Non-match week**: 1 full session (6-8 intervals).
- Ongoing. KPI: Anaerobic markers maintained, No fatigue carryover, Match performance stable.

**Bài tập 12: "Taper Anaerobic - Peak Week Protocol" (Giảm Tải Anaerobic - Giao Thức Tuần Đỉnh)"
- **Week of Major Tournament**:
  - Mon: 4×15s max / 60s rest (Sharpener).
  - Tue: Light tennis + Mobility.
  - Wed: Travel / Off.
  - Thu: 3×Point sim / 25s rest (Match intensity, Low volume).
  - Fri-Sun: Tournament.
- **Principles**: **Volume ↓↓ (80% reduction), Intensity ↔ (Match speed), Frequency ↔**.
- **NO**: High volume intervals, Lactate stacking, New drills, Fatigue accumulation.
- Per tournament. KPI: Freshness markers ↑, Anaerobic sharpness maintained, Match 1 quality.

## 6. Hiệp Đồng Liên Miền

**Với Aerobic Base (Bài 132):** Aerobic = **Recovery engine cho Anaerobic**. No aerobic = No RSA recovery.

**V với Strength (Bài 133):** Strength = **Force production cho Sprints**. Unilateral = COD power.

**V với Power (Bài 134):** Power = **Peak output cho Sprints**. RFD = First 5m.

**V với Speed/Agility (Bài 135):** Movement = **Anaerobic expression**. Mechanics = Efficiency.

**V với Periodization (Bài 130):** Anaerobic mesocycles = **Aligned với Tennis mesocycles**. Late Specific → Pre-Comp.

**V với Recovery (Bài 195, 196):** Anaerobic = **High stress**. Sleep, Nutrition, HRV critical.

**V với Monitoring (Bài 197, 198):** RSA, Lactate, HR recovery = **Anaerobic KPIs**. Monthly testing.

**V với Nutrition (Bài 195):** Glycogen, CHO availability, Buffering agents = **Anaerobic fuel**.
