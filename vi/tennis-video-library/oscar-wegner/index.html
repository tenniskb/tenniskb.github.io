Oscar Wegner

Total Videos: 10

Oscar Wegner is the legendary "Modern Tennis" coach — author of the iconic book and methodology that revolutionized how tennis is taught, especially the natural forehand. Visit source for more.

About the Coach

Oscar Wegner is one of the most influential tennis coaches in history. His "Modern Tennis" method revolutionized the sport by emphasizing the natural forehand and a more intuitive approach to technique. He has coached professional players for over 40 years.
