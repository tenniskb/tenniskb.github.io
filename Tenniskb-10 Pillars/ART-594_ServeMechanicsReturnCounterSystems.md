# ART-594: Return Recovery and Regeneration — Post-Match Care

## Executive Summary

Proper post-match recovery is essential for maintaining return quality over multiple matches and preventing injury. This article provides a comprehensive recovery protocol for returners.

## Immediate Post-Match Cool-Down

### Light Activity (5-10 minutes)
- Light walking to lower heart rate
- Gentle jogging or cycling
- Prevents blood pooling in the legs

### Static Stretching (10-15 minutes)
- Shoulder stretches: Hold 30 seconds each side
- Trunk stretches: Hold 30 seconds each side
- Leg stretches: Hold 30 seconds each side
- Wrist and forearm stretches: Hold 30 seconds each side

## Nutrition for Recovery

### Within 30 Minutes
- Consume a recovery meal with carbohydrates and protein
- Aim for a 3:1 ratio of carbohydrates to protein
- Examples: Chocolate milk, protein shake, chicken sandwich

### Hydration
- Drink 16-24 oz for every pound of body weight lost
- Continue drinking electrolyte drinks
- Monitor urine color (pale yellow = properly hydrated)

## Recovery Techniques

### Ice Baths
- 10-15 minutes in cold water (50-59°F)
- Reduces inflammation and muscle soreness
- Particularly beneficial after long matches

### Compression
- Wear compression garments for 2-4 hours post-match
- Improves circulation and reduces swelling
- Speeds up recovery

### Massage
- Self-massage or professional massage
- Focus on shoulders, arms, and legs
- Reduces muscle tension and improves recovery

### Sleep
- Aim for 8-10 hours of sleep
- Sleep is when the body repairs itself
- Essential for full recovery

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Failure | Result | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Soreness persists after matches | Poor recovery | Accumulated fatigue | Recovery protocol; stretching |
| Fatigue in later matches | Insufficient recovery | Declining performance | Nutrition; sleep; recovery techniques |
| Frequent injuries | Overuse | Time off court | Volume management; recovery emphasis |

## Practical Application: "The Recovery Routine"

After every match, follow this 30-minute recovery routine:
1. 10 minutes of light activity
2. 10 minutes of static stretching
3. Recovery meal and hydration within 30 minutes
4. Compression garments for 2-4 hours

This routine accelerates recovery and prepares you for the next match.
