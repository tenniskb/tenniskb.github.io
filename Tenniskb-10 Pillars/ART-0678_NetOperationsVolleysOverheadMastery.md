# ART-0678: The Volley Against the Return Drop Shot — Handling Soft Returns

## Executive Summary

Drop shot returns are used in doubles to neutralize an aggressive net player — the returner hits a soft drop shot that lands just over the net, forcing the net player to sprint forward and handle a difficult low ball. When you are the net player facing a drop shot return, you must explode forward, get low, and use a soft touch to handle the ball. The drop shot return is a high-percentage tactic against net players.

## The Drop Shot Return

The drop shot return: (1) is hit soft and short, (2) lands just over the net, (3) forces the net player to cover maximum distance. At the net, the drop shot return is one of the most challenging situations because the soft pace gives you no pace to redirect and the low bounce forces extreme knee bend.

## Technique for Handling Drop Shot Returns

Against drop shot returns: (1) explode forward from your split step, (2) use crossover steps to cover the distance, (3) get low with deep knee bend, (4) use a soft touch to handle the ball, (5) aim for depth or a drop shot of your own. The key is quick recovery and soft hands.

## Common Errors

Not recognizing the drop shot early (late start). Not getting low enough (frame shots). Hitting the ball too hard (errors). Not using a soft touch (the ball goes long). Giving up on the point.

## Summary

Drop shot returns demand quick recovery and soft touch. Explode forward, get low, and use a soft touch to handle the ball.

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Failure | Result | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cannot reach drop shot returns | Late recognition, slow movement | Missed volleys | Watch returner's racket face, explode forward |
| Hits drop shot returns too hard | No soft touch | Errors | Use soft hands, light grip pressure |
| Cannot get low enough | Poor knee bend | Frame shots | Bend knees deeply |
| Gives up on drop shot returns | Poor mental approach | Lost points | Always attempt recovery |

## Practical Application: "Explode Forward, Get Low, Soft Touch"

Against drop shot returns: (1) explode forward, (2) get low, (3) use soft touch. Quick recovery and soft hands are the keys.
