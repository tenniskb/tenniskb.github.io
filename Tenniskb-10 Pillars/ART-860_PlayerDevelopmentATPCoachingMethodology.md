# ART-860: Physical Profiling and Fitness Testing for Tennis

## Executive Summary

Physical profiling is the systematic assessment of a player's physical capabilities to identify strengths, weaknesses, and areas for development. Fitness testing provides objective data that informs training prescription, tracks progress, and predicts competitive readiness. A comprehensive physical profile includes assessments of strength, speed, agility, power, endurance, flexibility, and tennis-specific movement quality. Regular testing (every 8-12 weeks) allows coaches to monitor adaptations, adjust training programs, and ensure the player arrives at competition in optimal physical condition.

## The Physical Profile Battery

### Strength Assessment

| Test | What It Measures | Equipment | Protocol |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1RM Back Squat | Lower body strength | Barbell, squat rack | Progressive loading to maximum single rep |
| 1RM Bench Press | Upper body pressing strength | Barbell, bench | Progressive loading to maximum single rep |
| 1RM Deadlift | Posterior chain strength | Barbell | Progressive loading to maximum single rep |
| Grip Strength (Dynamometer) | Forearm/hand strength | Hand dynamometer | Maximum squeeze, dominant and non-dominant |
| Isokinetic Shoulder Strength | Rotator cuff strength | Isokinetic dynamometer | Internal and external rotation at 60 and 180 deg/sec |

### Speed Assessment

| Test | What It Measures | Equipment | Protocol |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10m Sprint | Acceleration | Timing gates | Flying start, maximum effort |
| 20m Sprint | Linear speed | Timing gates | Standing start, maximum effort |
| 40m Sprint | Maximum speed | Timing gates | Standing start, maximum effort |
| First-Step Quickness (5m) | Initial acceleration | Timing gates | Reactive start to visual/auditory cue |

### Agility Assessment

| Test | What It Measures | Equipment | Protocol |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Illinois Agility Test | Change of direction speed | Cones, timing gates | Standard Illinois course, maximum effort |
| 5-10-5 Pro Agility | Lateral change of direction | Timing gates | 5 yards right, 10 yards left, 5 yards right |
| Reactive Agility Test | Decision-making speed | Timing gates, video | React to video stimulus, move in indicated direction |
| T-Drill | Multi-directional agility | Cones, timing gates | Sprint, shuffle, backpedal course |

### Power Assessment

| Test | What It Measures | Equipment | Protocol |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Vertical Jump | Lower body power | Jump mat or force plate | Countermovement jump, maximum height |
| Standing Broad Jump | Horizontal power | Tape measure | Two-footed jump, maximum distance |
| Medicine Ball Chest Pass | Upper body power | Medicine ball (3 kg) | Maximum distance throw from chest |
| Medicine Ball Rotational Throw | Rotational power | Medicine ball (3 kg) | Maximum distance throw, rotation from hip |
| Serve Speed | Tennis-specific power | Radar gun | Maximum serve speed, average of 5 attempts |

### Endurance Assessment

| Test | What It Measures | Equipment | Protocol |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| VO2max Test (Lab) | Aerobic capacity | Metabolic cart, treadmill | Progressive treadmill test to exhaustion |
| Cooper Test (Field) | Aerobic capacity (estimate) | Track or treadmill | 12-minute run, maximum distance |
| Beep Test (Field) | Aerobic capacity (estimate) | Audio recording, court | Multi-stage shuttle run to exhaustion |
| 10 x 20m RSA Test | Repeated sprint ability | Timing gates | 10 x 20m sprints, 20-second rest between |
| 300m Shuttle Test | Anaerobic capacity | Timing gates | 6 x 50m shuttles, continuous |

### Flexibility and Mobility Assessment

| Test | What It Measures | Equipment | Protocol |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| GIRD Measurement | Shoulder internal rotation | Goniometer | Dominant and non-dominant shoulder internal rotation |
| Total Arc Measurement | Shoulder total rotation | Goniometer | Internal + external rotation (shoulder arc) |
| Sit and Reach | Hamstring flexibility | Sit and reach box | Maximum forward reach |
| Ankle Dorsiflexion | Ankle mobility | Goniometer | Knee-to-wall test or goniometric measurement |
| Hip Internal/External Rotation | Hip mobility | Goniometer | Seated hip rotation measurement |
| Thoracic Spine Rotation | Spine mobility | Goniometer | Seated trunk rotation measurement |

### Movement Quality Assessment

| Test | What It Measures | Equipment | Protocol |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Functional Movement Screen (FMS) | Movement quality and asymmetry | FMS kit | 7-test movement screening battery |
| Single-Leg Squat | Lower body stability | None | Single-leg squat observation |
| Star Excursion Balance Test | Dynamic balance | None | Single-leg reach in 8 directions |
| Overhead Squat | Full-body movement quality | Dowel rod | Overhead squat observation |

## Testing Protocols

### Pre-Testing Preparation

- Standardized warm-up before all tests
- Consistent testing environment (same time of day, same equipment)
- Adequate recovery between tests (minimum 5 minutes between efforts)
- Familiarization session before formal testing
- Consistent testing personnel

### Testing Order

1. **Movement Quality Screening** (FMS, single-leg squat) - no fatigue
2. **Flexibility and Mobility Assessment** - no fatigue
3. **Speed Tests** (10m, 20m sprints) - minimal fatigue
4. **Agility Tests** (Illinois, reactive) - moderate fatigue
5. **Power Tests** (vertical jump, broad jump, med ball) - moderate fatigue
6. **Endurance Tests** (RSA, beep test) - high fatigue
7. **Strength Tests** (1RM squat, bench, deadlift) - fresh or at end of session

### Frequency of Testing

| Test Type | Frequency | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Movement Quality | Monthly | Rapid changes; injury prevention |
| Speed and Power | Every 8 weeks | Moderate rate of adaptation |
| Strength | Every 8-12 weeks | Slower rate of adaptation |
| Endurance | Every 8 weeks | Moderate rate of adaptation |
| Flexibility/Mobility | Monthly | Rapid changes; daily training effect |

## Training Prescription Based on Results

### Identifying Weaknesses

Compare test results to benchmarks:
- **Significantly Below Benchmark:** High priority for development
- **Below Benchmark:** Moderate priority for development
- **At Benchmark:** Maintain current level
- **Above Benchmark:** Strength; maintain with reduced volume

### Example Prescription

**Player Profile:**
- Vertical Jump: 45 cm (Below benchmark of 50+ cm)
- Illinois Agility: 16.5 sec (At benchmark)
- VO2max: 55 mL/kg/min (Above benchmark)

**Training Prescription:**
- Increase plyometric training volume (2-3 sessions/week)
- Maintain agility training (1-2 sessions/week)
- Reduce aerobic conditioning volume (maintain only)
- Focus on lower body power development

| Illinois Agility | 16.5 sec | <17.0 sec | At | Low |
| VO2max | 55 mL/kg/min | >55 mL/kg/min | At | Low |

**Key Findings:**
1. Vertical jump below benchmark; prioritize lower body power
2. Speed and agility at benchmark; maintain current training
3. Aerobic fitness at benchmark; maintain current level

**Training Prescription:**
1. Increase plyometric training to 3 sessions/week
2. Add explosive strength training (Olympic lifts)
3. Maintain speed and agility training (2 sessions/week)
4. Reduce aerobic conditioning to maintenance level

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Failure | Result | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Player fatigues in third sets | Insufficient aerobic conditioning | Performance decline in extended matches | Increase aerobic training volume; repeated sprint training |
| Slow first step | Insufficient speed/power training | Late arrivals, defensive strokes | Plyometric training; acceleration drills; strength training |
| Poor change of direction | Insufficient agility training | Inefficient movement, injuries | Agility ladder drills; change of direction exercises |
| Low serve speed | Insufficient rotational power and leg drive | Predictable, returnable serve | Olympic lifts; plyometrics; rotational power training |
| Recurring injuries during matches | Poor movement quality, fatigue-related breakdown | Time loss, reduced performance | Movement screening; corrective exercises; load management |
