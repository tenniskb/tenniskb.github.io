# ART-0694: The Volley Against the Return with Extreme Depth — Handling Baseline Returns

## Executive Summary

Returns hit to the extreme baseline force the net player to hit a deep, defensive volley from a position near the service line or deeper. When you are the net player facing an extreme-depth return, you must let the ball drop, use a longer backswing (you have more time), and hit a deep volley to the baseline. Extreme-depth returns are a standard tactic to push the net player back and neutralize their attacking position.

## The Extreme-Depth Return

The extreme-depth return: (1) lands near or beyond the baseline, (2) forces the net player to volley from a deep position, (3) is hit with moderate pace. At the net, the extreme-depth return gives you more time but forces a deeper contact point, requiring adjustments to standard volley technique.

## Technique for Handling Extreme-Depth Returns

Against extreme-depth returns: (1) let the ball drop to a comfortable height, (2) use a slightly longer backswing (you have more time), (3) use the punch volley with full extension, (4) aim deep to the baseline, (5) use a firm wrist. The key is to use the extra time to hit a quality deep volley.

## Common Errors

Positioning too close to the net (forced to volley from an awkward height). Not adjusting technique for the deep ball (the standard volley may not work). Rushing when you have time. Not aiming deep (short volleys give the opponent attacking opportunities).

## Summary

Extreme-depth returns demand adjusted technique and deep positioning. Let the ball drop, use a longer backswing, and aim deep.

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Failure | Result | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Too close to net for deep returns | Standard positioning | Awkward contact height | Move deeper, adjust positioning |
| Does not adjust technique for deep returns | Same technique for all returns | Inconsistent volleys | Use longer backswing for deep returns |
| Rushes deep returns | Not using extra time | Errors | Take your time, the deep ball gives you time |
| Short volleys on deep returns | Not aiming deep | Attacking ball for opponent | Aim deep to baseline |

## Practical Application: "Deeper Position, Longer Swing, Deep Volley"

Against extreme-depth returns: (1) position deeper, (2) use a longer backswing, (3) aim deep. The deep ball gives you time — use it.
