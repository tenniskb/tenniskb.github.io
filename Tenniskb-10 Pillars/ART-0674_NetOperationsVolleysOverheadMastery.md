# ART-0674: The Volley Against the Return Down the Line — Handling Direct Returns

## Executive Summary

Returns hit down the line are the most direct passing shot in doubles — the ball travels straight down the sideline, past the net player. When you are the net player facing a down-the-line return, you must use the block volley to handle the pace and redirect the ball. The down-the-line return is a high-risk, high-reward shot for the returner — if they miss, they lose the point; if they hit it well, it is a winner.

## The Down-the-Line Return

The down-the-line return: (1) travels straight down the sideline, (2) is hit with pace (the returner is going for a winner), (3) is aimed past the net player. At the net, the down-the-line return is difficult because the ball travels past your body, requiring a quick lateral movement and block volley.

## Volley Technique Against Down-the-Line Returns

Against down-the-line returns: (1) use the block volley (absorb pace, redirect), (2) step laterally with the cross-step if needed, (3) hold the racket in front of your body, (4) redirect cross-court (the safest option), (5) use a firm wrist. The key is to block the ball and redirect it to safety.

## Common Errors

Swinging at the down-the-line return (no time). Not stepping laterally (reaching, not moving). Breaking the wrist (loss of control). Not using the block volley.

## Summary

Down-the-line returns demand the block volley: absorb pace, redirect cross-court, use a firm wrist. The block volley is your primary weapon against this direct passing shot.

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Failure | Result | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Swings at down-the-line return | No block technique | Late contact | Use block volley, no swing |
| Cannot reach down-the-line return | Not stepping laterally | Missed volley | Use cross-step for lateral movement |
| Wrist breaks on down-the-line | Grip too soft | Erratic direction | Firm wrist, increase grip pressure |
| Does not redirect cross-court | Going for winner | Error | Redirect cross-court, prioritize safety |

## Practical Application: "Block and Redirect Cross-Court"

Against down-the-line returns: (1) block the ball, (2) redirect cross-court. The block volley and cross-court redirect are the safest, highest-percentage options.
