# ART-0681: The Volley Against the Return with Extreme Slice — Handling Heavy Backspin

## Executive Summary

Returns with extreme slice are a challenge for net players because the heavy backspin causes the ball to stay low and skid after the bounce. When you are the net player facing a return with extreme slice, you must get very low, open the racket face, and use the slice volley to handle the low, skidding ball. The extreme slice return is particularly effective on grass and fast hard courts where the ball skids dramatically.

## The Extreme Slice Return

The extreme slice return: (1) is hit with heavy backspin, (2) stays low after the bounce, (3) skids on fast surfaces. At the net, the extreme slice return forces contact below net height, requiring deep knee bend and an open racket face.

## Technique for Handling Extreme Slice Returns

Against extreme slice returns: (1) bend your knees deeply to get very low, (2) open the racket face more than usual, (3) use the slice volley (high-to-low path), (4) aim for depth, (5) use a firm wrist. The key is getting very low and opening the face to lift the ball over the net.

## Common Errors

Standing upright (cannot reach the low ball). Closing the face (ball goes into the net). Not using enough backspin (the ball pops up). Breaking the wrist (loss of control).

## Summary

Extreme slice returns demand a low volley technique: deep knee bend, open racket face, slice volley path. Get low, open up, and slice through the ball.

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Failure | Result | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Volley into net | Standing upright, face closed | Net violation | Bend knees deeply, open face |
| Volley pops up | Face too open, no backspin | Attacking ball for opponent | Use slice volley, add backspin |
| Cannot reach low slice | Poor knee bend | Missed volley | Get lower, bend knees deeper |
| Wrist breaks on slice volley | Grip too soft | Erratic direction | Firm wrist, increase grip pressure |

## Practical Application: "Get Very Low, Open Up, Slice Through"

Against extreme slice returns: (1) get very low with deep knee bend, (2) open the racket face, (3) slice through the ball with a high-to-low path. The lower you get, the better your slice volley.
