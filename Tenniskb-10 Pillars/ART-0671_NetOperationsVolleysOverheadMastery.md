# ART-0671: The Volley Against the Slice Serve — Handling Wide, Swinging Returns

## Executive Summary

The slice serve swings wide, pulling the returner off the court. When you are the net player in doubles facing a slice serve return, the ball arrives from an extreme angle, requiring lateral movement and a compact volley. Handling slice serve returns requires quick lateral movement, a firm wrist, and the ability to redirect the ball from wide positions.

## The Slice Serve Return

The slice serve return: (1) is hit from wide of the court (the slice pulls the returner off), (2) has slice backspin that stays low, (3) is hit with moderate pace. At the net, the slice serve return arrives from an extreme angle, requiring lateral movement to intercept.

## Volley Technique Against Slice Serve Returns

Against slice serve returns: (1) use the cross-step to move laterally, (2) get low for the low-bouncing slice, (3) use a firm wrist to handle the pace, (4) redirect cross-court or down-the-line, (5) aim for depth. The key is quick lateral movement followed by a compact volley.

## Common Errors

Not moving laterally (reaching instead of stepping). Standing upright (cannot handle the low slice). Breaking the wrist (loss of control). Not anticipating the wide angle.

## Summary

Slice serve returns demand lateral movement and a compact volley. Use the cross-step to reach wide balls, get low for the slice, and redirect with a firm wrist.

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Failure | Result | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cannot reach wide slice returns | Not using cross-step | Missed volleys | Drill cross-step for lateral movement |
| Volley goes long on slice | Face too open | Ball past baseline | Close face slightly, firm wrist |
| Standing upright for slice | Not getting low | Frame shots | Bend knees deeply |
| Slow reaction to wide slice | Not anticipating angle | Late volleys | Watch server's racket face |

## Practical Application: "Cross-Step, Get Low, Redirect"

Against slice serve returns: (1) cross-step to reach the wide ball, (2) get low for the slice, (3) redirect with a firm wrist. Quick lateral movement is the key.
