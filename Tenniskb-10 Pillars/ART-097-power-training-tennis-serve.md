# ART:097: Power Training for the Tennis Serve

## Executive Summary
The tennis serve demands the highest power output of any tennis stroke, requiring explosive force production from the entire kinetic chain. Power training for the serve must address each link in the chain—from the leg drive through the trunk rotation to the shoulder internal rotation and forearm pronation that produce final racket head speed. This article presents specific power training exercises that target the serve's unique demands.

## Serve-Specific Power Exercises

### Leg Drive Power
- **Power cleans**: Develop explosive triple extension (ankle, knee, hip)
- **Box jumps**: Develop vertical explosive power
- **Split-stance jumps**: Develop unilateral leg drive power

### Trunk Rotation Power
- **Rotational medicine ball throws**: Develop explosive trunk rotation
- **Cable chops and lifts**: Develop rotational power against resistance
- **Landmine rotations**: Develop rotational power in a serve-like pattern

### Shoulder Internal Rotation Power
- **Medicine ball throws from a serve position**: Develop explosive internal rotation
- **Resistance band internal rotation at speed**: Develop rapid internal rotation
- **Plyometric push-ups**: Develop reactive shoulder power

### Forearm Pronation Power
- **Pronation with dumbbell**: Develop pronator strength
- **Towel snaps with pronation**: Develop rapid pronation speed
- **Resistance band pronation at speed**: Develop rapid pronation coordination

## Practical Application: "The Serve Power Routine"
Perform a serve-specific power routine 2-3 times per week: (1) 3 sets of 3 power cleans, (2) 3 sets of 5 box jumps, (3) 3 sets of 5 rotational medicine ball throws each side, (4) 3 sets of 5 medicine ball throws from a serve position, (5) 3 sets of 10 rapid pronation exercises. This 15-minute routine develops the specific power qualities that translate directly to increased serve velocity.
