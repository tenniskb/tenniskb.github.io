# ART-0679: The Volley Against the Return Lob Behind — Handling Deep Lobs

## Executive Summary

Deep lobs hit behind the net player are a standard defensive tactic in doubles — the returner lobs the ball deep over the net player's head, forcing a long retreat and a difficult overhead. When you are the net player facing a deep lob behind you, you must use the drop-step, retreat efficiently to the baseline, and hit a full overhead smash. The deep lob is the most common way to neutralize an aggressive net player.

## The Deep Lob Behind the Net Player

The deep lob behind the net player: (1) is hit high and deep, (2) lands near or beyond the baseline, (3) forces the net player to retreat a long distance. At the net, the deep lob behind you is a defensive shot by the returner — their goal is to push you back and buy time to recover.

## Technique for Handling Deep Lobs Behind

Against deep lobs behind: (1) drop-step immediately, (2) retreat with crossover steps, (3) track the ball with your non-dominant arm, (4) position yourself under the ball, (5) hit a full overhead smash with a deep trophy position. The key is early recognition, efficient retreat, and a full overhead motion.

## Common Errors

Not drop-stepping (slow start on retreat). Not tracking the ball (poor overhead contact). Abbreviating the trophy position (reduced power). Rushing the overhead (errors). Not communicating with your partner.

## Summary

Deep lobs behind the net player demand the full overhead smash: drop-step, retreat, track the ball, and smash with a deep trophy position. Practice the overhead until it is automatic.

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Failure | Result | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Slow retreat on deep lob | No drop-step | Late overhead | Drop-step immediately |
| Misses overhead | Poor tracking | Errors | Point non-dominant arm at ball |
| Overhead lacks power | Abbreviated trophy position | Weak overhead | Deepen trophy position, full throw |
| Rushes overhead | Impatience | Errors | Take your time, get in position |

## Practical Application: "Drop-Step, Retreat, Full Throw"

Against deep lobs behind: (1) drop-step, (2) retreat with crossover steps, (3) hit a full overhead smash with a deep trophy position. The deep lob is manageable with the right technique.
