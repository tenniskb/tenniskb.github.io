# ART-0680: The Volley Against the Return with Extreme Topspin — Handling Heavy Spin

## Executive Summary

Returns with extreme topspin are a challenge for net players because the heavy spin causes the ball to kick high and wide off the racket face. When you are the net player facing a return with extreme topspin, you must close the racket face more than usual, let the ball drop to a comfortable height, and use a downward punch volley. The extreme topspin return is particularly effective against net players who are not prepared for the kick.

## The Extreme Topspin Return

The extreme topspin return: (1) is hit with heavy topspin, (2) bounces high and kicks wide, (3) is difficult to control at the net. At the net, the extreme topspin return forces contact above the ideal volley height and causes the ball to jump off the racket face unpredictably.

## Technique for Handling Extreme Topspin Returns

Against extreme topspin returns: (1) let the ball drop to a comfortable contact height, (2) close the racket face more than usual, (3) use a downward punch volley, (4) aim deep to the baseline, (5) use a firm wrist to resist the spin. The key is to let the ball drop and close the face to handle the heavy topspin.

## Common Errors

Not letting the ball drop (contact too high). Not closing the face enough (ball goes long). Using a flat punch (the topspin causes the ball to jump). Not anticipating the high bounce.

## Summary

Extreme topspin returns demand a closed racket face and downward punch volley. Let the ball drop, close the face, and punch downward to control the heavy spin.

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Failure | Result | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Volley goes long | Face too open, not handling topspin | Ball past baseline | Close face more, punch downward |
| Contact too high | Not letting ball drop | Weak volley | Let ball drop to comfortable height |
| Cannot handle kick | Not anticipating topspin | Erratic contact | Close face, anticipate kick |
| Volley lacks downward angle | Flat punch | Easy return | Use downward punch volley |

## Practical Application: "Let It Drop, Close the Face, Punch Down"

Against extreme topspin returns: (1) let the ball drop, (2) close the racket face, (3) punch downward. The heavy spin is manageable with the right technique.
