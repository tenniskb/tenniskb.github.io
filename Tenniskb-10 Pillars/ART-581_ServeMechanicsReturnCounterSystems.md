# ART-581: Return Against Serve-and-Volley — Passing and Lob Options

## Executive Summary

Serve-and-volley servers present unique challenges for returners because they immediately follow their serve to the net. This article covers passing shot technique, lob options, and tactical strategies for handling net-rushers.

## Understanding the Serve-and-Volley

### Why Servers Rush the Net
- To take time away from the returner
- To hit a volley rather than a groundstroke
- To put pressure on the returner to hit a perfect pass

### The Challenge for Returners
- Less time to hit the return
- Must hit a passing shot or lob
- Must deal with the net player's positioning

## Passing Shot Technique

### Down-the-Line Pass
- Hit the return down the line past the server
- Requires precise timing and placement
- Most effective when the server is moving to the net

### Crosscourt Pass
- Hit the return crosscourt past the server
- Safer than down-the-line due to the lower net in the middle
- Effective when the server is positioned in the middle

### At the Server's Feet
- Aim the return at the server's feet as they approach the net
- Forces the server to hit up on a low volley
- Creates a weak reply that you can attack

## Lob Options

### The Defensive Lob
- Lob the ball over the server's head when you are off-balance
- Buys time to recover your position
- Effective when you cannot hit a passing shot

### The Offensive Lob
- Lob the ball over the server when they are crowding the net
- Can win the point outright if the lob is deep
- Effective against short net-rushers

### The Topspin Lob
- Hit a topspin lob that dips after clearing the net
- Harder for the server to reach than a flat lob
- Effective when the server is deep at the net

## Tactical Considerations

### Mix Up Your Returns
- Use passing shots and lobs to keep the server guessing
- Do not become predictable
- Vary the direction and depth of your returns

### Target the Server's Weaker Volley
- Most players have a weaker backhand volley
- Direct returns to the server's weaker side
- Forces them to hit from their weaker side

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Failure | Result | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cannot hit passing shots | Poor technique | Passing shots miss | Passing shot drills; technique practice |
| Lobs are too short | Insufficient power | Lobs intercepted | Lob practice; power development |
| Predictable returns | No variation | Server anticipates | Variation drills; mixed returns |

## Practical Application: "The Net-Rusher Drill"

Have a partner serve and rush the net. Practice hitting passing shots down the line and crosscourt, lobs over the partner's head, and returns at the partner's feet. This drill develops the specific skills needed for handling serve-and-volley servers.
