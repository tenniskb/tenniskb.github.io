# ART-0675: The Volley Against the Return Cross-Court — Handling Angled Returns

## Executive Summary

Returns hit cross-court are the most common return in doubles — the ball travels diagonally across the court toward the net player. When you are the net player facing a cross-court return, you are well-positioned to intercept the ball because it comes directly to you. The cross-court return is your opportunity to poach and win the point with a decisive volley.

## The Cross-Court Return

The cross-court return: (1) travels diagonally across the court, (2) is hit toward the net player, (3) is the most common return in doubles. At the net, the cross-court return is the shot you are waiting for — it comes to you, allowing an aggressive punch volley.

## Volley Technique Against Cross-Court Returns

Against cross-court returns: (1) use the punch volley (compact, powerful), (2) redirect down-the-line or cross-court, (3) aim for the open court or the opponent's feet, (4) step into the ball for power, (5) use a firm wrist. The key is to be aggressive — the cross-court return is your opportunity to win the point.

## Tactical Application

Against cross-court returns: (1) poach aggressively (intercept the cross-court return), (2) redirect down-the-line (the opponent is out of position on the cross-court side), (3) aim for the opponent's feet (the most difficult return), (4) communicate with your partner about coverage. The cross-court return is your bread-and-butter — poach and finish the point.

## Common Errors

Not poaching aggressively enough (missed opportunities). Using a block volley instead of a punch volley (the cross-court return is your shot to attack). Not redirecting down-the-line (the safest, highest-percentage shot). Not communicating with your partner.

## Summary

Cross-court returns are your opportunity to attack. Use the punch volley, redirect down-the-line, and communicate with your partner. Poach aggressively and finish the point.

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Failure | Result | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Not poaching cross-court returns | Passive net play | Missed opportunities | Poach on every cross-court return |
| Uses block volley on cross-court | Not attacking | Weak volley | Use punch volley, be aggressive |
| Does not redirect down-the-line | Hitting cross-court | Opponent recovers | Redirect down-the-line |
| No communication with partner | Confusion | Open court | Call "mine/yours" for every return |

## Practical Application: "Poach, Punch, Redirect"

Against cross-court returns: (1) poach the ball, (2) punch volley, (3) redirect down-the-line. The cross-court return is your shot to attack — take it.
