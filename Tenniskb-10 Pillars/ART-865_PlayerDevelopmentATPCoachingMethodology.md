# ART-865: Flexibility and Mobility Programs for Tennis

## Executive Summary

Flexibility (the ability of muscles to lengthen) and mobility (the ability of joints to move through their full range of motion) are essential for tennis performance and injury prevention. Tennis demands extreme ranges of motion: shoulder internal rotation during serving, hip rotation during groundstrokes, ankle dorsiflexion during low ready positions, and thoracic spine rotation during stroke production. A comprehensive flexibility and mobility program includes dynamic stretching (for warm-up), static stretching (for cool-down and dedicated flexibility sessions), and joint mobility exercises (for maintaining and improving range of motion). Regular flexibility and mobility work improves stroke mechanics, reduces injury risk, and enhances movement efficiency.

## Tennis-Specific Flexibility Requirements

### Shoulder Flexibility

| Movement | Requirement | Assessment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Internal Rotation | 90+ degrees (dominant shoulder) | Goniometer measurement |
| External Rotation | 90+ degrees (dominant shoulder) | Goniometer measurement |
| Total Arc | 180+ degrees (internal + external) | Goniometer measurement |
| GIRD | <18 degrees difference (dominant vs. non-dominant) | Goniometer measurement |

**GIRD (Glenohumeral Internal Rotation Deficit):** A difference of >18 degrees between dominant and non-dominant shoulder internal rotation indicates increased injury risk.

### Hip Flexibility

| Movement | Requirement | Assessment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Internal Rotation | 35+ degrees | Goniometer measurement |
| External Rotation | 45+ degrees | Goniometer measurement |
| Hip Flexion | 120+ degrees | Thomas test |
| Hip Extension | 20+ degrees | Thomas test |

### Spine Mobility

| Movement | Requirement | Assessment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Thoracic Rotation | 45+ degrees each direction | Seated rotation test |
| Thoracic Extension | 20+ degrees | Prone extension test |
| Lumbar Flexion | Normal range | Forward bend test |

### Ankle Mobility

| Movement | Requirement | Assessment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Dorsiflexion | 30+ degrees | Knee-to-wall test |
| Plantarflexion | 50+ degrees | Goniometer measurement |
| Inversion/Eversion | Normal range | Goniometer measurement |

## Dynamic Stretching for Warm-Up

### Purpose

- Increase blood flow to muscles
- Raise muscle temperature
- Prepare joints for movement
- Activate the nervous system
- Improve range of motion for the upcoming activity

### Dynamic Stretching Routine (10 minutes)

| Exercise | Duration | Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Arm circles (forward and backward) | 30 sec each direction | Shoulder mobility |
| Leg swings (front-to-back) | 30 sec each leg | Hip flexion/extension |
| Leg swings (side-to-side) | 30 sec each leg | Hip abduction/adduction |
| Walking lunges with rotation | 1 min | Hip and trunk mobility |
| Thoracic spine rotations | 30 sec each side | Spine mobility |
| Hip circles | 30 sec each direction | Hip mobility |
| Ankle circles | 30 sec each ankle | Ankle mobility |
| Calf raises | 30 sec | Ankle mobility and activation |
| High knees | 30 sec | Hip flexion and activation |
| Butt kicks | 30 sec | Hamstring activation |

## Static Stretching for Cool-Down

### Purpose

- Restore muscle length after exercise
- Reduce muscle tension
- Improve long-term flexibility
- Promote relaxation and recovery

### Static Stretching Routine (15 minutes)

| Stretch | Duration | Target Area |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Standing quad stretch | 30 sec each leg | Quadriceps |
| Standing hamstring stretch | 30 sec each leg | Hamstrings |
| Hip flexor stretch (kneeling) | 30 sec each side | Hip flexors |
| Pigeon pose | 30 sec each side | Glutes and hip rotators |
| Shoulder cross-body stretch | 30 sec each arm | Posterior shoulder |
| Triceps stretch | 30 sec each arm | Triceps and shoulder |
| Chest stretch (doorway) | 30 sec | Pectorals |
| Lat stretch (kneeling) | 30 sec each side | Latissimus dorsi |
| Lower back stretch (child's pose) | 30 sec | Lower back |
| Neck stretches (all directions) | 20 sec each direction | Neck |
| Sleeper stretch | 30 sec each arm | Shoulder internal rotation |
| Forearm stretch (wrist flexor) | 30 sec each arm | Forearm flexors |
| Forearm stretch (wrist extensor) | 30 sec each arm | Forearm extensors |

## Joint Mobility Exercises

### Shoulder Mobility

**Shoulder CARs (Controlled Articular Rotations):**
- Slowly rotate the shoulder through its full range of motion
- 5 rotations forward, 5 rotations backward, each arm
- Focus on smooth, controlled movement

**Wall Slides:**
- Stand with back against a wall
- Arms in a goalpost position (elbows at 90 degrees)
- Slide arms up and down the wall, maintaining contact
- 2 sets of 10 reps

**Band Pull-Aparts:**
- Hold a resistance band in front of you
- Pull the band apart, squeezing the shoulder blades
- 2 sets of 15 reps

### Hip Mobility

**Hip CARs:**
- Slowly rotate the hip through its full range of motion
- 5 rotations each direction, each leg
- Focus on smooth, controlled movement

**90/90 Hip Stretch:**
- Sit with one leg in front (90 degrees) and one to the side (90 degrees)
- Lean forward over the front leg
- 30 sec each side

**Couch Stretch:**
- Kneel with one foot behind you, shin against a wall or couch
- Squeeze the glute of the kneeling leg
- 30 sec each side

### Spine Mobility

**Cat-Cow:**
- On hands and knees, alternate between arching and rounding the back
- 10 reps, slow and controlled

**Thoracic Spine Rotation (Quadruped):**
- On hands and knees, place one hand behind the head
- Rotate the elbow toward the ceiling
- 10 reps each side

**Foam Roller Thoracic Extension:**
- Lie on a foam roller across the upper back
- Extend the spine over the roller
- 10 reps at different levels

### Ankle Mobility

**Knee-to-Wall Drill:**
- Stand facing a wall, one foot forward
- Bend the knee to touch the wall, keeping the heel down
- Move the foot back and repeat
- 10 reps each ankle

**Ankle CARs:**
- Slowly rotate the ankle through its full range of motion
- 5 rotations each direction, each ankle

## Flexibility and Mobility Program

### Daily Routine (20-30 minutes)

**Morning (10 minutes):**
- Dynamic stretching routine
- Joint mobility exercises (shoulder CARs, hip CARs, ankle CARs)

**Pre-Training/Match (10 minutes):**
- Dynamic stretching routine
- Neural activation exercises

**Post-Training/Match (15 minutes):**
- Static stretching routine
- Foam rolling (5 minutes)

### Dedicated Flexibility Session (30-45 minutes, 2-3x/week)

- Dynamic stretching: 10 minutes
- Joint mobility exercises: 10 minutes
- Static stretching: 15 minutes
- Foam rolling: 10 minutes

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Failure | Result | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Shoulder pain during serving | GIRD, poor shoulder mobility | Rotator cuff tendinopathy | Sleeper stretches; shoulder CARs; band pull-aparts |
| Limited hip rotation during groundstrokes | Poor hip flexibility | Reduced power, compensatory movement | Hip CARs; 90/90 stretch; couch stretch |
| Low ready position difficult to maintain | Poor ankle dorsiflexion, tight calves | Standing too upright, slow first step | Knee-to-wall drill; calf stretches; ankle CARs |
| Lower back pain during serving | Poor thoracic mobility, excessive lumbar extension | Disc issues, muscle strains | Thoracic spine mobility; cat-cow; foam roller extension |
| Forearm and wrist injuries | Poor forearm flexibility | Tendinopathy, TFCC tears | Forearm stretches; wrist CARs; grip strength balance |
