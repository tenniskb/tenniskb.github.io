# ART-868: Injury Prevention Exercises for Tennis

## Executive Summary

Injury prevention exercises are structured prehabilitation programs designed to reduce the risk of common tennis injuries by addressing modifiable risk factors: muscle imbalances, flexibility deficits, poor movement quality, and inadequate joint stability. Research indicates that structured injury prevention programs can reduce tennis injury incidence by 30-50%. A comprehensive tennis injury prevention program addresses the five most commonly injured areas: shoulder, elbow, lower back, knee, and ankle. These exercises should be performed 3-4 times per week, either as part of the warm-up or as a dedicated prehabilitation session.

## Shoulder Prehabilitation

### Common Shoulder Injuries in Tennis

- Rotator cuff tendinopathy
- Shoulder impingement
- Labral tears
- GIRD (Glenohumeral Internal Rotation Deficit)

### Shoulder Prehabilitation Program (Thrower's Ten)

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| External Rotation at 0 degrees Abduction | 2 x 15 | Rotator cuff strength |
| Internal Rotation at 0 degrees Abduction | 2 x 15 | Rotator cuff strength |
| External Rotation at 90 degrees Abduction | 2 x 15 | Rotator cuff strength |
| Internal Rotation at 90 degrees Abduction | 2 x 15 | Rotator cuff strength |
| Shoulder Abduction | 2 x 15 | Deltoid and rotator cuff |
| Scaption (Scapular Plane Elevation) | 2 x 15 | Supraspinatus and deltoid |
| Prone Horizontal Abduction | 2 x 15 | Posterior shoulder and scapular |
| Prone Rowing | 2 x 15 | Rhomboids and middle trapezius |
| Prone External Rotation | 2 x 15 | Infraspinatus and teres minor |
| Push-Ups (Plus) | 2 x 15 | Serratus anterior and shoulder stability |

### Shoulder Flexibility

**Sleeper Stretch:**
- Lie on the dominant side, elbow at 90 degrees
- Use the opposite hand to push the forearm toward the floor
- Stretches the posterior capsule and internal rotators
- Hold 30 seconds, 3 reps each side

**Cross-Body Stretch:**
- Pull the dominant arm across the body
- Stretches the posterior shoulder
- Hold 30 seconds, 3 reps each side

## Elbow Prehabilitation

### Common Elbow Injuries in Tennis

- Lateral epicondylitis (Tennis Elbow)
- Medial Epicondylitis (Golfer's Elbow)
- UCL (Ulnar Collateral Ligament) strain

### Elbow Prehabilitation Program

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Wrist Curl (Flexion) | 2 x 15 | Forearm flexor strength |
| Wrist Extension | 2 x 15 | Forearm extensor strength |
| Reverse Wrist Curl | 2 x 15 | Forearm extensor strength |
| Forearm Pronation | 2 x 15 | Pronator strength |
| Forearm Supination | 2 x 15 | Supinator strength |
| Eccentric Wrist Extension | 2 x 15 | Eccentric extensor strength (tennis elbow prevention) |
| Ball Squeeze | 2 x 15 | Grip strength and forearm |
| Finger Extensions (Band) | 2 x 15 | Extensor strength balance |

### Elbow Flexibility

**Wrist Flexor Stretch:**
- Extend the arm, palm up
- Pull the fingers back with the other hand
- Hold 30 seconds, 3 reps each arm

**Wrist Extensor Stretch:**
- Extend the arm, palm down
- Push the hand down with the other hand
- Hold 30 seconds, 3 reps each arm

## Lower Back Prehabilitation

### Common Lower Back Injuries in Tennis

- Muscle strains
- Disc herniation
- Spondylolysis (stress fracture)
- Sacroiliac joint dysfunction

### Lower Back Prehabilitation Program

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Plank | 3 x 30-60 sec | Core stability (anti-extension) |
| Side Plank | 3 x 30 sec each side | Core stability (anti-lateral flexion) |
| Dead Bug | 3 x 10 each side | Core stability with limb movement |
| Bird Dog | 3 x 10 each side | Contralateral stability |
| Cat-Cow | 3 x 10 | Spine mobility |
| Pelvic Tilt | 3 x 15 | Lumbar stability awareness |
| Glute Bridge | 3 x 15 | Glute and hip stability |
| Single-Leg Glute Bridge | 3 x 10 each leg | Unilateral hip stability |

### Lower Back Flexibility

**Child's Pose:**
- Kneel and reach forward, sitting back on heels
- Stretches the lower back and latissimus dorsi
- Hold 30 seconds, 3 reps

**Knee-to-Chest Stretch:**
- Lie on back, pull knees to chest
- Stretches the lower back
- Hold 30 seconds, 3 reps

**Pigeon Pose:**
- Front leg bent, back leg extended
- Stretches the hip rotators and lower back
- Hold 30 seconds, 3 reps each side

## Knee Prehabilitation

### Common Knee Injuries in Tennis

- Patellar tendinopathy (Jumper's Knee)
- Meniscal tears
- Patellofemoral pain syndrome
- ACL injuries (less common)

### Knee Prehabilitation Program

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Bodyweight Squat | 3 x 15 | Quad and glute strength |
| Single-Leg Squat | 3 x 10 each leg | Unilateral strength and stability |
| Lateral Band Walk | 3 x 15 steps each direction | Glute medius strength |
| Nordic Hamstring Curl | 3 x 8 | Eccentric hamstring strength |
| Calf Raise (Straight Knee) | 3 x 15 | Gastrocnemius strength |
| Calf Raise (Bent Knee) | 3 x 15 | Soleus strength |
| Step-Down | 3 x 10 each leg | Quad control and knee stability |
| Single-Leg Balance | 3 x 30 sec each leg | Proprioception and stability |

### Knee Flexibility

**Quad Stretch:**
- Standing, pull heel to buttock
- Hold 30 seconds, 3 reps each leg

**Hamstring Stretch:**
- Standing, reach toward toes
- Hold 30 seconds, 3 reps

**Hip Flexor Stretch:**
- Kneeling lunge, push hips forward
- Hold 30 seconds, 3 reps each side

## Ankle Prehabilitation

### Common Ankle Injuries in Tennis

- Lateral ankle sprains
- Achilles tendinopathy
- Stress fractures

### Ankle Prehabilitation Program

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Single-Leg Balance (Eyes Open) | 3 x 30 sec each leg | Balance and proprioception |
| Single-Leg Balance (Eyes Closed) | 3 x 20 sec each leg | Proprioception |
| Single-Leg Balance on Unstable Surface | 3 x 30 sec each leg | Advanced proprioception |
| Calf Raise | 3 x 15 | Calf strength |
| Eccentric Calf Lowering | 3 x 10 each leg | Eccentric calf strength (Achilles prevention) |
| Ankle Dorsiflexion Mobilization | 3 x 10 each ankle | Ankle mobility |
| Star Excursion Balance Test Practice | 3 x 5 reaches each direction | Dynamic balance |

### Ankle Flexibility

**Calf Stretch (Gastrocnemius):**
- Lean against a wall, back leg straight
- Hold 30 seconds, 3 reps each leg

**Calf Stretch (Soleus):**
- Lean against a wall, back leg bent
- Hold 30 seconds, 3 reps each leg

## Practical Application: "The 15-Minute Prehabilitation Session"

Combine the most important exercises from each area:

**Shoulder:** External rotation (90 degrees abduction): 2 x 15
**Elbow:** Eccentric wrist extension: 2 x 15
**Lower Back:** Plank: 2 x 45 sec; Dead bug: 2 x 8 each side
**Knee:** Single-leg squat: 2 x 8 each leg; Nordic hamstring curl: 2 x 6
**Ankle:** Single-leg balance: 2 x 30 sec each leg

Total time: 15 minutes. Perform 3-4 times per week.

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Failure | Result | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Recurring shoulder pain | Weak rotator cuff, GIRD | Rotator cuff tendinopathy | Thrower's Ten program; sleeper stretches; shoulder mobility |
| Tennis elbow symptoms | Weak forearm extensors, poor technique | Lateral epicondylitis | Eccentric wrist extension; forearm strengthening; technique review |
| Lower back pain during serving | Poor core stability, excessive lumbar extension | Disc issues, muscle strains | Core stability program; anti-extension exercises; technique review |
| Knee pain during jumping | Weak quadriceps, poor landing mechanics | Patellar tendinopathy | Quad strengthening; eccentric squat program; landing mechanics |
| Ankle instability | Poor proprioception, weak stabilizers | Recurrent ankle sprains | Balance training; proprioceptive exercises; ankle strengthening |
