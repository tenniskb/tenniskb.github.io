# ART-577: Return and Nutrition — Energy Systems for Return Games

## Executive Summary

Proper nutrition and hydration are essential for maintaining return quality throughout a match. This article provides a nutrition guide focused on the energy demands of return games.

## Pre-Match Nutrition

### 2-3 Hours Before the Match
- A balanced meal with carbohydrates, protein, and healthy fats
- Examples: Grilled chicken with rice and vegetables; pasta with lean meat sauce
- Avoid heavy, fatty foods that digest slowly

### 30-60 Minutes Before the Match
- A light snack for quick energy
- Examples: Banana, energy bar, toast with peanut butter
- Avoid high-sugar foods that cause energy crashes

## During-Match Nutrition

### Between Points
- Sip water or electrolyte drinks between points
- Avoid drinking too much at once

### During Changeovers
- Drink 4-8 oz of water or electrolyte drink
- Eat a small snack if needed (energy gel, banana, orange slices)

## Post-Match Nutrition

### Within 30 Minutes
- Consume a recovery meal with carbohydrates and protein
- Examples: Chocolate milk, protein shake, chicken sandwich
- Aim for a 3:1 ratio of carbohydrates to protein

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Failure | Result | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Energy drops during return games | Poor pre-match nutrition | Declining return quality | Pre-match meal planning; balanced nutrition |
| Dehydration symptoms during returning | Inadequate hydration | Reduced performance | Hydration protocol; electrolyte supplementation |
| Poor recovery after matches | No post-match nutrition | Delayed recovery | Post-match meal planning; recovery nutrition |

## Practical Application: "The Nutrition Plan"

Create a personalized nutrition plan for match days: 2-3 hours before (balanced meal), 30-60 minutes before (light snack), during the match (sip water between points, small snacks during changeovers), and after the match (recovery meal within 30 minutes).
