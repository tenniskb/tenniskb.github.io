# ART-582: Return in Mixed Doubles — Gender-Specific Tactical Considerations

## Executive Summary

Mixed doubles presents unique return challenges because of the different strengths and weaknesses typically associated with male and female players. This article covers gender-specific tactics for effective mixed doubles returning.

## Mixed Doubles Dynamics

### Common Configurations
- Male server vs. female returner
- Female server vs. male returner
- Male server vs. male returner
- Female server vs. female returner

### Key Differences
- Men generally serve faster and with more power
- Women often have better placement and spin
- Return tactics should account for these differences

## Returning the Male Server

### Positioning
- Stand deeper to handle faster serves
- Use block returns on fast first serves
- Be ready to handle powerful second serves

### Tactical Options
- Target the female partner at the net with returns
- Direct returns to the male server's weaker side
- Use lobs to neutralize the net player

## Returning the Female Server

### Positioning
- Stand closer to the baseline (slower serves)
- Use drive returns on second serves
- Be ready to attack weaker serves

### Tactical Options
- Direct returns to the female server's weaker side
- Attack second serves aggressively
- Use the return to set up your net player

## Mixed Doubles Return Tactics

### Target the Weaker Player
- If the female is the weaker player, direct returns to her
- If the male is the weaker player, direct returns to him
- This puts pressure on the weaker player and creates errors

### Use the Lob
- The lob is effective against aggressive net players
- Lob over the net player's head
- This neutralizes the net player's advantage

### Communicate with Your Partner
- Signal your intended return direction
- Coordinate with your net partner
- Adjust positioning based on your partner's signals

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Failure | Result | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cannot handle male server's pace | Poor positioning | Mishits and errors | Move deeper; block return practice |
| Not attacking female server's serves | Passive returning | Missed opportunities | Aggressive return drills; attack practice |
| No communication with partner | No signals | Confusion and errors | Signal practice; communication drills |

## Practical Application: "The Mixed Doubles Return Drill"

Practice returning in mixed doubles scenarios: male server to female returner, female server to male returner. Focus on adjusting positioning and tactics based on the server's gender and tendencies.
