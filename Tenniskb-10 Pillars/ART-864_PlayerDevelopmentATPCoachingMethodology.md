# ART-864: Power Development for Tennis Players

## Executive Summary

Power—the ability to generate maximum force in minimum time—is the physical quality that distinguishes elite tennis players from average ones. Power determines serve speed, groundstroke pace, and the explosiveness of first steps and directional changes. Tennis-specific power development focuses on lower body power (for serve leg drive and court movement), rotational power (for stroke production), and upper body power (for serves and overheads). A comprehensive power training program includes Olympic lifting variations, plyometric exercises, medicine ball throws, and tennis-specific power drills that transfer directly to on-court performance.

## The Physics of Tennis Power

### Power = Force x Velocity

In tennis, power manifests as:
- **Serve power:** Leg drive + trunk rotation + shoulder internal rotation + wrist snap
- **Groundstroke power:** Leg drive + hip rotation + shoulder rotation + arm extension
- **Movement power:** First-step explosion + acceleration + deceleration

### Power Contribution by Segment

| Segment | Serve Power | Forehand Power |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Legs and Hip Drive | 40-50% | 40-50% |
| Trunk Rotation | 25-35% | 25-35% |
| Shoulder Rotation | 15-20% | 15-20% |
| Arm and Wrist | 10-15% | 10-15% |

## Olympic Lifting for Tennis

### Hang Clean

**Purpose:** Develops full-body explosive power (triple extension of hips, knees, ankles).

**Technique:**
- Start with barbell at mid-thigh
- Explosive extension of hips, knees, and ankles
- Pull the barbell upward, catching at the shoulders
- Lower to starting position

**Sets and Reps:** 4 sets of 3-5 reps at 75-85% 1RM
**Rest:** 2-3 minutes between sets
**Frequency:** 2 sessions per week

### Push Press

**Purpose:** Develops upper body pressing power with leg drive.

**Technique:**
- Barbell at shoulders
- Dip with the knees (quarter squat)
- Explosive extension, pressing the barbell overhead
- Lower to shoulders

**Sets and Reps:** 4 sets of 3-5 reps at 75-85% 1RM
**Rest:** 2-3 minutes between sets
**Frequency:** 2 sessions per week

### Hang Snatch

**Purpose:** Develops explosive pulling power and overhead stability.

**Technique:**
- Start with barbell at mid-thigh
- Explosive extension, pulling the barbell overhead
- Catch the barbell overhead in a squat position
- Stand up with the barbell overhead

**Sets and Reps:** 4 sets of 3-5 reps at 70-80% 1RM
**Rest:** 2-3 minutes between sets
**Frequency:** 1-2 sessions per week

## Plyometric Training for Tennis

### Lower Body Plyometrics

**Box Jumps (30-60 cm):**
- Stand facing a box
- Jump onto the box with both feet
- Step down (or drop down for advanced)
- 3 sets of 5 reps

**Depth Jumps (30-45 cm):**
- Stand on a box
- Step off the box (do not jump off)
- Upon landing, immediately jump vertically
- 3 sets of 5 reps

**Bounding (20m):**
- Run with exaggerated, explosive strides
- Emphasize horizontal distance per stride
- 3 sets of 20m

**Single-Leg Hops (10m):**
- Hop forward on one leg
- Emphasize distance and height
- 3 sets of 10m per leg

**Lateral Box Jumps:**
- Jump laterally onto a box
- Step down and repeat to the other side
- 3 sets of 5 reps per side

### Upper Body Plyometrics

**Medicine Ball Chest Pass:**
- Hold a 3-5 kg medicine ball at chest
- Explosively pass to a partner or wall
- Catch and repeat immediately
- 3 sets of 8 reps

**Medicine Ball Rotational Throw:**
- Stand perpendicular to a wall
- Rotate the trunk and throw the ball against the wall
- Catch and repeat
- 3 sets of 6 reps per side

**Medicine Ball Overhead Throw:**
- Hold the ball overhead
- Explosively throw it forward (like an overhead smash)
- 3 sets of 8 reps

**Plyometric Push-Ups:**
- Perform a push-up with enough force to leave the hands from the ground
- Land softly and immediately repeat
- 3 sets of 8 reps

## Rotational Power Training

### Cable Woodchops

**High to Low:**
- Stand sideways to a cable machine
- Pull the cable from high to low across the body
- Emphasize rotational power from the hips
- 3 sets of 8 reps per side

**Low to High:**
- Pull the cable from low to high across the body
- Emphasize rotational power and shoulder involvement
- 3 sets of 8 reps per side

### Medicine Ball Rotational Throws

**Side Throw:**
- Stand perpendicular to a wall
- Rotate the trunk and throw the ball against the wall
- Catch and repeat immediately
- 3 sets of 6 reps per side

**Shotput Throw:**
- Hold the ball at the shoulder
- Rotate and throw the ball (like a shotput)
- 3 sets of 6 reps per side

### Landmine Rotational Press

- Hold the end of a barbell in a landmine setup
- Rotate and press the barbell upward
- 3 sets of 8 reps per side

## Tennis-Specific Power Drills

### Serve Power Drill

**Medicine Ball Overhead Throw (Simulating Serve):**
- Stand in the service stance
- Hold a medicine ball overhead
- Simulate the service motion, throwing the ball forward
- 3 sets of 8 reps

### Forehand Power Drill

**Medicine Ball Rotational Throw (Simulating Forehand):**
- Stand in a forehand stance
- Hold a medicine ball at the side
- Rotate and throw the ball (simulating a forehand swing)
- 3 sets of 6 reps per side

### First-Step Power Drill

**Reactive Box Jump:**
- Stand in the ready position
- Coach provides a visual or auditory cue
- Immediately jump onto a box
- 3 sets of 6 reps

## Power Training Program

### Off-Season Power Program (3 sessions/week)

**Session 1: Lower Body Power**
- Hang Clean: 4 x 4
- Box Jumps: 3 x 5
- Bounding: 3 x 20m
- Single-Leg Hops: 3 x 10m per leg

**Session 2: Rotational Power**
- Medicine Ball Rotational Throw: 3 x 6 per side
- Cable Woodchop (High to Low): 3 x 8 per side
- Cable Woodchop (Low to High): 3 x 8 per side
- Medicine Ball Shotput Throw: 3 x 6 per side

**Session 3: Upper Body Power**
- Push Press: 4 x 4
- Medicine Ball Chest Pass: 3 x 8
- Plyometric Push-Ups: 3 x 8
- Medicine Ball Overhead Throw: 3 x 8

### Pre-Season Power Program (2 sessions/week)

**Session 1: Full-Body Power**
- Hang Clean: 3 x 3
- Medicine Ball Rotational Throw: 3 x 5 per side
- Box Jumps: 3 x 5
- Cable Woodchop: 3 x 6 per side

**Session 2: Tennis-Specific Power**
- Medicine Ball Overhead Throw (serve simulation): 3 x 8
- Medicine Ball Rotational Throw (forehand simulation): 3 x 6 per side
- Reactive Box Jump: 3 x 6
- Depth Jumps: 3 x 5

### In-Season Power Maintenance (1-2 sessions/week)

**Session 1: Power Maintenance**
- Hang Clean: 3 x 3
- Box Jumps: 3 x 5
- Medicine Ball Rotational Throw: 3 x 5 per side

## Practical Application: "The Power Assessment Test"

Use three tests to assess power:

1. **Vertical Jump:** Target >60 cm (male), >50 cm (female)
2. **Standing Broad Jump:** Target >2.5 m (male), >2.0 m (female)
3. **Medicine Ball Rotational Throw:** Target >8 m (male), >6 m (female)

Retest every 8-12 weeks to track improvement.

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Failure | Result | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Serve speed plateau | Weak leg drive, poor power transfer | Predictable, returnable serve | Hang cleans; box jumps; medicine ball overhead throws |
| Groundstroke lacks pace | Insufficient rotational power | Passive rally shots | Rotational plyometrics; cable woodchops; medicine ball throws |
| Slow first step | Insufficient lower body power | Late arrivals, defensive strokes | Depth jumps; reactive plyometrics; single-leg hops |
| Upper body power deficit | Weak shoulder and arm contribution | Reduced serve and overhead power | Push press; plyometric push-ups; medicine ball chest passes |
| Power drops in later matches | Insufficient power endurance | Reduced explosiveness | Power endurance circuits; combined power and conditioning |
