# ART-838: Pre-Match Routines and Mental Preparation

## Executive Summary

Pre-match routines are structured sequences of physical, mental, and tactical preparation that transition the player from everyday readiness to competitive performance. Elite tennis players develop consistent pre-match routines that prepare the body for high-intensity activity, focus the mind on the task at hand, and build confidence for the challenges ahead. An effective pre-match routine addresses all dimensions of performance: physical warm-up, mental focus, tactical preparation, and emotional regulation. The routine should be personalized to the player's needs, practiced until it becomes automatic, and adapted for different match situations (Grand Slams, early-round matches, finals).

## The Purpose of Pre-Match Routines

### Physical Preparation

- Raise core temperature and prepare muscles for high-intensity activity
- Activate key muscle groups used in tennis (shoulders, hips, legs)
- Prepare the nervous system for explosive movements
- Establish rhythm and feel through progressive hitting

### Mental Preparation

- Shift from everyday mindset to competitive focus
- Build confidence through preparation and positive visualization
- Establish the emotional state for competition (energized, focused, composed)
- Clear the mind of distractions and irrelevant thoughts

### Tactical Preparation

- Review the opponent's tendencies and patterns
- Confirm the tactical plan for the match
- Visualize key tactical patterns and responses
- Prepare adjustments for different scenarios

### Emotional Preparation

- Establish optimal arousal level (not too high, not too low)
- Build confidence and positive expectations
- Develop composure and readiness for pressure
- Create a sense of readiness and anticipation

## The Pre-Match Routine Timeline

### The Day Before

**Tactical Review (30 minutes):**
- Review opponent scouting report
- Identify key patterns and tendencies
- Confirm tactical plan and adjustments
- Visualize the tactical plan in action

**Physical Preparation:**
- Light training session (30-45 minutes)
- Stretching and foam rolling
- Adequate hydration and nutrition

**Mental Preparation:**
- Visualization of the match (15 minutes)
- Positive self-talk and confidence building
- Review of pre-match routine and timing

**Logistics:**
- Confirm match time and venue
- Prepare equipment (rackets, strings, shoes, clothing)
- Plan travel to venue
- Set alarm and morning schedule

### Match Day Morning

**Wake-Up Routine:**
- Wake at the same time as always (consistency)
- Drink 500 mL of water immediately
- Light stretching in bed (5 minutes)
- Positive self-talk: "I am prepared and ready"

**Breakfast (3-4 hours before match):**
- Familiar, balanced meal (oats, eggs, fruit, toast)
- Avoid new or unusual foods
- Moderate portion (not too heavy)
- Continue hydration

**Pre-Match Snack (1-2 hours before):**
- Light, easily digestible snack (banana, energy bar)
- Sports drink or water
- Avoid heavy, fatty, or high-fiber foods

**Travel to Venue:**
- Arrive 60-90 minutes before the match
- Listen to energizing music (if desired)
- Mental rehearsal during travel

### At the Venue (60-90 minutes before match)

**Check-In (60 minutes before):**
- Report to tournament desk
- Use the restroom
- Gather equipment and prepare for warm-Up

**General Warm-Up (50-40 minutes before):**
- Light jogging or cycling (5 minutes)
- Dynamic mobility (5 minutes)
- Neural activation (5 minutes)

**On-Court Warm-Up (35-20 minutes before):**
- Progressive baseline rally (5 minutes)
- Serve and return practice (5 minutes)
- Approach and volley (5 minutes)
- Serve practice to all targets (5 minutes)

**Final Preparation (20-10 minutes before):**
- Hydration
- Mental rehearsal (visualize first 10 points)
- Tactical review (key points)
- Breathing and arousal regulation
- Positive self-talk and confidence building

**Walk to Court (5 minutes before):**
- Final hydration
- Shadow serves for rhythm
- Deep breath and focus cue
- Walk with confidence and purpose

## Components of the Pre-Match Routine

### The Physical Warm-Up

See Article 832 for detailed warm-up protocols. Key elements:
- General activity to raise temperature
- Dynamic mobility for joint preparation
- Neural activation for muscle preparation
- Sport-specific hitting for rhythm and feel

### The Mental Warm-Up

**Confidence Building:**
- Review past successes and preparation quality
- Positive self-talk: "I am prepared, I am ready, I will compete"
- Visualize successful performance
- Focus on process, not outcome

**Focus Development:**
- Shift attention to the present moment
- Use focus cues (e.g., "Watch the ball," "Move your feet")
- Clear the mind of distractions
- Establish a competitive mindset

**Arousal Regulation:**
- Use breathing techniques to manage energy level
- Music (if desired) to establish mood
- Movement to activate or calm the body
- Visualization to create the desired emotional state

### The Tactical Warm-Up

**Opponent Review (5 minutes):**
- Serve tendencies (placement, type, speed)
- Return patterns (position, aggression)
- Groundstroke patterns (direction, spin, depth)
- Strengths and weaknesses

**Game Plan Confirmation (5 minutes):**
- Primary tactical plan
- Secondary tactical plan (if primary fails)
- Key patterns to execute
- Adjustments for different scenarios

### The Emotional Warm-Up

**Confidence:**
- "I have prepared well for this match"
- "I am ready for this challenge"
- "I trust my training and my team"

**Composure:**
- "I will stay focused on each point"
- "I will manage my emotions effectively"
- "I will compete with intensity and control"

**Intention:**
- "I will give maximum effort on every point"
- "I will compete with courage and determination"
- "I will enjoy the challenge"

## Adapting the Routine

### For Grand Slams

- Extended warm-up time (90-120 minutes before match)
- More detailed tactical preparation
- Greater emphasis on mental preparation
- Additional hydration and nutrition management

### For Early-Round Matches

- Standard routine (60-90 minutes before match)
- Focus on establishing rhythm and confidence
- Less emphasis on opponent analysis (less information available)

### For Finals

- Enhanced mental preparation (greater pressure)
- More detailed tactical planning
- Additional confidence-building activities
- Stronger emphasis on arousal regulation

### For Back-to-Back Matches

- Compressed routine (less time available)
- Focus on efficiency (prioritize key elements)
- Enhanced recovery between matches
- Simplified tactical preparation

## Practical Application: "The Pre-Match Routine Card"

Create a pocket-sized card with the pre-match routine:

**Front Side (Timeline):**
- 3-4 hours before: Breakfast
- 2-3 hours before: Travel to venue
- 90 minutes before: Arrive at venue
- 60 minutes before: General warm-up begins
- 40 minutes before: On-court warm-up begins
- 20 minutes before: Final preparation
- 5 minutes before: Walk to court

**Back Side (Key Points):**
- Confidence cues: "I am prepared and ready"
- Focus cues: "Watch the ball, move your feet"
- Breathing: "Deep breath before each serve"
- Tactical reminder: "Serve to the backhand, +1 to the open court"
- Emotional reminder: "Stay composed, compete with intensity"

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Failure | Result | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Slow starts in matches | Inconsistent pre-match routine, poor preparation | Lost early games, momentum deficit | Develop and rehearse a consistent pre-match routine; practice timing |
| Nerves affecting performance | Insufficient mental preparation, anxiety | Reduced performance, choking | Mental preparation techniques; breathing; visualization; confidence building |
| Lack of focus at match start | Insufficient mental warm-up | Unforced errors, poor decision-making | Mental warm-up routine; focus cue development; present-moment training |
| Inconsistent performance across matches | Inconsistent preparation, variable routines | Unpredictable results | Standardize the pre-match routine; practice consistently; track preparation quality |
