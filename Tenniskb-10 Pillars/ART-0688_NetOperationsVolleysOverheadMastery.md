# ART-0688: The Volley Against the Return with Deep Placement — Handling Deep Returns

## Executive Summary

Deep returns that land near the baseline force the net player to hit a deeper, more defensive volley. When you are the net player facing deep returns, you must position yourself to handle the deeper contact point and use a more defensive volley technique. Deep returns are a standard tactic to push the net player back and neutralize their attacking position.

## The Deep Return

The deep return: (1) lands near the baseline, (2) forces the net player to volley from a deeper position, (3) is more defensive than a short return. At the net, the deep return is less dangerous than a short return because it gives you more time but forces a deeper contact point.

## Technique for Handling Deep Returns

Against deep returns: (1) position slightly deeper at the net (6-9 feet), (2) let the ball drop to a comfortable height, (3) use the punch volley with a slightly longer backswing (you have more time), (4) aim deep to the baseline, (5) use a firm wrist. The key is to adjust your positioning and use the extra time to hit a quality volley.

## Common Errors

Positioning too close to the net (forced to volley from an awkward height). Not adjusting technique for the deep ball (the standard volley may not work). Not using the extra time effectively (rushing when you have time). Not aiming deep (short volleys give the opponent attacking opportunities).

## Summary

Deep returns demand deeper positioning and adjusted technique. Position deeper, let the ball drop, and use the extra time to hit a quality deep volley.

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Failure | Result | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Too close to net for deep returns | Standard positioning | Awkward contact height | Move deeper, 6-9 feet |
| Does not adjust technique for deep returns | Same technique for all returns | Inconsistent volleys | Use slightly longer backswing for deep returns |
| Rushes deep returns | Not using extra time | Errors | Take your time, the deep ball gives you time |
| Short volleys on deep returns | Not aiming deep | Attacking ball for opponent | Aim deep to baseline |

## Practical Application: "Deeper Position, Longer Swing, Deep Volley"

Against deep returns: (1) position deeper, (2) use a slightly longer backswing, (3) aim deep. The deep ball gives you time — use it.
