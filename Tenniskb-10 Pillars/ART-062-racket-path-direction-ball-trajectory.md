# ART:062: Racket Path Direction and Ball Trajectory

## Executive Summary
The direction of the racket path through the contact zone is the primary determinant of ball trajectory and depth. A more upward path produces higher, deeper shots; a more downward path produces lower, shorter shots; a more forward path produces flatter, faster shots. Understanding how to manipulate racket path direction enables players to control depth and trajectory for tactical advantage.

## Racket Path Components

### Vertical Component
- **Upward path**: Higher trajectory, deeper ball
- **Level path**: Medium trajectory, medium depth
- **Downward path**: Lower trajectory, shorter ball

### Forward Component
- **More forward**: Faster ball, flatter trajectory
- **Less forward**: Slower ball, higher trajectory

## Practical Application: "The Path Control"
To control depth, adjust the vertical component of your racket path. To hit deeper, brush up more; to hit shorter, brush down more. To control direction, adjust the horizontal component of your face angle. Mastering racket path control allows you to vary your shots tactically, keeping the opponent guessing.
