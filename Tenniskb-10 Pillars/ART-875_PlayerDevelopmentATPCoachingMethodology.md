# ART-875: Static Stretching Guidelines for Tennis

## Executive Summary

Static stretching—holding a stretched position for 20-60 seconds—is a valuable tool for improving flexibility, promoting recovery, and reducing muscle tension in tennis players. However, research clearly shows that static stretching before activity can reduce power output and should be reserved for cool-down periods and dedicated flexibility sessions. A well-designed static stretching program targets the muscle groups most affected by tennis (shoulders, forearms, hips, hamstrings, and lower back) and is performed after training, after matches, or during dedicated flexibility sessions to improve long-term range of motion and recovery.

## When to Static Stretch

### Appropriate Times for Static Stretching

1. **Post-Training/Match (Cool-Down):** After activity, when muscles are warm
2. **Dedicated Flexibility Sessions:** Stand-alone sessions (2-3x/week)
3. **Before Bed:** Gentle stretching to promote relaxation and recovery
4. **Rest Days:** Flexibility-focused sessions to improve range of motion

### When NOT to Static Stretch

1. **Before Training/Matches:** Reduces power output by 2-5%
2. **Before Explosive Activities:** Decreases serve speed and jump height
3. **With Acute Injuries:** May aggravate the injury
4. **When Muscles Are Cold:** Risk of muscle strain

## Static Stretching Protocol

### Guidelines

- **Duration:** Hold each stretch for 20-60 seconds
- **Intensity:** Stretch to the point of mild discomfort, not pain
- **Repetitions:** 2-3 repetitions per stretch
- **Breathing:** Breathe deeply and relax into the stretch
- **Frequency:** Daily for flexibility gains; post-activity for recovery

### Target Stretches for Tennis

**Shoulder Stretches:**

| Stretch | Duration | Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cross-body stretch | 30 sec each arm | Posterior shoulder |
| Sleeper stretch | 30 sec each arm | Internal rotator flexibility |
| Triceps stretch | 30 sec each arm | Triceps and shoulder |
| Doorway chest stretch | 30 sec | Pectorals |
| Lat stretch (kneeling) | 30 sec each side | Latissimus dorsi |

**Arm and Forearm Stretches:**

| Stretch | Duration | Target |
| :--- | :--- :--- | :--- |
| Wrist flexor stretch | 30 sec each arm | Forearm flexors |
| Wrist extensor stretch | 30 sec each arm | Forearm extensors |
| Finger flexor stretch | 30 sec each hand | Finger flexors |

**Hip and Leg Stretches:**

| Stretch | Duration | Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Standing quad stretch | 30 sec each leg | Quadriceps |
| Standing hamstring stretch | 30 sec each leg | Hamstrings |
| Hip flexor stretch (kneeling) | 30 sec each side | Hip flexors |
| Pigeon pose | 30 sec each side | Glutes and hip rotators |
| IT band stretch | 30 sec each side | Iliotibial band |
| Calf stretch (straight knee) | 30 sec each leg | Gastrocnemius |
| Calf stretch (bent knee) | 30 sec each leg | Soleus |
| Groin stretch (butterfly) | 30 sec | Adductors |

**Spine Stretches:**

| Stretch | Duration | Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Child's pose | 30 sec | Lower back |
| Knee-to-chest stretch | 30 sec | Lower back |
| Neck stretches (all directions) | 20 sec each direction | Neck |
| Thoracic rotation stretch | 30 sec each side | Thoracic spine |

## Post-Match Static Stretching Routine (15 minutes)

1. Cross-body shoulder stretch: 30 sec each arm
2. Triceps stretch: 30 sec each arm
3. Wrist flexor stretch: 30 sec each arm
4. Wrist extensor stretch: 30 sec each arm
5. Standing quad stretch: 30 sec each leg
6. Standing hamstring stretch: 30 sec each leg
7. Hip flexor stretch: 30 sec each side
8. Pigeon pose: 30 sec each side
9. Calf stretch: 30 sec each leg
10. Child's pose: 30 sec
11. Neck stretches: 20 sec each direction

## Dedicated Flexibility Session (30-45 minutes, 2-3x/week)

1. General warm-up: 5 minutes light activity
2. Full static stretching routine: 20-30 minutes (all stretches, 2-3 reps each)
3. Foam rolling: 10 minutes

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Failure | Result | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Muscle tightness limiting movement | Insufficient stretching, poor flexibility | Reduced range of motion, compensatory movement | Daily static stretching routine; dedicated flexibility sessions |
| Reduced power after pre-match stretching | Static stretching before activity | Decreased serve speed | Move static stretching to cool-down; use dynamic warm-up before activity |
| Chronic muscle tension | Insufficient cool-down, poor recovery | Stiffness, pain, reduced flexibility | Post-match static stretching; foam rolling; massage |
| Flexibility plateau | Insufficient stretching frequency or duration | No improvement in range of motion | Increase stretching frequency; hold longer; add PNF stretching |
