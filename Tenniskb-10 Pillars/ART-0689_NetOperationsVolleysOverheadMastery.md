# ART-0689: The Volley Against the Return with Short Placement — Handling Short Returns

## Executive Summary

Short returns that land between the service line and net are a gift to the net player — they allow an aggressive drive volley or punch volley that can end the point. When you are the net player facing a short return, you must move forward quickly, take the ball early, and hit an aggressive volley to finish the point. Short returns are the net player's best opportunity to attack.

## The Short Return

The short return: (1) lands between the service line and net, (2) is hit with moderate pace, (3) gives the net player an attacking opportunity. At the net, the short return is a mistake by the returner — you should punish it with an aggressive volley.

## Technique for Handling Short Returns

Against short returns: (1) move forward quickly to take the ball early, (2) use the drive volley or punch volley (you have time for a full swing), (3) aim for the open court or the opponent's feet, (4) step into the ball for power, (5) use a firm wrist. The key is to be aggressive — the short return is your opportunity to finish the point.

## Common Errors

Not moving forward (waiting for the ball instead of attacking). Using a block volley instead of a punch or drive (missing the opportunity). Not aiming for the open court (hitting to the opponent's strength). Not stepping into the ball (weak volley).

## Summary

Short returns demand aggressive net play. Move forward, take the ball early, and hit a drive volley or punch volley to finish the point.

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Failure | Result | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Does not move forward on short returns | Waiting for the ball | Missed opportunity | Move forward quickly, take the ball early |
| Uses block volley on short returns | Not attacking | Weak volley | Use drive volley or punch volley |
| Does not aim for open court | Hitting to opponent's strength | Strong return | Aim for open court or opponent's feet |
| Does not step into the ball | Static position | Weak volley | Step into the ball, generate power |

## Practical Application: "Move Forward, Take Early, Attack"

Against short returns: (1) move forward quickly, (2) take the ball early, (3) attack with a drive volley or punch volley. The short return is a gift — punish it.
