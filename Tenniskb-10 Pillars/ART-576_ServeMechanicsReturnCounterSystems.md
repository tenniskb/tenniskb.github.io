# ART-576: Return Injury Prevention — Ankle, Knee, and Back Considerations

## Executive Summary

Returning serve places unique stresses on the body, particularly the ankles, knees, and lower back. This article provides a comprehensive injury prevention protocol for returners.

## Common Return-Related Injuries

### Ankle Injuries
- Ankle sprains from rolling the ankle during lateral movement
- Achilles tendinopathy from explosive movement

### Knee Injuries
- Patellar tendinopathy from bending and exploding
- Meniscus strain from twisting during lateral movement

### Back Injuries
- Lower back strain from bending and rotating
- Muscle spasms from sudden, involuntary contractions

## Pre-Return Warm-Up Protocol

### Phase 1: General Activation (5 minutes)
- Light jogging, ankle circles, knee bends, torso twists

### Phase 2: Joint-Specific Warm-Up (5 minutes)
- Ankle mobility, knee mobility, lower back mobility exercises

### Phase 3: Return-Specific Progression (10 minutes)
- Gentle returns at 60-70%, moderate at 75-85%, match-intensity at 85-95%

## Strength Training for Injury Prevention

### Ankle Strengthening
- Calf raises, ankle circles with resistance band, single-leg balance holds

### Knee Strengthening
- Squats, single-leg squats, lunges

### Core Strengthening
- Plank holds, side plank holds, dead bugs

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Failure | Result | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Ankle pain after returning | Insufficient warm-up or overuse | Ankle sprain risk | Warm-up protocol; ankle strengthening |
| Knee pain on returns | Poor mechanics or overuse | Patellar tendinopathy | Mechanics correction; knee strengthening |
| Lower back pain on returns | Poor core stability | Lower back strain | Core strengthening; mechanics modification |

## Practical Application: "The 10-Minute Rule"

Before every practice or match, commit to 10 minutes of return-specific warm-up: 3 minutes of general warm-up, 4 minutes of joint-specific exercises, and 3 minutes of progressive returning.
