# ART-661: The Volley Against the Counterpuncher — Beating Reactive Players

## Executive Summary

The counterpuncher is a reactive player who uses the opponent's pace against them, redirecting hard shots with precision and consistency. Counterpunchers are particularly dangerous against net players because they excel at passing shots — they use the net player's forward momentum to redirect the ball past them. Beating counterpunchers at the net requires varied pace, unpredictable shot selection, and the ability to take pace off the ball when needed.

## The Counterpuncher's Game

A counterpuncher: (1) redirects pace rather than generating their own, (2) hits precise passing shots using the opponent's forward momentum, (3) moves well and retrieves everything, (4) changes direction frequently to keep opponents off-balance, (5) rarely makes unforced errors. The counterpuncher's goal is to make the aggressor miss by redirecting their pace with precision.

## The Challenge for Net Players

Counterpunchers challenge net players because: (1) they use the net player's forward momentum to redirect passing shots, (2) they hit precise, well-placed passing shots that are difficult to reach, (3) they change direction frequently, making anticipation difficult, (4) they rarely make errors, requiring the net player to hit multiple volleys. The net player must adjust their tactics to handle these reactive, precise opponents.

## Volley Adjustments Against Counterpunchers

Against counterpunchers: (1) vary your pace — do not always hit hard volleys (the counterpuncher thrives on pace), (2) use drop volleys to take pace off the ball (counterpunchers struggle with soft balls), (3) use lob volleys to change the rhythm, (4) hit to unexpected directions (counterpunchers anticipate based on patterns), (5) be prepared for multiple volleys — counterpunchers retrieve everything.

## Tactical Application Against Counterpunchers

Against counterpunchers: (1) approach selectively — only when you have a high-percentage opportunity, (2) vary your shot selection (mix hard volleys, soft volleys, drop volleys, and lob volleys), (3) do not always hit to the open court — counterpunchers anticipate this, (4) use the body volley to jam the counterpuncher, (5) be patient — you may need to hit 3-4 volleys to win the point. The key is unpredictability — do not let the counterpuncher settle into a rhythm.

## Common Errors Against Counterpunchers

Always hitting hard volleys (gives the counterpuncher pace to redirect). Always hitting to the open court (counterpuncher anticipates). Not varying shot selection (becomes predictable). Not using drop volleys enough (missed opportunities). Not being patient enough to hit multiple volleys.

## Summary

Beating counterpunchers at the net requires varied pace, unpredictable shot selection, and patience. Use drop volleys to take pace off the ball, lob volleys to change the rhythm, and body volleys to jam the counterpuncher. The key is unpredictability — do not let the counterpuncher settle into a comfortable pattern.

## Practical Application: "Vary, Disguise, Be Patient"

Against counterpunchers: (1) vary your pace (hard, soft, drop), (2) disguise your direction (same preparation for all shots), (3) be patient (multiple volleys may be needed). The counterpuncher wants you to be predictable — do not give them the satisfaction.
