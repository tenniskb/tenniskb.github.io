# ART-0699: The Volley Against the Return with Perfect Placement — Handling Ideal Returns

## Executive Summary

Returns with perfect placement — hit to the exact corner with ideal pace and spin — are the most difficult to handle because they are designed to be unreturnable. When you are the net player facing a perfectly placed return, you must use every tool at your disposal: quick lateral movement, the block volley, and the willingness to accept that some returns cannot be reached. Perfect placement returns are the returner's best weapon — respect them, but do not fear them.

## The Perfect Placement Return

The perfect placement return: (1) is hit to the exact corner (deep, angled, with ideal pace), (2) is designed to be unreturnable, (3) represents the returner's highest-quality shot. At the net, the perfect placement return is almost impossible to reach — but you must still try.

## Technique for Handling Perfect Placement Returns

Against perfect placement returns: (1) use the cross-step to move laterally as quickly as possible, (2) use the block volley if you can reach the ball, (3) accept that some returns cannot be reached — do not risk injury trying, (4) if you cannot reach the ball, let it go and reset for the next point, (5) focus on the returns you can reach. The key is effort and acceptance — try your best, but accept the limits.

## Common Errors

Not trying to reach the ball (giving up). Risking injury to reach an unreturnable ball (not worth it). Getting frustrated by perfect placement returns (loss of focus). Not resetting after a perfect placement return (carrying frustration to the next point).

## Summary

Perfect placement returns are the returner's best weapon. Try your best to reach them, but accept that some are unreturnable. Focus on the returns you can reach and reset for the next point.

## Practical Application: "Try Your Best, Accept the Limits"

Against perfect placement returns: (1) try your best to reach the ball, (2) accept that some returns are unreturnable, (3) reset for the next point. Effort and acceptance are the keys.
