# ART-0668: The Volley Against the Topspin Backhand — Handling High-Bouncing Shots

## Executive Summary

The topspin backhand is a high-bouncing passing shot that kicks up at the net player. When you are at the net, the topspin backhand is challenging because the high bounce forces contact above the ideal volley height. Handling the topspin backhand requires positioning to let the ball drop, a more closed racket face, and a downward punch volley.

## The Topspin Backhand

The topspin backhand: (1) bounces high and kicks forward, (2) is hit with heavy topspin that causes the ball to jump, (3) is hit with pace from the baseline. At the net, the topspin backhand forces contact at head height or above, requiring adjustments to standard volley technique.

## Volley Technique Against the Topspin Backhand

Against the topspin backhand: (1) let the ball drop to a comfortable contact height, (2) close the racket face more than usual (the topspin causes the ball to jump off the racket), (3) use a downward punch volley to bring the ball down, (4) aim deep to the baseline. The key is to handle the high bounce and heavy topspin with a closed face and downward angle.

## Common Errors

Not letting the ball drop (contact too high). Not closing the face enough (ball goes long). Using a flat punch (the topspin causes the ball to jump). Not anticipating the high bounce.

## Summary

The topspin backhand demands a closed racket face and downward punch volley. Let the ball drop, close the face, and punch downward to control the high-bouncing shot.

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Failure | Result | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Volley goes long | Face too open, not handling topspin | Ball past baseline | Close face more, punch downward |
| Contact too high | Not letting ball drop | Weak volley | Let ball drop to comfortable height |
| Cannot handle kick | Not anticipating topspin | Erratic contact | Close face, anticipate kick |
| Volley lacks downward angle | Flat punch | Easy return | Use downward punch volley |

## Practical Application: "Let It Drop, Close the Face, Punch Down"

Against the topspin backhand: (1) let the ball drop to a comfortable height, (2) close the racket face, (3) punch downward to control the high-bouncing ball.
