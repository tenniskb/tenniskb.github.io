# ART-876: Hydration Strategies for Tennis Performance

## Executive Summary

Hydration is a critical determinant of tennis performance, with even mild dehydration (2% body weight loss) reducing cognitive function, reaction time, physical capacity, and decision-making. Tennis matches lasting 2-5 hours in varying environmental conditions can result in sweat losses of 1-3 liters per hour, making systematic hydration essential. Effective hydration strategies involve pre-match hydration, during-match fluid replacement, and post-match rehydration, with attention to electrolyte replacement in hot conditions or prolonged matches. Players who maintain hydration throughout competition perform better in later sets and recover faster between matches.

## Fluid Losses in Tennis

### Sweat Rate Factors

| Factor | Effect on Sweat Rate |
| :--- | :--- |
| Temperature | Higher temperature = more sweating |
| Humidity | Higher humidity = reduced evaporative cooling = more sweating |
| Intensity | Higher intensity = more sweating |
| Match Duration | Longer matches = greater total losses |
| Individual Variation | Sweat rates vary from 0.5 to 3.0 L/hour |
| Acclimatization | Acclimatized players sweat more (but earlier and more dilute) |

### Typical Sweat Losses

| Condition | Sweat Rate (L/hour) | 2-Hour Match Loss | 3-Hour Match Loss |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cool (15-20 C) | 0.5-1.0 | 1.0-2.0 L | 1.5-3.0 L |
| Moderate (20-30 C) | 1.0-1.5 | 2.0-3.0 L | 3.0-4.5 L |
| Hot (30+ C) | 1.5-3.0 | 3.0-6.0 L | 4.5-9.0 L |

## Hydration Assessment

### Urine Color Chart

| Color | Hydration Status | Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Pale yellow (1-3) | Well-hydrated | Maintain current intake |
| Yellow (4-5) | Mild dehydration | Increase fluid intake |
| Dark yellow (6-7) | Moderate dehydration | Aggressive rehydration |
| Amber (8+) | Severe dehydration | Immediate rehydration; consider withdrawal |

### Body Weight Monitoring

- Weigh before and after training/matches
- Each 1 kg lost = approximately 1 L of fluid lost
- Replace 150% of weight lost (1 kg lost = 1.5 L fluid)

## Hydration Protocols

### Daily Hydration

- Baseline: 35-40 mL per kg body weight per day
- Example: 70 kg player = 2.5-3.0 L per day
- Additional: 500 mL per hour of training or competition

### Pre-Match Hydration

| Timing | Amount | Type |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3-4 hours before | 500-600 mL | Water or electrolyte drink |
| 1-2 hours before | 250-300 mL | Water |
| 15-30 minutes before | 150-200 mL | Water or sports drink |

### During-Match Hydration

| Opportunity | Amount | Type |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Every changeover (90 sec) | 150-250 mL | Water or sports drink |
| Set break (120 sec) | 250-350 mL | Sports drink with carbohydrates |
| Medical timeout | 500 mL | Electrolyte drink |

### Post-Match Rehydration

- Replace 150% of body weight lost
- Example: Lost 2 kg = drink 3.0 L over next 4-6 hours
- Include sodium to enhance fluid retention
- Include carbohydrates to replenish glycogen

## Electrolyte Replacement

### Why Electrolytes Matter

Sweat contains sodium, potassium, magnesium, and chloride. Replacing only water without electrolytes can lead to hyponatremia (low blood sodium), which is dangerous and impairs performance.

### Electrolyte Replacement Guidelines

| Condition | Sodium Replacement | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Matches < 1 hour | Minimal needed | Normal diet sufficient |
| Matches 1-2 hours | 300-600 mg/L | Sports drink |
| Matches > 2 hours | 600-1000 mg/L | Sports drink + electrolyte tablets |
| Hot conditions (>30 C) | 1000-1500 mg/L | Electro electrolyte drink + salt |

### Sports Drink Composition

A good sports drink for tennis should contain:
- Carbohydrates: 6-8% (60-80 g/L)
- Sodium: 300-600 mg/L
- Potassium: 100-200 mg/L
- Palatable flavor to encourage drinking

## Practical Application: "The Match Day Hydration Plan"

**Morning (3-4 hours before):**
- Drink 500 mL water with breakfast
- Urine check: should be pale yellow

**Pre-Match (1-2 hours before):**
- Drink 250-300 mL water
- Begin warm-up

**During Warm-Up:**
- Drink 150-200 mL water

**During Match (every changeover):**
- Drink 150-250 mL sports drink
- Set break: 300 mL sports drink

**Post-Match:**
- Weigh immediately
- Begin rehydration: 500 mL within first 30 minutes
- Continue drinking 150% of weight lost over next 4-6 hours

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Failure | Result | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cramping during matches | Dehydration, electrolyte imbalance | Time loss, reduced performance | Hydration protocol; electrolyte supplementation |
| Performance decline in later sets | Dehydration, fatigue | Errors, reduced movement | During-match hydration; aggressive fluid replacement |
| Dark urine before matches | Insufficient pre-match hydration | Reduced performance, health risk | Pre-match hydration protocol; daily hydration monitoring |
| Weight loss during matches >2% | Insufficient fluid replacement | Significant performance decline | Increase during-match fluid intake; weigh before/after matches |
| Gastrointestinal discomfort from drinks | Too concentrated, wrong type | Nausea, cramping | Dilute sports drinks; try different brands; practice during training |
