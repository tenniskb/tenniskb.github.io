About This Site

This knowledge base grew out of my need for systematic, in-depth knowledge — not haphazard tennis tips. Most coaching tells you what to do — "get under the ball," "hit through it," "use your legs" — but never explains why or how your nervous system actually learns these patterns.

Everything here is grounded in:

  • Authoritative coaching materials — drawn from textbooks and research.
  • Bilingual by design — not translated, but written natively in both languages.
  • Neural control theory — understanding movement as the brain learns it.
  • Practical application — drills and progressions you can use today.

For educational purposes only. Not a substitute for professional coaching.
