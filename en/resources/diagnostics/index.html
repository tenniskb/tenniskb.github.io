    Diagnostics¶

    Find Your Broken Link in 5 Minutes.

    The Principle¶

    Every error traces to ONE broken variable. Fix the link, the chain works.

    The CORE Diagnostic (12 Variables)¶

    Your Symptom Likely Broken Link Confirmation Test Fix Drill
    Late contact, shanks, mis-hits Q (Quiet Eye) Eyes jump at YOUR contact, not opponent's "Park & Hold" drill
    Head moves, face opens/closes V (Vestibular) Chin leaves shoulder, head turns to target "Chin Tuck & Freeze"
    No power, falling back, weak ROOT (Rooting) Back foot doesn't screw, arch collapses "Screw & Lift"
    Arm-only swing, elbow pain F/P (Fascia/Proprio) Forearm tight, no stretch feel in core "Line Trace" + "Eyes Closed"
    Muscled swing, no whip, tired T/SSC (Tensegrity/SSC) Pause at top of takeback, forearm tight "Bounce-Drop-Push" + "Tent Trace"
    Big takeback, late, rushed 8 (Figure-8) Racket goes way back, pauses at top "Shadow 8s" + "Size Regulation"
    Flat balls, no margin, no spin 45° (Swing Plane) Hand path flat across chest, not diagonal "Back Pocket → Shoulder"
    Pushy, no penetration, no pop BRAKE No front leg stomp, torso keeps rotating "Wall Push Pulse" + "Brake & Freeze"
    Inconsistent under pressure JIN No feel, fight pace, muscled release "Eyes Closed Shadow" + "Absorb & Redirect"

    Quick Self-Test (2 Minutes Per Variable)¶

    Q Test: "Park Rate"¶

    1. Partner feeds 20 balls
    2. Say "PARK" aloud at THEIR contact
    3. Count: did eyes jump to YOUR contact zone?
    4. Target: >85% park rate

    V Test: "Chin Check"¶

    1. Film side view of 10 forehands
    2. Check: chin over front shoulder through contact?
    3. Target: 10/10 chin still

    ROOT Test: "Arch Lift"¶

    1. Stand on one leg, barefoot
    2. Screw foot, lift arch, hold 10s
    3. Feel: spiral line tension from foot to ribs
    4. Target: Both sides equal, strong tension

    F Test: "Line Continuity"¶

    1. Slow shadow forehand
    2. Trace Spiral Line with opposite hand: foot→hip→ribs→shoulder→hand
    3. Target: No slack, continuous tension

    P Test: "Eyes Closed Contact"¶

    1. Partner feeds 10 easy balls
    2. Close eyes at bounce
    3. Call: "FH/BH, Clean/Off, Open/Closed"
    4. Target: 8/10 correct

    T Test: "Tent Trace"¶

    1. Slow shadow, feel "bones float, fascia holds"
    2. Check: any local knots (forearm, trap, calf)?
    3. Target: Zero knots, continuous tent

    SSC Test: "Bounce-Drop-Push"¶

    1. Drop ball, let bounce
    2. Racket MUST move forward BEFORE ball peaks
    3. Target: 20/20 continuous, no pause

    8 Test: "Shadow 8s"¶

    1. 30 seconds continuous figure-8s
    2. Target: No pause anywhere, smooth crossover

    45° Test: "Side Film"¶

    1. Film 5 forehands from side
    2. Check: hand path = back pocket → opposite shoulder
    3. Target: All 5 on 45° diagonal

    BRAKE Test: "Freeze Frame"¶

    1. Shadow swing → hard brake at exit → freeze 3s
    2. Check: torso stopped? front leg braced?
    3. Target: Instant stop, stable freeze

    PULSE Test: "Wall Pulse"¶

    1. 1" from wall, palm on wall
    2. Push 1 inch, feel ground→hand
    3. Target: Sharp 30ms impulse, not slow push

    JIN Test: "Feel the Stretch"¶

    1. Eyes closed, shadow swing
    2. Rate stretch feel 1-10 at max load
    3. Target: 6-7/10 clear stretch sensation

    Stroke-Specific Diagnostics¶

    Forehand Push¶

    Symptom Variable Drill
    Wide/long Q, BRAKE Park & Hold, Brake & Freeze
    Into net 8, 45° Shadow 8s, Back Pocket → Shoulder
    No power ROOT, BRAKE, PULSE Screw & Lift, Wall Pulse
    Elbow pain F, P Line Trace, Eyes Closed
    Inconsistent JIN Variable Pace Rally

    Backhand Saber¶

    Symptom Variable Drill
    Long V, BRAKE Chin Over Shoulder, Brake Side-On
    Into net 8, ROOT Shadow 8s, Screw & Lift
    No spin F, PULSE Line Trace (Back Line), Saber Pulse
    Shoulder pain F, T Lat Stretch, Tent Trace
    Late 8, Q Small 8, Park Early

    Serve¶

    Symptom Variable Drill
    Long Q, BRAKE Park Toss Peak, Brake Front Hip
    Net 45°, PULSE Glass Plane, Wall Pulse
    Wide V, ROOT Chin Tuck, Screw & Lift
    No power SSC, BRAKE Trophy Stretch, Brake & Freeze
    Shoulder F, T Line Trace, Tent Trace

    Return¶

    Symptom Variable Drill
    Late/jammed Q, 8 Park at Server Contact, Tiny 8
    Floats long BRAKE, F Meet Early, Firm Face
    Into net 45°, ROOT Open Face for Kick, Screw Front Foot
    Weak block PULSE, JIN Pulse Regulation, Absorb & Redirect

    Diagnostic Decision Tree¶

    START: What's the PRIMARY symptom?
│
├── CONTACT ISSUES (shanks, late, mis-hits)
│   └── → Q Test → V Test → ROOT Test
│
├── DIRECTION ISSUES (wide, long, net)
│   └── → BRAKE Test → 45° Test → 8 Test
│
├── POWER ISSUES (weak, no pop, pushy)
│   └── → ROOT Test → BRAKE Test → PULSE Test → SSC Test
│
├── SPIN/CONTROL ISSUES (flat, no margin)
│   └── → 45° Test → F Test → P Test
