Long-Term Periodization & Bio-Agentic Conditioning

Video: Tennis Fitness Lesson with ATP Pro (Top Tennis Training - Pro Tennis Lessons).

> PRO CUE: Structure training into macro, meso, and microcycles; balance explosive anaerobic alactacid capacity with aerobic recovery buffering.

Conditioning Foundations

Tennis is metabolically defined by a 1:3 to 1:5 work-to-rest ratio consisting of 5–10 second explosive anaerobic bursts followed by 20–25 seconds of recovery. Long-term periodization ensures peak power development while preventing central nervous system (CNS) fatigue and overtraining.

Step-by-Step Periodization Setup

1. Preparation Phase (General Aerobic Base): Build VO2 max and eccentric braking strength via high-volume foundational movement.

2. Pre-Competitive Phase (Explosive Power): Transition to high-intensity interval training (HIIT) matching match-play work-rest ratios.

3. Competitive Tapering: Reduce volume by 40% while maintaining explosive movement intensity to maximize CNS sharpness.

Periodization Macro-Cycle Matrix

| Cycle Phase | Duration | Primary Athletic Focus | Volume / Intensity Ratio |

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| General Preparation | $4 - 6\text{ Weeks}$ | Aerobic Base & Eccentric Braking | High Volume / Moderate Intensity |

| Specific Power | $3 - 4\text{ Weeks}$ | Anaerobic Alactacid Explosiveness | Moderate Volume / High Intensity |

| Competitive Taper | $1 - 2\text{ Weeks}$ | CNS Recovery & Sharpness | Low Volume / Maximal Intensity |

> CONDITIONING DEFECT WARNING: Neglecting aerobic recovery training causes slow lactate clearance between points, destroying shot precision in late match sets.
