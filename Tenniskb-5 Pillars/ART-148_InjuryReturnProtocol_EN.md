# ART-148: Injury Return Protocol — The Bridge from Rehab to Performance

## Executive Summary
Returning from injury is not "getting cleared"; it is a structured re-integration of the 5-Pillar system. Most re-injuries occur because the athlete returns to *competition load* with *rehab capacity*. This article provides the phased Return-to-Play (RTP) protocol.

## The RTP Continuum (5 Phases)

### Phase 1: Clinical Healing (Medical Clearance)
*   **Criteria:** Pain-free ROM, normal strength (manual muscle test), imaging clearance.
*   **Pillar Focus:** I (Tissue tolerance), V (Sleep/Nutrition for repair).
*   **Tennis:** None. Shadow swings only (no ball contact).

### Phase 2: Physical Preparation (Gym/Field)
*   **Criteria:** Symmetry > 90% on force plates/hop tests. Strength benchmarks met (e.g., 1.5x BW squat).
*   **Pillar Focus:** I (Kinetic chain loading), V (Work capacity).
*   **Tennis:** Medicine ball throws (non-dominant side first). Footwork drills (no sliding).

### Phase 3: Tennis-Specific Load Introduction (On-Court)
*   **Criteria:** Pain-free high-intensity gym session. Psychological readiness (ACL-RSI / similar > 80).
*   **Pillar Focus:** III (Controlled contact), II (Reaction), IV (Positioning).
*   **Protocol:**
    *   Week 1: Mini-tennis (service line) — 20 min, 3x/wk. Feed only.
    *   Week 2: Baseline rally — 30 min, 3x/wk. Cooperative targets.
    *   Week 3: Live ball drills — 45 min. Constrained patterns (CC only).
    *   Week 4: Serve progression — Flat → Slice → Kick. Volume capped.

### Phase 4: Simulated Competition (Practice Matches)
*   **Criteria:** 3 consecutive full training weeks pain-free.
*   **Pillar Focus:** All 5 integrated. Pressure exposure.
*   **Protocol:** Practice sets with scoreboard. Coach/Physio on court. "Stop rules" defined (pain > 3/10, compensation visible).

### Phase 5: Full Return to Competition
*   **Criteria:** 2 pain-free practice matches. Full week of tournament schedule simulated.
*   **Pillar Focus:** V (Tournament load management), II (Match pressure).
*   **Guardrails:**
    *   Match cap: Max 2 matches/day, 3 days consecutive.
    *   Monitoring: Daily wellness questionnaire + RPE.
    *   Maintenance: Continued rehab exercises 3x/wk *indefinitely*.

## The "Load vs. Capacity" Ledger
Track **Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio (ACWR)** daily.
*   **Green Zone:** 0.8 – 1.3.
*   **Red Zone:** > 1.5 (High re-injury risk).
*   **Action:** If ACWR spikes, reduce on-court load *immediately*; do not wait for pain.

## Psychological Return Barriers
*   **Fear of Re-injury (Kinesiophobia):** Graded exposure therapy. Start with "safe" shots, progress to "risky" shots.
*   **Protective Motor Patterns:** Video analysis to detect guarding (e.g., not loading injured leg on serve).
*   **Identity Crisis:** "Am I still a player?" → Reframe: "I am an athlete managing a condition."

## Practical Application: "The Clearance is the Start Line"
Medical clearance means "safe to start loading," not "ready to play." Respect the bridge. The 5th pillar of rehab is patience.
