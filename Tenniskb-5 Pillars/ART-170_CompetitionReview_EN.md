# ART-170: The 5-Pillar Competition Review Template

## Executive Summary
Every match is a data goldmine — but only if you review it systematically. This article provides a comprehensive post-match review template that covers all 5 Pillars, ensuring you extract actionable insights from every competition.

## The Post-Match Review Philosophy

### 1. The 24-Hour Rule
Never review emotionally immediately after a match. Wait 24 hours for the emotional charge to dissipate, then review objectively.

### 2. The Multiplier Effect
Reviewing the wrong pillar wastes time. The correct review identifies the ONE pillar that determined the outcome and fixes it.

## The Summary Action Plan
Based on the review, identify:
1.  **Top priority pillar:** The one that most determined the outcome
2.  **Specific fix:** What exactly needs to change
3.  **Training integration:** How next week's training addresses it

## Practical Application: "The Autopsy"
A match autopsy isn't about blame — it's about learning. Review every match like a scientist, and every match makes you smarter.
