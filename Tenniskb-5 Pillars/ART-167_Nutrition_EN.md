# ART-167: Nutrition & Supplementation Deep Dive — Fueling the 5 Pillars

## Executive Summary
Nutrition is not "eating healthy"; it is the biochemical substrate for the 5 Pillars. This article provides the evidence-based nutrition framework for tennis: periodized fueling, hydration thermodynamics, supplement hierarchy, and the practical protocols for training, match play, and recovery.

## The Nutrition Philosophy: "Fuel for the Work Required"

### Periodized Nutrition (Matching Intake to Demand)
| Phase | Carbohydrate (g/kg/day) | Protein (g/kg/day) | Fat (g/kg/day) | Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Base / High Volume** | 6–8 | 1.6–2.0 | 1.0–1.5 | Glycogen Maximization, Gut Training. |
| **Competition / High Intensity** | 5–7 | 2.0–2.5 | 1.0 | Glycogen Availability, Muscle Protection. |
| **Taper / Low Volume** | 3–5 | 2.5–3.0 | 1.0–1.5 | Body Comp Management, Immune Support. |
| **Injury / Rehab** | 3–5 | **2.5–3.0** | 1.5 (Omega-3 High) | Tissue Repair, Atrophy Prevention. |

*Assumes 70kg athlete → Adjust absolute values. Periodize daily: High CHO on Hard Days, Moderate CHO on Easy Days.*

## The Match Day Fueling Protocol

### Pre-Match (3-4 Hours Before)
*   **Goal:** Top off glycogen, minimize GI distress, hydrate.
*   **Meal:** 2-3 g/kg CHO (Low Fiber, Low Fat, Moderate Protein).
    *   *Example:* White rice/pasta + chicken/egg + cooked veg + electrolyte drink.
*   **Hydration:** 5-7 ml/kg water + sodium (300-500mg).
*   **Caffeine:** 3-6 mg/kg (60 min pre) — *Tested in practice only.*

### During Match (Per Changeover / Set)
*   **Goal:** Maintain blood glucose, delay fatigue, replace sodium.
*   **Carbohydrate:** 30-60 g/hr (Glucose:Fructose 2:1 or 1:0.8 for >60g/hr). Gels, Chews, Drink Mix.
*   **Sodium:** 500-700 mg/hr (Higher in heat/humidity). Salt tabs + Drink.
*   **Fluid:** 150-250 ml/changeover (Ad libitum but structured).
*   **Temperature:** Cool fluids (4-10°C) enhance palatability & gastric emptying.

### Post-Match (0-30 min → 2-4 hr)
*   **0-30 min (Golden Window):** 1.0-1.2 g/kg CHO + 0.3-0.4 g/kg Protein (3:1 or 4:1 ratio). Liquid preferred (Shake/Chocolate Milk).
*   **2-4 hr:** Solid Meal. 1.5 g/kg CHO + 0.4 g/kg Protein + Color (Antioxidants).
*   **Rehydration:** 150% Fluid Loss (Weight Pre - Post). Sodium 50-80 mmol/L.

## Hydration Thermodynamics

### The Sweat Rate Calculation
1.  Weigh Pre (Nude, Towel Dry).
2.  Weigh Post (Nude, Towel Dry).
3.  **Fluid Loss (L) = Pre - Post + Fluid Consumed - Urine.**
4.  **Sweat Rate (L/hr) = Fluid Loss / Duration.**
5.  **Target Replacement:** 80-100% of Sweat Rate *during* play.

### Sodium: The Critical Electrolyte
*   **Sweat Sodium Concentration:** Highly Individual (200-2000 mg/L). *Test via patch or "Salty Sweater" signs (white stains, cramp history).*
*   **Protocol:** 
    *   Low Sweat [Na+] (<500 mg/L): 300-500 mg/hr.
    *   Medium (500-1000 mg/L): 500-800 mg/hr.
    *   High (>1000 mg/L): 800-1200 mg/hr + Pre-load (1500 mg night before).

## The Supplement Hierarchy (Evidence-Based)

### Category A: Strong Evidence (Performance/Health) — **USE**
1.  **Caffeine (3-6 mg/kg):** Ergogenic. CNS alertness, RPE reduction. *Timing Critical.*
2.  **Creatine Monohydrate (5g/day):** Power, Repeat Sprint Ability, Cognitive. *Load not needed. Chronic.*
3.  **Beta-Alanine (3.2-6.4g/day, split):** Buffering (Carnosine). High-Intensity > 60s. *Paresthesia management.*
4.  **Sodium Bicarbonate (0.2-0.3 g/kg):** Acute Buffering. Pre-Match (60-90 min). *GI Risk High. Test.*
5.  **Vitamin D3 (1000-4000 IU/day):** If Deficient (<40 ng/ml). Muscle, Immune, Bone.
6.  **Omega-3 (EPA/DHA 2-3g/day):** Inflammation, Recovery, Neural.
7.  **Protein Powder (Whey/Casein/Blend):** Convenience to hit Protein Target. *Not magic.*
8.  **Electrolyte Drink Mix (CHO + Na+):** Match Fueling Vehicle.

### Category B: Conditional / Emerging Evidence — **CONSIDER**
1.  **Citruline Malate (6-8g):** Blood Flow, Fatigue Resistance. Pre-Match.
2.  **Tart Cherry Juice (30ml Conc.):** Sleep, Inflammation, DOMS. Post-Match/Evening.
3.  **Probiotics (Multi-Strain):** Gut Health, Immunity, GI Distress Prevention. Travel.
4.  **Iron / B12 / Folate:** *Only if Deficient (Blood Work).* Female athletes high risk.
5.  **Collagen (15g + 50mg Vit C):** Tendon/Ligament Health. Pre-Training (30-60 min).

### Category C: Weak / No Evidence / Risk — **AVOID / CAUTION**
*   BCAA (Redundant if Protein Adequate).
*   Glutamine (Gut Health only, not Muscle).
*   Test Boosters / "Proprietary Blends" (Contamination Risk).
*   Excessive Antioxidants (Vit C/E High Dose) — *Blunts Training Adaptation.*

## The "Gut Training" Protocol
*   **Problem:** GI Distress ruins matches.
*   **Solution:** Train the gut like a muscle.
*   **Protocol:** 8-12 Weeks Out. Gradually increase CHO intake during long practices (up to 90g/hr). Practice exact match day nutrition *every* Saturday.

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Nutrition Failure | Symptom | Protocol Adjustment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| "Bonking" (Hypoglycemia) Set 3 | Low CHO Availability | Increase Daily CHO (6-8g/kg). Increase Intra-Match CHO (60-90g/hr). |
| Cramping (Heat) | High Sweat [Na+] / Low Intake | Sweat Test. Sodium Pre-Load. 800-1200mg/hr Intra. |
| GI Distress (Runner's Trots) | High Fiber/Fat Pre-Match / Untrained Gut | Low Residue Diet 24h Pre. Gut Training Protocol. |
| Poor Recovery / DOMS | Low Protein / Low CHO Post / Low Sleep | 3:1 Recovery Shake 0-30min. Tart Cherry. Sleep Hygiene. |
| Frequent Illness | Low Energy Availability (LEA) / Low Vit D / Gut | Screen EA (EA > 45 kcal/kg FFM). Vit D. Probiotics. |

## Practical Application: "The Engine Doesn't Run on Air"
You wouldn't put 87 Octane in an F1 car. You wouldn't run a marathon on water only. The 5-Pillar machine requires specific biochemical substrates at specific times. Nutrition is not lifestyle; it is **equipment specification**. Spec it. Test it. Execute it.
