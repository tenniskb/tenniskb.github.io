# ART-185: The Science of Hydration — More Than Just Water

## Executive Summary
Hydration is not just about drinking water — it's about maintaining the precise fluid and electrolyte balance that every pillar depends on. This article provides a comprehensive hydration protocol for tennis athletes.

## The Hydration Challenge

### 1. Why Tennis Hydration Is Unique
*   **Duration:** Matches can last 3+ hours with continuous sweating
*   **Intensity:** Intermittent high-intensity bursts increase sweat rates
*   **Environment:** Outdoor play in heat amplifies fluid loss
*   **Consequences:** 2% dehydration = measurable cognitive and physical decline

### 2. The 5-Pillar Hydration Protocol

**Pillar I (Biomechanics):**
*   Dehydrated muscles lose elasticity → increased injury risk
*   Joint lubrication decreases → joint stress increases
*   Protocol: Pre-hydrate 5-7 ml/kg 4 hours before match

**Pillar II (Neuro-Cognition):**
*   2% dehydration = cognitive impairment equivalent to 0.08% blood alcohol
*   Decision speed and accuracy decline with dehydration
*   Protocol: Sip 150-200ml every 15-20 min during play

**Pillar III (Technique):**
*   Grip strength decreases with dehydration → racket control suffers
*   Fine motor control declines → stroke quality degrades
*   Protocol: Electrolyte drinks (not just water) to maintain grip

**Pillar V (Conditioning):**
*   Cardiovascular strain increases with dehydration → heart rate rises
*   Thermoregulation impaired → overheating risk
*   Protocol: 150% fluid replacement within 2 hours post-exercise

### 3. Electrolyte Balance
*   **Sodium:** Primary electrolyte lost in sweat (500-700mg/L)
*   **Potassium:** Muscle function and cramp prevention
*   **Magnesium:** Energy production and muscle relaxation

## Practical Application: "The Hydration Habit"
Hydration is not a match-day decision — it's a daily habit. Start hydrated, stay hydrated, and recover hydrated. The best players treat water like fuel.
