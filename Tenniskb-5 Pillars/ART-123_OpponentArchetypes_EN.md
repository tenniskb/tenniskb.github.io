# ART-123: Opponent Archetypes — Diagnosing and Dismantling

## Executive Summary
Every opponent is a system with a signature. This article defines the six dominant playing style archetypes, their structural strengths and exploitable fault lines, and provides counter-pattern playbooks for each — turning tactical preparation into a systematic process rather than guesswork.

## The Six Archetypes

### 1. The Power Baseliner
*   **Signature:** Heavy first-strike forehand; short aggressive swings; dictates from the baseline.
*   **Fault Lines:** Struggles with junk (low slices, slow pace); movement to wide balls; low volleys when drawn in.
*   **Counter-Playbook:** Vary spin and height (moonballs to backhand); attack with depth not pace; use their pace with block redirects; pull them forward then lob.

### 2. The Counterpuncher
*   **Signature:** Elite speed and consistency; zero unforced errors; feeds on your pace.
*   **Fault Lines:** No finishing weapon; passive second serve; short angled balls force creation.
*   **Counter-Playbook:** Patient point construction with high margins; attack only on truly short balls; serve-and-first-strike to avoid rallies; accept longer matches — test their conditioning.

### 3. The Serve-and-Volleyer
*   **Signature:** First-serve rush; net pressure every point.
*   **Fault Lines:** Weak passing-shot cover habits; second serve attackable; baseline rally inferiority.
*   **Counter-Playbook:** Return low at the feet; lob over the net player; pass down-the-line (they shade cross); stretch rallies on their service games.

### 4. The All-Court Artist
*   **Signature:** Touch, variety, drop shots, angle play; improvisational.
*   **Fault Lines:** Inconsistency under sustained power; hates being rushed; physical beating over 3 sets.
*   **Counter-Playbook:** Big, heavy, central balls deny angles; deep returns to push back their court position; attack second serves; embrace physical games.

### 5. The Grind-Returner
*   **Signature:** Elite return game; stands deep; turns defense into offense.
*   **Fault Lines:** Vulnerable to wide serves + first volleys; drop shots pull them from deep comfort.
*   **Counter-Playbook:** Wide slider serves; serve-and-volley surprises; drop-shot-and-lob combos; attack their forecourt.

### 6. The Big Server
*   **Signature:** Free points on serve; tiebreak specialist.
*   **Fault Lines:** Return game weakness; movement; rally tolerance.
*   **Counter-Playbook:** Block returns deep to start neutral rallies; extend every return game; mental discipline in tiebreaks — high-percentage patterns only.

## The Pre-Match Scouting Protocol
1.  **Video (if available):** Note serve pattern distribution, rally tolerance, error triggers.
2.  **Warm-up read:** Grip tells (extreme grips = spin archetype); movement quality; backhand confidence.
3.  **First 2 games:** Confirm archetype; identify their Plan B when pressured.

## Practical Application: The "System Breaker"
Every archetype is strong where they're practiced and weak where they're not. Your job: force points played in the zones they've never mapped. Structure beats improvisation.
