# ART-203: Master Plan — Academy System Plan (The "Factory Blueprint")

## Executive Summary
The Academy is the factory that produces the 5-Pillar athlete. The "Academy System Plan" is the architectural blueprint for a high-performance development environment: curriculum vertical alignment, coaching standards, talent ID pipeline, parent management, facility/resource allocation, and quality assurance systems. It transforms ad-hoc coaching into a reproducible system.

## The Academy System Architecture: Six Sub-Systems

### Sub-System 1: Curriculum & Vertical Alignment (The "Product Spec")
*   **5-Pillar Vertical Curriculum (U8 → Pro):**
    *   *Pillar I (Biomechanics):* Movement ABCs → Kinetic Chain Mastery → Individualized Efficiency.
    *   *Pillar II (Neuro-Cognitive):* Perception-Action Coupling → Anticipation/Decision Training → Pressure Inoculation → Flow State Access.
    *   *Pillar III (Technical):* Feel-Based → Framework Installation → Weapon Development → Personal Style Optimization.
    *   *Pillar IV (Tactical):* Problem Solving → Pattern Library → Game Plan Execution → Opponent Manipulation.
    *   *Pillar V (Conditioning):* Physical Literacy → General Prep → Specific Prep → Peaking/Regeneration → Longevity.
*   **Stage-Gate Competencies:** Clear "Graduation Criteria" per Age/Stage (U10, U12, U14, U16, U18, Pro). Technical, Tactical, Physical, Mental, Educational benchmarks.
*   **Individualized Learning Plans (ILP):** Every athlete has a living ILP updated quarterly. Links Academy Curriculum → Athlete Career Plan (ART-191).

### Sub-System 2: Coaching Faculty & Standards (The "Engineers")
*   **Coaching Hierarchy & Roles:**
    *   *Technical Director:* Curriculum Ownership. Coach Development. Quality Assurance.
    *   *Head Coaches (per Stage):* Program Delivery. Athlete Management. Parent Interface.
    *   *Assistant Coaches:* Session Delivery. Data Collection. Skill Acquisition Support.
    *   *Specialist Coaches:* S&C, Mental Skills, Doubles, Wheelchair/Adaptive.
*   **Coaching Standards (Non-Negotiable):**
    *   Session Architecture Compliance (ART-177).
    *   Constraint-Led Pedagogy (ART-161).
    *   Feedback Ratios (Q:I > 1:1, External Focus > 80%).
    *   Safeguarding & Ethics Compliance (ART-179).
*   **Coach Development System (ART-178):**
    *   Mentor/Supervisor/Peer Structure for ALL coaches.
    *   Quarterly Video Audits. Annual Certification Renewal.
    *   Career Pathway: Assistant → Head Coach → Technical Director.

### Sub-System 3: Talent ID & Pipeline Management (The "Supply Chain")
*   **Multi-Dimensional ID Model (ART-164):**
    *   *Biological:* Maturation Status (PHV Tracking), Anthropometrics.
    *   *Psychological:* Grit, Growth Mindset, Competitiveness, Autonomy.
    *   *Technical/Tactical:* Skill Acquisition Rate, Game Sense, Competitive Instinct.
    *   *Environmental:* Family Support, Educational Flexibility, Resource Access.
*   **Pipeline Tiers:**
    *   *Tier 1 (Foundation U8-U10):* Broad Base. Multi-Sport. Fun-First. No Cuts.
    *   *Tier 2 (Development U11-U14):* Selection Begins. Bio-Banding. ILP Initiation.
    *   *Tier 3 (Performance U15-U18):* High-Performance Track. Pro/College Pathway. Full Support Team.
    *   *Tier 4 (Transition 18+):* Pro Transition Program. College Placement. Career Transition Support.
*   **Flow Management:** Promotion/Demotion Criteria. Exit Interviews. Alumni Tracking.

### Sub-System 4: Parent & Stakeholder Integration (The "Shareholders")
*   **Parent Education Curriculum (Mandatory):**
    *   *Level 1 (Entry):* LTAD Principles. Role Definition. Communication Protocols. "Car Ride Rule."
    *   *Level 2 (Annual Refresh):* Nutrition/Sleep Basics. Growth Mindset. Tournament Parenting. Safeguarding.
    *   *Level 3 (Performance Parents):* Periodization Understanding. Data Literacy. Transition Planning.
*   **Governance Structure:**
    *   *Parent Advisory Council:* Elected. Advisory Only. No Technical Vote.
    *   *Annual General Meeting:* Strategy Presentation. Financial Transparency. Policy Votes.
    *   *Complaints/Escalation Pathway:* Coach → Technical Director → Independent Welfare Officer → Board.

### Sub-System 5: Facilities, Resources & Technology (The "Plant & Equipment")
*   **Court Allocation Model:** Priority Matrix (Pro > Performance > Development > Foundation). Surface Distribution (Clay/Hard/Grass/Indoor). Lighting/Heating Standards.
*   **S&C Facility:** Equipment Standards. Space per Athlete. Safety Protocols.
*   **Recovery Center:** Hydrotherapy, Compression, Soft Tissue, Sleep Pods, Mental Skills Room.
*   **Technology Stack:** Video Analysis (Dartfish/Nacsport), Wearable Platform (Catapult/Polar), Ball Machine (Playmate/Slinger), Court Tracking (Hawk-Eye/FOXTECH), Athlete Management System (Smartabase/Kinduct).
*   **Data Governance:** Athlete Data Ownership. Privacy Compliance (GDPR/COPPA). Access Controls.

### Sub-System 6: Quality Assurance & Continuous Improvement (The "ISO")
*   **KPI Dashboard (Academy Level):**
    *   *Retention:* Year-over-Year by Tier.
    *   *Progression:* % Athletes Meeting Stage-Gate Competencies.
    *   *Output:* Scholarships Earned. Pro Contracts. National Team Selections.
    *   *Coaching Quality:* Session Audit Scores. Coach Retention. Coach Development Completion.
    *   *Parent Satisfaction:* Annual NPS. Complaint Resolution Time.
    *   *Safety:* Injury Incidence Rate. Safeguarding Incidents (Target: 0).
*   **Audit Cycle:**
    *   *Monthly:* Technical Director Walkthrough. KPI Review.
    *   *Quarterly:* Full Curriculum Audit. Coach Video Audit. Parent Survey.
    *   *Annual:* External Review (Independent Expert). Strategic Plan Update. Budget Approval.
*   **Innovation Pipeline:** Pilot Programs (New Tech, New Methodology). Research Partnerships. "Skunkworks" Budget (5% of Revenue).

## The "Red Flag" Triggers for Academy
1.  **Retention < 75%** in any tier for 2 consecutive quarters.
2.  **Stage-Gate Attainment < 60%** for any age group.
3.  **Coach Audit Score < 3.5/5.0** for any coach.
4.  **Safeguarding Incident** (Immediate: Independent Investigation).
5.  **Parent NPS < 20** or Formal Complaint > 3/quarter.
6.  **Financial Deficit** > 2 consecutive quarters without approved plan.

## Practical Application: "The Factory Foreman's Clipboard"
The Academy System Plan is the **Factory Foreman's Clipboard**. It ensures every station (court, gym, classroom, office) knows *what* to produce, *how* to produce it, *when* to inspect it, and *who* is accountable. The Athlete Career Plan (ART-191) and Coach Program Plan (ART-192) are the **Work Orders** flowing through this factory. **If the factory blueprint doesn't match the work orders, you get defective products.** Align the system. Audit the output. Improve the process.
