# ART-048: Detailed Energy Leak Diagnostics & Correction Protocol

## Executive Summary
Energy leakage is the hidden enemy of tennis performance. Most players do not lack strength; they lack efficiency. This protocol provides a systematic method for identifying the exact point in the kinetic chain where energy is lost, and for applying targeted corrections to restore full efficiency.

## The Audit Protocol

### Step 1: The Ground Check
*   **Observation:** Does the player have an active split step? Is the knee flexion sufficient?
*   **Test:** Measure the vertical jump height before and after a tennis session. A significant drop indicates poor ground energy harvesting.
*   **Correction:** Plyometric training and split-step timing drills.

### Step 2: The Pelvic Check
*   **Observation:** Does the pelvis snap and then stop (micro-stop), or does it spin continuously?
*   **Test:** Place a marker on the ground at 45 degrees from the start position. The pelvis should not rotate past this marker before impact.
*   **Correction:** Wall-stop drills and rotation control exercises.

### Step 3: The Core Check
*   **Observation:** Does the torso maintain its alignment during impact, or does it collapse or tilt?
*   **Test:** Video analysis from the front view. Look for lateral tilting or forward folding at impact.
*   **Correction:** Anti-rotation core exercises (e.g., Pallof Press) and isometric bracing drills.

### Step 4: The Shoulder Check
*   **Observation:** Does the shoulder move independently of the torso (decoupling), or does it move as a block?
*   **Test:** Measure the angular separation (X-Factor) at the peak of the backswing versus at impact.
*   **Correction:** Scapular mobility and thoracic rotation exercises.

### Step 5: The Distal Check
*   **Observation:** Does the wrist maintain its structure, or does it collapse or flick?
*   **Test:** Slow-motion video analysis of the impact zone. Look for sudden changes in the wrist angle.
*   **Correction:** Wrist stabilization drills and locked-wrist shadow swings.

## The Correction Priority
Corrections must be applied from the ground up.
*   **Do not fix the wrist if the core is collapsing.** The distal problem is usually a symptom of a proximal failure.
*   **The Golden Rule:** Fix the largest leak first. A 10% improvement at the pelvis is worth more than a 20% improvement at the wrist.

## The "Full Chain" Test
To verify that all leaks have been sealed, the athlete performs a maximum-effort stroke while being monitored for:
1.  Vertical Force (Fz) harvest.
2.  Pelvic Snap and Micro-Stop.
3.  Core Bracing at impact.
4.  Shoulder Decoupling.
5.  Wrist Stability.
When all five elements are synchronized, the athlete will experience a sudden increase in power and a reduction in fatigue, confirming that the kinetic chain is operating at maximum efficiency.
