# ART-114: Tactics & Geometry Review — Pillar IV Comprehensive Synthesis

## Executive Summary
This article synthesizes the tactical principles covered in Pillar IV (Articles 071-080). It provides a unified reference for court coverage, pattern recognition, match strategy, and geometric shot selection essential for elite tennis performance.

## The Tactical Framework Synthesis

### 1. Court Geometry & Positioning
*   **Recovery Position:** Bisects the angle of opponent's possible replies (not simply center of court).
*   **T-Zone Control:** The intersection of service boxes; controlling this area dictates rally tempo.
*   **Depth Zones:** 
    *   Zone 1 (Baseline): Neutral/Defensive.
    *   Zone 2 (Mid-Court): Offensive transition.
    *   Zone 3 (Inside Baseline): Finishing zone.

### 2. Pattern Recognition Library
| Pattern | Trigger | High-Percentage Response |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Serve + 1 (Forehand) | Wide serve / T-serve | Inside-out forehand to open court |
| Cross-Court Exchange | Deep, heavy ball | Wait for short ball → redirect down-the-line |
| Approach Shot | Opponent hits short (< service line) | Approach down-the-line → cover cross-court pass |
| Defensive Lob | Pushed wide/deep | High, deep recovery lob → reset to neutral |

### 3. Match Strategy Windows
*   **0-3 Games:** Information gathering (test weaknesses, establish patterns).
*   **4-6 Games:** Pattern execution (exploit identified weaknesses).
*   **7-10 Games:** Pressure management (high-percentage play, scoreboard awareness).

### 4. Shot Selection Geometry
*   **High % Play:** Cross-court over net strap (longest distance, lowest net).
*   **Change Direction Rule:** Only redirect down-the-line when:
    1.  Ball is short/attackable.
    2.  You are balanced.
    3.  Opponent is recovering to wrong side.

## Diagnostic & Training Matrix

| Observation | Root Cause | Corrective Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Caught out of position | Poor recovery geometry | "Shadow recovery" drills — move to bisector immediately |
| Forcing errors on DTL | Violating change-direction rule | "Decision training" — feed random balls, player calls "redirect/hold" |
| Losing close matches | Poor scoreboard strategy | Simulated pressure sets (start at 4-4, 30-30) |

## Practical Application: The "Tactical Compass"
Every shot answers: **Where is opponent? Where is open court? What is my margin?** The compass needle points to the highest percentage play.
