# ART-075: Forehand Strategy & Backhand Integration

## Executive Summary
The forehand is the most fundamental stroke in tennis, yet it is often the most misunderstood. This article examines the strategic principles that allow elite players to control the forehand, integrate it with the backhand, and maximize their chances of winning points. Understanding these principles is essential for developing a complete, integrated athlete.

## The Forehand Strategy Framework

### 1. The 1st Forehand
The 1st forehand is the most critical forehand of the rally. It sets the tone for the entire rally.
*   **The Ideal Strategy:** The athlete hits the forehand to a specific target (e.g., down-the-T, cross-court) and adjusts their forehand based on the opponent's return pattern.
*   **The Common Errors:**
    *   **The "Random" Forehand:** The athlete hits the forehand randomly, causing them to be out of position.
    *   **The "Power" Forehand:** The athlete hits the forehand for power, causing them to be out of position.
