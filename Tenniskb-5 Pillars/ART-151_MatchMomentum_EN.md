## Executive Summary
Momentum is not magic — it's physics. This article breaks down the biomechanics of momentum shifts, showing how the 5-Pillar system creates and destroys flow states. It provides a framework for building a custom, momentum-adapted system.

## The Momentum Integration Framework

### 1. The Momentum Decision Cycle
Elite players use a 5-step decision cycle:
1.  **Pillar Analysis:** Identify which pillar needs optimization (e.g., "My momentum is inconsistent because of my kinetic chain alignment").
2.  **Momentum Gap Analysis:** Calculate what the momentum doesn't provide (e.g., "My momentum is inconsistent because of my kinetic chain alignment").
3.  **Margin Analysis:** Calculate the cost of each option (e.g., "If I adjust my kinetic chain alignment, I'll have to adjust my momentum technique but gain better momentum speed").
4.  **Implementation:** Adjust training to accommodate the new momentum.
5.  **Evaluation:** Measure the impact on performance (e.g., "My momentum speed increased by 5 km/h").

### 2. The Momentum Memory System
The brain stores three types of momentum knowledge:
*   **Procedural:** Automatic responses to common situations (e.g., "When I hit a kick serve, I always move to the backhand side").
*   **Declarative:** Explicit rules (e.g., "I never attack a serve-and-volleyer's second serve").
*   **Episodic:** Context-specific memories (e.g., "In this match, he's been hitting his first serves to the deuce side").
