# ART-163: Eye Tracking — Training the Quiet Eye

## Executive Summary
Quiet Eye (QE) is the final fixated gaze before movement execution — and it's one of the most trainable predictors of elite performance. This article explains how eye tracking technology measures visual attention and provides protocols to extend QE duration under pressure.

## The Science of Quiet Eye

### 1. What Is Quiet Eye?
QE is the last moment the eyes lock onto a target before initiating movement. In tennis:
*   **Return of serve:** QE on the ball toss and contact point
*   **Groundstrokes:** QE on the opponent's contact → ball flight → own contact preparation
*   **Net play:** QE on the ball as it passes the net

Longer QE = better motor program selection = higher accuracy.

### 2. Elite Benchmarks
| Situation | Novice QE | Elite QE |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Return of serve | 150-200 ms | 300-500 ms |
| Forehand contact | 100-150 ms | 250-400 ms |
| Volley at net | 80-120 ms | 200-300 ms |

### 3. The 5-Pillar QE Integration
*   **Pillar II (Neuro-Cognition):** QE is THE core visual attention skill
*   **Pillar III (Technique):** Longer QE = cleaner contact = better stroke quality
*   **Pillar IV (Tactics):** QE on opponent's body = anticipation = tactical advantage
*   **Pillar V (Conditioning):** QE degrades with fatigue — training stamina preserves it

## The QE Training Protocol
1.  **Baseline Measurement:** Use a head-mounted eye tracker during 20-ball rallies
2.  **Cue-Based Drills:** Coach calls "LOOK" at contact — athlete must maintain gaze 200ms minimum
3.  **Pressure QE Drill:** Score points only if QE duration exceeds threshold (measured post-point)
4.  **Dual-Task QE:** Add cognitive load (counting backward) while maintaining QE — builds resilience

## Technology Options
*   **Professional:** Tobii Pro Glasses 3 (100Hz sampling) — lab-grade accuracy
*   **Consumer:** EyeSeeCam or Pupil Labs — affordable for club-level
*   **Field-No-Tech:** Coach observes eyes + verbal feedback on "staring" vs "glancing"

## Practical Application: "The Gaze of Champions"
Champions don't have better eyes — they have better control over where they look and for how long. Train the gaze, and the strokes follow.
