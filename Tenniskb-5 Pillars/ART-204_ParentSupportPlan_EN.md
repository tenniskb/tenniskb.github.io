# ART-204: Master Plan — Parent Support Plan (The "Shareholder Agreement")

## Executive Summary
Parents are the shareholders in the athlete's career enterprise. The "Parent Support Plan" operationalizes the Parent-Athlete-Coach Triangle (ART-163) into a structured, year-long support curriculum: education, communication protocols, boundary enforcement, logistical/financial planning, and emotional regulation. It transforms "tennis parents" into "high-performance parents."

## The Parent Support Plan Architecture: Five Pillars

### Pillar 1: Education Curriculum (The "Knowledge Base")
*   **Phase 1: Onboarding (Entry + 30 Days)**
    *   LTAD 101: Why Early Specialization Fails. The 10-Year Rule. Bio-Banding.
    *   Role Clarity: Athlete = CEO. Coach = COO. Parent = Board/Investor.
    *   Communication Protocols: Weekly Stand-Up. Monthly Board Meeting. Match Day Rules. Car Ride Rule.
    *   Safeguarding: Background Checks. Rule of Two. Reporting Pathways.
*   **Phase 2: Quarterly Deep Dives (Ongoing)**
    *   Q1: Physical Literacy & Growth. PHV Tracking. Nutrition/Sleep Foundations. Injury Prevention.
    *   Q2: Mental Skills for Parents. Growth Mindset Language. Emotional Regulation. "Process over Outcome" Praise.
    *   Q3: Periodization & Tournament Parenting. Schedule Design. Travel Logistics. Match Day Routines.
    *   Q4: Transition & Life Skills. Education Balance. Career Planning. Identity Separation.
*   **Phase 3: Advanced Electives (Performance Parents)**
    *   Data Literacy: Reading KPI Dashboards. Understanding HRV/ACWR. Video Analysis Basics.
    *   Nutrition Periodization: Fueling for Training vs. Competition. Supplement Safety (NSF).
    *   College/Pro Pathway: NCAA Eligibility. Agent Selection. Financial Planning. NIL/Commercial.

### Pillar 2: Communication & Governance Protocols (The "Operating Agreement")
*   **Weekly "Stand-Up" (15 min, Monday):**
    *   Athlete + Coach (Parents Optional/Monthly).
    *   Agenda: Last Week Data → This Week Focus → Red Flags (Injury/Mental/School).
    *   Output: Shared Note (Google Doc/App). Parents Read-Only Access.
*   **Monthly "Board Meeting" (30 min):**
    *   Athlete + Coach + Parents (All).
    *   Agenda: Development Trajectory (LTAD Check) → Tournament Schedule → Financial/Logistics → School Balance → Values Check.
    *   Rules: Coach Presents Plan. Parents Ask Clarifying Questions. Athlete Voices Preference. **No Technical Debate.**
*   **Annual General Meeting (60-90 min):**
    *   Strategy Presentation by Coach. Financial Transparency. Policy Review. Contract Renewals.
*   **Escalation Ladder (Conflict Resolution):**
    1.  Coach-Athlete (Technical) → "Disagree & Commit" 2-Week Trial → Data Review.
    2.  Parent-Coach (Philosophy/Load/Values) → Private Conference → Technical Director Mediation → Independent Welfare Officer.
    3.  Parent-Athlete (Burnout/Motivation/Values) → Coach Facilitates → "Athlete Voice Supreme" → 4-Week Cooling Off Mandate.

### Pillar 3: Boundary Enforcement System (The "Fence")
*   **The "Lane Contract" (Signed Annually):**
    *   *Technical Lane:* Coach Only. Parents: Fund Lessons. Buy Spec'd Equipment.
    *   *Tactical Lane (Match):* Coach/Athlete Only. Parents: Spectate. No Signals. No Coaching.
    *   *Schedule Lane:* Coach Proposes. Parents Book/Fund. Athlete Inputs Preferences.
    *   *Medical Lane:* Medical Team Decides. Parents Approve/Fund. Coach Informed.
    *   *Emotional Lane:* Parents = Unconditional Support. "Proud of Effort." No Critique.
*   **Match Day Protocol (Non-Negotiable):**
    *   Pre-Match: Coach Talks Strategy (5 min). Parent Says: "Have fun / Proud of you." **Zero Technical Reminders.**
    *   During Match: Parents = Spectators. No Signals. No Body Language Coaching. No Official Interaction.
    *   Post-Match (0-60 min): **No Analysis.** Food. Water. "Great effort." Coach: No Technical.
    *   Post-Match (24 hrs): Debrief (Coach + Athlete). Parents Receive Summary.
*   **Car Ride Rule:** Music / Food / Silence. **Zero Tennis Talk.** Violation = Formal Warning.
*   **Sideline Behavior Code:** Applaud Good Play (Both Sides). No Coaching. No Officiating. No Emotion Dumping. Violation = Removal from Venue.

### Pillar 4: Logistical & Financial Operations (The "Back Office")
*   **Annual Budget & Cash Flow:**
    *   Coaching Fees. Tournament Travel (Flights, Hotels, Transport). Medical/Physio. Equipment. Tech/Analysis. Education/Tutoring. Contingency (10%).
    *   Quarterly Review: Actual vs. Budget. Sponsorship/Grant Applications.
*   **Travel & Logistics Management:**
    *   Calendar Sync (Shared Google Cal). Visa/Passport Tracking (6-month buffer).
    *   Travel Agent / Parent Coordinator Role. Accommodation Standards (Quiet, Kitchen, Gym Access).
    *   Tournament Entry Deadlines. Protected Ranking Applications. Wildcard Requests.
*   **Education & Tutoring Coordination:**
    *   School Partnership / Online School Enrollment. Tutor Schedule (Synced with Training).
    *   Exam/Assignment Calendar. NCAA Core Course Tracking (if US College Path).

### Pillar 5: Parent Self-Regulation & Support (The "Oxygen Mask")
*   **Parent Mental Skills:**
    *   Emotional Regulation: "Name it to Tame it." Breathing (4-7-8). Cognitive Reframing ("My child is learning" vs "My child is losing").
    *   Identity Separation: "I am a parent of a tennis player" ≠ "I am a tennis parent." Life outside tennis mandatory.
*   **Parent Peer Support:**
    *   Monthly Parent Circle (Facilitated by Welfare Officer). Normalize Struggles. Share Resources.
    *   Mentor Parents (Experienced → New). "Been there" guidance without advice-giving.
*   **Burnout Prevention:**
    *   Mandatory "Parent Off-Weeks" (2x/year). No Tournaments. No Tennis Talk.
    *   Hobby/Health Non-Negotiables. Marriage/Relationship Maintenance.

## The "Parent Support Plan" Document Template

### Section 1: Family & Athlete Profile
*   Athlete Name, DOB, Stage, Current Ranking. Parents/Guardians Names, Professions, Tennis Background.

### Section 2: Education Commitment
*   Onboarding Completion Date. Quarterly Deep Dive Attendance Log. Electives Selected.

### Section 3: Communication & Governance Log
*   Weekly Stand-Up Dates. Monthly Board Meeting Dates. AGM Date. Escalation Log (Date, Issue, Resolution).

### Section 4: Signed Lane Contract & Match Day Protocol
*   Signed Document. Annual Renewal Date. Violation Log (Date, Violation, Consequence).

### Section 5: Financial & Logistical Plan
*   Annual Budget. Quarterly Actuals. Travel Calendar. Visa/Passport Expiry. Education Plan.

### Section 6: Parent Self-Regulation Plan
*   Mental Skills Practice Log. Peer Circle Attendance. Off-Weeks Scheduled. Life Outside Tennis Activities.

### Section 7: Signatures & Review Dates
*   Parent 1. Parent 2. Athlete (if age appropriate). Coach. Welfare Officer. Next Review Date.

## The "Red Flag" Triggers for Parents
1.  **Technical Coaching from Sideline** (Immediate: Removal from Venue).
2.  **Car Ride Tennis Talk** > 2 incidents/quarter (Formal Warning).
3.  **Athlete Expresses Dread/Fear of Parent Reaction** (Immediate: Welfare Officer Intervention).
4.  **Parent-Coach Conflict Escalates to Athlete** (Immediate: Mediation).
5.  **Financial Arrears > 30 Days** (Schedule Review).
6.  **Parent Burnout Score > 7/10** (Mandatory Off-Week + Support).

## Practical Application: "The Shareholder's Portfolio"
The Parent Support Plan is the **Shareholder's Portfolio**. It defines the investment (money, time, emotion), the governance (voting rights, board meetings, reporting), the risk management (boundaries, escalation, insurance), and the shareholder's own wellbeing (diversification, health checks). **The best tennis parents don't coach tennis; they parent the human.** They fund the factory. They trust the COO. They protect the CEO. They enjoy the game.
