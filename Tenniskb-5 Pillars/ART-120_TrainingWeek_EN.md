# ART-120: The 5-Pillar Training Week — Periodized Session Blueprint

## Executive Summary
Knowledge without structure remains theory. This article provides the weekly training architecture that integrates all five pillars into a coherent, periodized schedule — ensuring technical, tactical, physical, and cognitive development progress in harmony without overtraining.

## The Weekly Blueprint (Performance Phase)

### Daily Session Structure (90-120 min)
1.  **Neural Activation (10 min):** Reaction games, light plyometrics (Pillar I + II).
2.  **Technical Block (30 min):** One stroke focus with constraint drills (Pillar III + I).
3.  **Tactical Block (25 min):** Pattern play, point construction (Pillar IV + II).
4.  **Conditioning Block (20 min):** Day-specific energy system work (Pillar V).
5.  **Integration Play (15 min):** Live points with theme constraint (all pillars).
6.  **Recovery & Review (10 min):** Breathing, video snippet, one takeaway (Pillar V + II).

### Weekly Schedule

| Day | Primary Pillar Emphasis | Session Theme |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monday** | I + III (Biomechanics + Technique) | Ground force and stroke mechanics refinement |
| **Tuesday** | V-heavy (Conditioning) | Anaerobic power: repeated sprint + serve clusters |
| **Wednesday** | II + IV (Neuro + Tactics) | Anticipation training, pattern drills, video |
| **Thursday** | I (Strength) | Strength maintenance, corrective work, mobility |
| **Friday** | IV + III (Tactics + Technique) | Match simulation, pressure point play |
| **Saturday** | V-light + Match | Competition or live match play |
| **Sunday** | V (Recovery) | Sleep priority, active recovery, weekly review |

### Load Management Rules
1.  **High/Low Principle:** Never stack two high-CNS days consecutively. Tuesday (high) is followed by Wednesday (moderate cognitive).
2.  **Hard ceiling:** Total weekly on-court volume capped at 12-14 h for performance athletes; tracks against HRV trend.
3.  **Flex Day:** If morning HRV drops > 10% below 7-day average, the day's conditioning block converts to technical-only.

### Block Periodization (12-Week Cycle)
*   **Weeks 1-4 (Accumulation):** Volume emphasis — technical reps, aerobic/anaerobic base.
*   **Weeks 5-8 (Transmutation):** Intensity emphasis — power conversion, tactical complexity.
*   **Weeks 9-11 (Realization):** Speed emphasis — match simulation, taper begins.
*   **Week 12 (Competition/Deload):** Taper to tournament or structured deload.

## Practical Application: The "Architecture Mindset"
Batches of drills do not make athletes; architected weeks do. Every session has one hashtag: today's theme. If a drill doesn't serve the theme, it doesn't happen today.
