# ART-177: The Science of Changeover Strategy — The 90-Second Window

## Executive Summary
Changeovers are not breaks — they are strategic opportunities. This article provides a structured 90-second changeover protocol that optimizes all 5 Pillars, ensuring you use every moment between games to your advantage.

## The 90-Second Window

### 1. The Changeover Structure
*   **0-30 seconds:** Physical recovery and hydration
*   **30-60 seconds:** Tactical analysis and adjustment
*   **60-90 seconds:** Mental preparation for the next game

### 2. The 5-Pillar Changeover Protocol

**Pillar I (Biomechanics):**
*   Sit down, elevate legs if possible
*   Light stretching of hip flexors and shoulders
*   Deep breathing to lower heart rate

**Pillar II (Neuro-Cognition):**
*   Quick pattern recognition update (what's working, what's not)
*   Decision on next game's tactical emphasis
*   Cue word selection for next game

**Pillar III (Technical):**
*   One technical reminder maximum (not a list)
*   Shadow swing if needed to reinforce feel
*   Confidence trigger activation

**Pillar IV (Tactics):**
*   Score-based strategy adjustment
*   Opponent tendency update
*   Serve/return pattern planning for next game

**Pillar V (Conditioning):**
*   Hydration (150-200ml with electrolytes)
*   Small carb snack if needed (banana, energy chew)
*   Heart rate recovery maximization

### 3. The Changeover Mindset
*   **Winning the set:** Maintain pressure, don't ease up
*   **Losing the set:** Reset emotionally, focus on the next game
*   **Tiebreak preparation:** Begin mental preparation one game early

## Practical Application: "The 90-Second Coach"
Use changeovers like a coach would — analyze, adjust, and prepare. The best players treat every changeover as a mini-coaching session with themselves.
