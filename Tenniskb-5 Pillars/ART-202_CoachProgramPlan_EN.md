# ART-202: Master Plan — Coach Program Plan (The "COO Document")

## Executive Summary
The Head Coach is the COO of the athlete's career enterprise. The "Coach Program Plan" operationalizes the Athlete Career Plan into a coherent 12-month technical-tactical-physical-mental curriculum. It defines the coaching philosophy, the periodized syllabus, the skill acquisition roadmap, the coaching behavior standards, and the coach's own development plan.

## The Program Plan Architecture: Four Pillars

### Pillar 1: Coaching Philosophy & Identity (The "Why")
*   **Coaching Vision:** "I develop [player profile] who win by [method]." (e.g., "I develop aggressive all-court players who win by dictating with forehand +1 and emotional regulation.")
*   **Core Values:** Top 5 Coaching Non-Negotiables (e.g., Athlete Autonomy, Evidence-Based, Long-Term over Short-Term, Psychological Safety, Integrity).
*   **Coaching Style Profile:** Situational Leadership Self-Assessment. Default style (Directive / Supportive / Delegative). Adaptability Plan.
*   **The "Contract" with Athlete:** Explicit agreement on Roles, Communication, Decision Rights, Conflict Resolution. (Signed annually).

### Pillar 2: The 12-Month Periodized Syllabus (The "What & When")
*   **Macrocycle Map:** Aligned with Athlete Career Plan Schedule (ART-191).
*   **Mesocycle Themes (Monthly Blocks):**
    *   *Base Phase (8-12 wks):* Technical Reconstruction / Physical Base / Tactical Foundation / Mental Skills Install.
    *   *Pre-Comp Phase (4-6 wks):* Pattern Automation / Specific Endurance / Simulation / Routine Mastery.
    *   *Competition Phase (Peak):* Sharpening / Match Management / Recovery Focus / Trust.
    *   *Transition (2-4 wks):* Regeneration / Review / Technical Audit / Education.
*   **Microcycle Templates (Weekly):** Standard Training Week (ART-170). Tournament Week. Travel Week. Regeneration Week.
*   **Skill Acquisition Roadmap:** 
    *   *Technical:* Priority 1, 2, 3 Skills per Quarter. Constraint Progression Map (ART-161).
    *   *Tactical:* Pattern Library Development. Opponent Profile Templates. Game Plan Templates.
    *   *Physical:* S&C Periodization Sync. Testing Calendar.
    *   *Mental:* Skill Install Sequence (Routines → Pressure → Competition → Adversity).

### Pillar 3: Coaching Behavior Standards (The "How")
*   **The "Coaching Behavior Budget":**
    *   Question:Instruction Ratio Target > 1:1.
    *   Feedback Frequency: Faded Schedule (ART-177).
    *   Athlete Talk Time Target > 60% of session.
    *   External Focus Cue % Target > 80%.
*   **Session Architecture Compliance:** Universal Session Architecture (ART-177) mandatory for all sessions.
*   **Communication Protocols:**
    *   Pre-Session: Contract (5 min).
    *   In-Session: Constraint Leadership. Silent Observation in Sim/Comp phases.
    *   Post-Session: Download (5 min). Documentation.
*   **Relationship Boundaries:** Professional. No Dual Relationships. Safeguarding Compliance (ART-179).

### Pillar 4: Coach Development Plan (The "Growth")
*   **Mentor/Supervisor/Peer Structure (ART-178):**
    *   Mentor: Monthly Career/Philosophy.
    *   Supervisor: Bi-Weekly Session Review.
    *   Peer Circle: Monthly Case Consultation.
*   **Deliberate Practice Curriculum:**
    *   Quarterly: Video Audit of Coaching Behavior (CBAS).
    *   Monthly: "Teach Back" Complex Concept in 2 min.
    *   Annual: Conference / Certification / 5-Pillar Deep Dive.
*   **Self-Regulation:** Coach HRV. Load Monitoring. Mental Skills Coach for Coach. Annual Burnout Screen (MBI).

## The "Coach Program Plan" Document Template

### Section 1: Coach Profile
*   Name, Certifications, Experience, Current Athletes, Coaching Tenure with Athlete.

### Section 2: Philosophy & Identity
*   Coaching Vision Statement. Core Values (5). Style Profile. Athlete Contract Summary.

### Section 3: Annual Periodization Overview
*   Macrocycle Visual Map. Mesocycle Table (Month, Theme, Primary Focus, Secondary Focus, Key KPIs, Testing Dates).

### Section 4: Quarterly Skill Acquisition Plans (Q1-Q4)
*   **Technical:** Skill, Current Level, Target Level, Constraint Progression (Week 1-12), Assessment Method.
*   **Tactical:** Patterns to Install, Scouting Templates, Game Plan Variants.
*   **Physical:** S&C Phase, Key Lifts/Conditioning, Testing Benchmarks.
*   **Mental:** Skill to Install, Drill/Protocol, Application Context.

### Section 5: Weekly Microcycle Templates
*   Standard Week. Tournament Week (Home). Tournament Week (Away). Regeneration Week. Travel Week.
*   Each: Daily Session Themes, Volume/Intensity Targets, Coach Focus Points.

### Section 6: Coaching Behavior KPIs & Audit Schedule
*   Target Ratios. Video Audit Dates. Supervisor Session Dates. Peer Circle Dates.

### Section 7: Coach Development Calendar
*   Mentor Meetings. Supervision Sessions. Peer Circles. Conferences. Certifications. Mental Skills Sessions. Self-Care/Recovery Plan.

### Section 8: Risk Management & Contingencies
*   Athlete Injury Protocols. Slump Protocols. Athlete-Coach Conflict Resolution. Personal Emergency Plan.

### Section 9: Review & Signatures
*   Quarterly Review Dates. Annual Review Date. Signatures: Coach, Athlete, Program Director (if applicable).

## The "Red Flag" Triggers for Coach
1.  **Two consecutive weeks** of Session Architecture non-compliance.
2.  **Athlete Talk Time < 40%** for 3+ sessions.
3.  **Coach HRV > 10% below baseline** for 5+ days.
4.  **Athlete KPI stagnation** for 4+ weeks without protocol change.
5.  **Values breach** (Safeguarding, Autonomy, Integrity).

## Practical Application: "The COO's Clipboard"
The Coach Program Plan is not a binder on a shelf. It is the **COO's daily clipboard** — digital or physical. It dictates *what* happens in every session, *how* the coach behaves, and *when* the coach grows. The Athlete Career Plan (ART-191) is the Strategy; the Coach Program Plan is the **Operations Manual**. They must be version-controlled together. **If the Coach Plan doesn't change when the Athlete Plan changes, the operation is broken.**
