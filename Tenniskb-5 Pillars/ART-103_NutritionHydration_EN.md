# ART-103: Nutrition & Hydration for Performance

## Executive Summary
Nutrition and hydration are critical components of elite tennis performance. They allow the athlete to maintain high-intensity performance throughout the match and recover effectively between points and matches. This article examines the nutrition and hydration protocols that allow elite players to maintain high-intensity performance and recover effectively. Understanding these protocols is essential for developing a complete, integrated athlete.

## The Nutrition & Hydration Framework

### 1. The 30-Minute Nutrition & Hydration Protocol
The 30-minute nutrition and hydration protocol is a simple but effective way to improve nutrition and hydration. It allows the athlete to maintain high-intensity performance for short periods of time.
*   **The Ideal Protocol:** The athlete completes 30-minute nutrition and hydration protocols with 30-minute rest intervals.
*   **The Common Errors:**
    *   **The "Long" Protocol:** The athlete completes protocols that are too long, causing them to fatigue too quickly.
    *   **The "Short" Protocol:** The athlete completes protocols that are too short, causing them to not maintain high-intensity performance.
