## The Second-Half Trajectory (ART 131–200)
Based on the audit, the Second Half will focus on:
1.  **Psychological Mastery (131-140):** Mental toughness, rituals, anxiety, goals, relationships, self-correction.
2.  **Specialized Application (141-170):** Surface mastery, doubles, opponent archetypes, equipment, analytics, junior/pro pathways.
3.  **Advanced Integration (171-190):** Real-time decision making, match management, coaching others, injury return, peak performance.
4.  **The Final Capstone (191-200):** The Complete 5-Pillar Manual, The Master Plan, The Legacy.

## The Audit Protocol
1.  **Self-Assessment:** Athlete scores honestly.
2.  **Coach Assessment:** Coach scores independently.
3.  **Comparison Meeting:** Discuss discrepancies (these are the blind spots).
4.  **Action Plan:** Top 3 priorities for the next 10 articles.
5.  **Re-Audit:** At ART-200.

## Practical Application: The "Mirror"
This audit is not a test. It is a mirror. If the reflection shows a fragmented athlete, the Second Half builds the glue. If it shows a complete athlete, the Second Half sharpens the blade.
