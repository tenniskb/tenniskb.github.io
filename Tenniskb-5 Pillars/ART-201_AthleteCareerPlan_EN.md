# ART-201: Master Plan — Athlete Career Plan (The "CEO Document")

## Executive Summary
The athlete is the CEO of their career. The "Athlete Career Plan" is the strategic business plan — a living document that aligns Vision → Outcome → Performance → Process goals across the 5 Pillars, with quarterly reviews, annual pivots, and a 10-year horizon. This article provides the template and governance system.

## The Career Plan Architecture: Four Horizons

### Horizon 1: The Vision (10-Year / "The North Star")
*   **Identity Statement:** "I am a [playing style] player who [unique value proposition]." (e.g., "I am an aggressive all-court player who dictates with forehand +1 and mental resilience.")
*   **Legacy Goals:** What do you want said at your retirement press conference? (Ranking peak, Slam results, Impact, Character)
*   **Values Compass:** Top 5 Non-Negotiables (e.g., Integrity, Growth, Family, Health, Excellence). Every decision filters through these.

### Horizon 2: The Outcome Goals (3-5 Year / "The Summit Targets")
*   **Ranking Targets:** Year 1: Top X. Year 3: Top Y. Year 5: Top Z.
*   **Tournament Milestones:** Slam Main Draw (Year X), Slam QF (Year Y), Masters 1000 Win (Year Z), Olympics/World Team.
*   **Weapon Certification:** "Forehand = Elite." "Serve = Weapon." "Return = Neutralizer." (Validated by 5-Pillar KPIs).

### Horizon 3: The Performance Goals (Annual / "The Season KPIs")
*   **5-Pillar KPI Dashboard Targets:**
    *   Pillar I: GRF Symmetry > 95%, Hip Separation > 40°.
    *   Pillar II: Routine Fidelity > 95%, Quiet Eye > 300ms.
    *   Pillar III: Serve % > 65%, FH RPM > 3200, BH Depth > 70%.
    *   Pillar IV: 1st Strike Efficiency > 55%, Pattern Unpredictability > 70%.
    *   Pillar V: HRV Baseline > X, Injury Days < 5/yr, Sleep 8.5h avg.
*   **Schedule Design:** Surface Blocks, Peaking Windows, Travel Load Budget (TLU < 30/wk).

### Horizon 4: The Process Goals (Quarterly-Monthly-Weekly-Daily / "The Daily Grind")
*   **Quarterly:** Full 5-Pillar Assessment (ART-118). Protocol Pivot.
*   **Monthly:** KPI Trend Review. Skill Acquisition Block Focus.
*   **Weekly:** Performance Board Meeting (ART-170). Theme Setting (ART-177).
*   **Daily:** Morning Contract (ART-177). Evening Download (ART-177). Load Management.

## The Governance System: "The Board of Directors"

### The Athlete (CEO)
*   **Authority:** Final say on Vision, Values, Schedule, Team Hiring/Firing.
*   **Duty:** Execute Process Goals. Report Honestly. Own the Outcome.

### The Head Coach / Program Director (COO)
*   **Authority:** Technical/Tactical/Physical Program Design. Periodization. Daily Training Environment.
*   **Accountability:** To Athlete (CEO) & Ethics Committee. Quarterly Performance Review.

### The Performance Team (VPs)
*   **S&C Coach (VP Physical):** Capacity, Resilience, Peaking.
*   **Mental Skills Coach (VP Cognitive):** Routines, Pressure, Identity.
*   **Physio/Medical (VP Health):** RTP, Prevention, Longevity.
*   **Nutritionist (VP Fuel):** Periodized Nutrition, Supplement Governance.
*   **Data Scientist (VP Intelligence):** Dashboard, Analytics, Tech Integration.
*   **Agent/Manager (VP Operations):** Schedule, Contracts, Logistics, Commercial.

### The Ethics Committee (Independent Board)
*   **Role:** Safeguarding, Medical Autonomy, Anti-Doping, Commercial Conflict Resolution. (ART-179).

## The Review Cadence

| Cadence | Meeting | Participants | Agenda | Output |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Daily** | Morning Contract / Evening Download | Athlete + Coach | Theme, Commitment, 1 Win / 1 Adjust | Focus Alignment |
| **Weekly** | Performance Board | Full Team | Medical → Physical → Tech → Tactical → Mental → Logistics → Decisions | Adjusted Weekly Plan |

## The "Career Plan" Document Template (Living Document)

### Section 1: Athlete Profile
*   Name, DOB, Nationality, Handedness, Playing Style, Current Ranking, Bio-Age, Injury History.

### Section 2: Vision & Values (Horizon 1)
*   Identity Statement. Legacy Goals. Values Compass. "Why I Play."

### Section 3: Outcome Targets (Horizon 2)
*   Ranking Trajectory Table. Tournament Milestone Map. Weapon Certification Status.

### Section 4: 5-Pillar KPI Targets (Horizon 3)
*   Current Baseline → Year Target → Gap → Primary Intervention per Pillar.

### Section 5: Periodized Schedule (Horizon 3)
*   Macrocycle Map. Surface Blocks. Peaking Windows. TLU Budget. Protected Weeks.

### Section 6: Team & Contracts (Governance)
*   Role, Name, Contract Term, KPIs, Review Date, Compensation Structure.

### Section 7: Risk Register & Contingencies
*   Injury Scenarios (RTP Protocols). Slump Protocols (ART-171). Burnout Signals. Commercial Risks. Ethics Triggers.

### Section 8: Financial Plan
*   Revenue Projections (Prize Money, Sponsorship, Appearance). Expense Budget (Team, Travel, Medical, Tech, Living). Savings/Investment Targets.

### Section 9: Education & Post-Career
*   Current Education Status. Certification Pathway. Internship/Network Plan. Transition Fund.

### Section 10: Signatures & Commitment
*   Athlete. Head Coach. Agent. Parent/Guardian (if minor). Ethics Rep. Date. Next Review Date.

## The "Red Pen" Rule (Accountability)
*   **If KPI misses target for 2 consecutive quarters → Mandatory Protocol Change.** Not "try harder." Change the *system* (Drill, Load, Coach, Environment, Schedule).
*   **If Values Compass violated → Immediate Ethics Review.** No exceptions for ranking/results.

## Practical Application: "The CEO's Desk"
The Athlete Career Plan sits on the CEO's desk. It is not a PDF in a folder. It is a **living spreadsheet / Notion workspace / whiteboard** updated weekly. The CEO (Athlete) reads it every Monday. The COO (Coach) references it every session. The Board reviews it quarterly. **If it's not current, it's not a plan — it's a wish.**
