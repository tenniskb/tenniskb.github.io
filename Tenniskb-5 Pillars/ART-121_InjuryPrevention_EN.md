# ART-121: Injury Prevention — The 5-Pillar Resilience System

## Executive Summary
Injury is not random bad luck; it is the predictable output of load exceeding capacity. This article maps the most common tennis injuries to their biomechanical root causes and provides a 5-Pillar prevention system that builds capacity while managing load.

## The Injury Map — Root Cause Analysis

### 1. Shoulder (Rotator Cuff, Impingement)
*   **Primary Cause:** Kinetic chain leak — arm compensates for insufficient hip-torso separation on serve.
*   **5-Pillar Link:** Pillar I failure (pelvic snap absent) → Pillar I conditioning gap (eccentric cuff weakness).
*   **Prevention Protocol:** Crossover symmetry / band external rotations (daily), serve volume caps, hip separation mobility work.

### 2. Elbow (Tennis Elbow, Lateral Epicondylitis)
*   **Primary Cause:** Late contact + wrist-led strokes + grip death-grip.
*   **5-Pillar Link:** Pillar II (poor timing → late contact) + Pillar III (arming the ball).
*   **Prevention Protocol:** Eccentric wrist extensor loading (Tyler twist), contact-point drills, grip pressure awareness (4/10 scale).

### 3. Lower Back (Lumbar Strain)
*   **Primary Cause:** Lumbar rotation substituting for thoracic rotation; repetitive kick serve extension without core support.
*   **Prevention Protocol:** Thoracic mobility (open books, foam roller), anti-rotation core work (Pallof press), serve count management.

### 4. Knee (Patellar Tendinopathy)
*   **Primary Cause:** High-volume open-stance deceleration on hard courts; weak glute medius valgus control.
*   **Prevention Protocol:** Heavy slow resistance goblet squats, lateral band walks, landing mechanics training.

### 5. Wrist & Ankle
*   **Wrist:** Ulnar-sided pain from extreme grips + off-center contact → strengthen with rice bucket, improve timing.
*   **Ankle:** Sprains from lateral lunges → single-leg balance progression, proprioceptive training.

## The Load-Capacity Equation
**Injury Risk = Acute Load / Chronic Capacity**
*   Acute: this week's training + match volume.
*   Chronic: rolling 4-week average capacity.
*   **Safe zone:** ratio 0.8–1.3. Above 1.5 = red flag.

## The Daily 15-Minute Resilience Routine
1.  Band external rotation: 2 x 15 (shoulder).
2.  Pallof press: 2 x 10/side (core anti-rotation).
3.  Tyler twist: 1 x 15 (elbow).
4.  Lateral band walk: 2 x 12/direction (hips/knees).
5.  Single-leg balance with reach: 2 x 8/leg (ankle).

## Practical Application: The "Capacity Builder" Mindset
Don't just avoid injury — build so much capacity that match loads become trivial. Prevention is training, not rest.
