# ART-186: The Science of Travel Recovery — Beating Jet Lag

## Executive Summary
International competition demands rapid timezone adaptation. This article provides a science-based jet lag protocol covering all 5 Pillars, ensuring you arrive at international tournaments ready to perform.

## The Jet Lag Challenge

### 1. Why Jet Lag Impairs Performance
*   **Circadian disruption:** Body clock misaligned with local time
*   **Sleep disruption:** Poor sleep quality and reduced sleep duration
*   **Cognitive impairment:** Decision speed and accuracy decline
*   **Physical fatigue:** Reduced power output and slower recovery

### 2. The 5-Pillar Jet Lag Protocol

**Pre-Travel (3-5 days before):**
*   Gradually shift sleep/wake times toward destination timezone
*   Adjust meal times to match destination schedule
*   Begin light exposure management

**During Travel:**
*   Stay hydrated (3L+ water, avoid alcohol)
*   Move regularly on the plane (walk, stretch, compression socks)
*   Sleep according to destination night-time
*   Use eye mask, earplugs, neck pillow for quality sleep

**Upon Arrival:**
*   Immediate light exposure in the morning (30+ min sunlight)
*   Light exercise within 2 hours of arrival (walk, jog, mobility)
*   No naps longer than 30 minutes
*   Match sleep schedule to local time immediately

**Pillar-Specific Adjustments:**
*   **Pillar I:** Extra mobility work to combat stiffness from travel
*   **Pillar II:** Cognitive tests on arrival to assess impairment level
*   **Pillar III:** Light hit on arrival day to re-establish feel
*   **Pillar IV:** Simplified tactical approach for first match
*   **Pillar V:** HRV monitoring to adjust training load

## Practical Application: "The Time Zone Warrior"
Jet lag is not an excuse — it's a challenge to be managed. Arrive early when possible, follow the protocol, and your body will adapt faster than your opponents.
