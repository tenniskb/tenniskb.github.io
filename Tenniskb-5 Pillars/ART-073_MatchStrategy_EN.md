# ART-073: Match Strategy & Point Play

## Executive Summary
Match strategy is not just about winning points; it is about managing the match. Elite players understand that every point is a piece of a larger puzzle. This article examines the strategic principles that allow elite players to control the match, adapt to their opponent's style, and maximize their chances of winning. Understanding these principles is essential for developing a complete, integrated athlete.

## The Match Strategy Framework

### 1. The 3-Point Window
The 3-point window is a simple but effective way to manage the match. It divides the match into three phases (0-3, 4-6, 7-10) and allows the athlete to adjust their strategy based on the current score.
*   **The Ideal Strategy:** The athlete adjusts their strategy based on the current score, ensuring that they are always in control of the match.
*   **The Common Errors:**
    *   **The "All-Out" Strategy:** The athlete tries to win every point, causing them to be out of position.
    *   **The "Passive" Strategy:** The athlete tries to save every point, causing them to be out of position.
