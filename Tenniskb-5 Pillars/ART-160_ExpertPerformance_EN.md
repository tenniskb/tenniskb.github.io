2.  **Expertise Gap Analysis:** Calculate what the expertise doesn't provide (e.g., "My expertise is inconsistent because of my kinetic chain alignment").

4.  **Implementation:** Adjust training to accommodate the new expertise.
5.  **Evaluation:** Measure the impact on performance (e.g., "My expertise speed increased by 5 km/h").

### 2. The Expertise Memory System
The brain stores three types of expertise knowledge:
*   **Procedural:** Automatic responses to common situations (e.g., "When I hit a kick serve, I always move to the backhand side").
*   **Declarative:** Explicit rules (e.g., "I never attack a serve-and-volleyer's second serve").
*   **Episodic:** Context-specific memories (e.g., "In this match, he's been hitting his first serves to the deuce side").
