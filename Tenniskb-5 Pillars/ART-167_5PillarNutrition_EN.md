# ART-167: The 5-Pillar Nutrition System — Fueling Every Pillar

## Executive Summary
Nutrition is not one-size-fits-all — each pillar has specific fuel demands. This article provides pillar-specific nutrition protocols to optimize biomechanical output, cognitive function, technical consistency, tactical clarity, and recovery across the training cycle.

## Pillar-Specific Nutrition

### 1. Pillar I (Biomechanics) — Structural Fuel
*   **Protein:** 1.6-2.2 g/kg body weight daily for tissue repair
*   **Omega-3:** 2-3g EPA/DHA for joint health and inflammation control
*   **Collagen:** 15g + 50mg vitamin C 30 min before training for tendon/ligament support

### 2. Pillar II (Neuro-Cognition) — Brain Fuel
*   **Creatine:** 3-5g daily for cognitive processing speed under fatigue
*   **Caffeine:** 2-3 mg/kg 45 min before competition for alertness
*   **L-Theanine:** 200mg with caffeine to smooth focus without jitters

### 3. Pillar III (Technique) — Glycogen Management
*   **Carb Periodization:** High carb on technical days, lower on tactical/light days
*   **Pre-Session:** 30-60g carbs 60-90 min before for brain glucose
*   **Intra-Session:** 30g carbs/hour for sessions > 90 min

### 4. Pillar IV (Tactics) — Hydration for Cognition
*   **Dehydration > 2% body weight:** Impaired decision-making equivalent to 0.08% BAC
*   **Protocol:** 5-7 ml/kg 4 hours before, sip 150-200ml every 15-20 min during

### 5. Pillar V (Conditioning) — Recovery Nutrition
*   **Post-Session (within 30 min):** 0.4g/kg protein + 1.0g/kg carbs
*   **Pre-Sleep:** 40g casein for overnight muscle protein synthesis
*   **Hydration:** 150% of fluid losses within 2 hours post-exercise

## Practical Application: "Eat for the Pillar"
Don't just eat healthy — eat specifically for what you're training tomorrow. The right fuel at the right time accelerates adaptation in that pillar.
