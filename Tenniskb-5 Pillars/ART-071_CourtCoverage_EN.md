# ART-071: Court Coverage & Movement Patterns for Elite Tennis

## Executive Summary
Court coverage is not just about moving quickly; it is about moving intelligently. Elite players understand that every movement on the court has a tactical purpose. This article examines the movement patterns that allow elite players to cover the court efficiently, recover quickly, and maintain control of the rally. Understanding these patterns is essential for developing a complete, integrated athlete.

## The Movement Patterns Framework

### 1. The 3-6-9 System
The 3-6-9 system is a simple but effective way to cover the court. It divides the court into three vertical zones (3, 6, 9) and three horizontal zones (1, 2, 3).
*   **The Ideal Movement:** The athlete moves from one zone to another, ensuring that they are always in position to receive the ball.
*   **The Common Errors:**
    *   **The "Random" Movement:** The athlete moves without a clear pattern, causing them to be out of position.
    *   **The "Static" Movement:** The athlete stays in one position, causing them to be out of position.

### 2. The 1-2-3 System
