    Drills Library¶

    Train the Chain. Every Drill Has a Variable Target.

    How to Use This Library¶

    Step Action
    1 Take the Diagnostic → find your broken link
    2 Find that variable's drills below
    3 Do the drill for 15-20 minutes
    4 Re-test immediately → ball behavior changed?
    5 If yes → integrate. If no → wrong variable or wrong drill

    Variable-Specific Drills¶

    Q - Quiet Eye¶

    Drill Setup Execution Target
    Park & Hold Partner feeds Say "PARK" at their contact. Hold eyes on your zone until ball crosses net. 90% park rate, 200ms+ hold
    Eyes Closed Shadow No ball Shadow stroke, eyes closed. Feel contact zone. Open eyes at "contact." Accurate face angle 80%+
    Two-Ball Track Coach feeds 2 balls rapid Track first to contact, instantly jump to second contact zone. <100ms jump time
    Quiet Eye Pressure Points with consequence Miss park = pushup. Make park = point. Play to 11. >85% park under pressure

    V - Vestibular¶

    Drill Setup Execution Target
    Chin Tuck Freeze Shadow or ball Chin tucked, freeze at contact 3 sec. Check head still. Zero head movement
    VOR Cancellation Fixate thumb, move head L/R Thumb stays clear while head moves. 30 sec. No blur, no saccades
    Head-Still Rally Rally with partner Both keep head frozen (chin to shoulder). Rally 20 balls. 20 balls, head still
    Gaze Stability Progression Stand on foam/pillow Fixate target, move head → stand on one leg → eyes closed. Stable gaze all levels

    ROOT - Rooting¶

    Drill Setup Execution Target
    Screw & Lift Barefoot, athletic stance Screw R foot CW, feel arch lift → oblique tension. Hold 5s. 10/side. Feel spiral line tension
    Single-Leg Root On one leg, tripod Screw, lift arch, hold 10s. Add perturbation (partner pushes). Stable, no wobble
    Shadow with Root Cues Shadow strokes "Screw → Lift → Load → Brake" verbalized each phase. Consistent feel
    Med Ball Root Throws 3kg ball, athletic stance Screw back foot, load, throw diagonally. Feel ground→hand. Power from ground

    F - Fascia (Spiral & Back Lines)¶

    Drill Setup Execution Target
    Line Trace Slow shadow Trace spiral line (FH) or back line (BH) with opposite hand. Feel continuity. No slack, no knots
    Dynamic Stretch Flow 5 min pre-practice World's Greatest Stretch → Scorpion → Arm Bar → Thread Needle. Lines fluid
    Band Resisted Patterns Light band Anchor band, trace FH/BH pattern against light resistance. Tension throughout
    Loaded Carries Farmer / Suitcase / Overhead Walk 30m, maintain line tension. No leaks. Chain integrity under load

    P - Proprioception¶

    Drill Setup Execution Target
    Eyes Closed Contact Partner feeds easy Close eyes at bounce. Call "FH/BH, Clean/Off, Open/Closed." 80%+ accuracy
    Palm Pressure Scale Hit balls Rate palm pressure 1-10 at contact. Target 6-7. Consistent 6-7/10
    Face Angle Guess Blindfolded Partner sets racket face. Guess open/closed/neutral degrees. ±5° accuracy
    Uneven Surface Balance Balance pad/BOSU Shadow strokes on unstable surface. Maintain tripod. Stable stroke

    T - Tensegrity¶

    Drill Setup Execution Target
    Tent Trace Slow shadow Feel "bones float, fascia holds." No local knots. Continuous tension. Zero slack
    Anti-Rotation Press Band/cable Press out, resist rotation. 3×10/side. Torso stable, limbs move
    Dead Bug Variations Floor Opposite arm/leg, low back pinned. 3×10/side. Deep core = tent poles
    Breathing Reset Between points 2 slow breaths: inhale expand 360°, exhale tension out. Parasympathetic shift

    SSC - Stretch-Shortening Cycle¶

    Drill Setup Execution Target
    Bounce-Drop-Push Drop ball, let bounce Racket moving FORWARD before ball peaks. Forces short amortization. 20 reps, continuous
    Pogo Hops In place Stiff ankle, quick contacts. 3×20s. <200ms contact
    Depth Jumps 30-40cm box Step off, land, immediate jump up. 3×5. RSI >2.0
    Reactive Med Ball Partner throws, you catch & throw back Minimize hold time. 3×8. Minimal amortization

    8 - Figure-8 Regulation¶

    Drill Setup Execution Target
    Shadow 8s No ball Continuous figure-8s. No pause anywhere. 2 min. Smooth, no flat spots
    Size Regulation Partner calls "20% / 50% / 100%" → same path, change size/speed. Same path, diff scale
    8 + Brake Shadow 8 → hard front leg brake at exit → freeze 3s. Brake at 8 exit
    Rally 8 Count Rally Count "1-2-3-4" = one 8 cycle. Feel rhythm. Consistent timing

    45° - Swing Plane¶

    Drill Setup Execution Target
    Back Pocket → Shoulder Shadow Hand traces diagonal: back pocket → opposite shoulder. Film side. 45° diagonal
    Glass Plane Shadow Imagine glass Racket stays ON 45° glass. Brake ON glass. Pulse THROUGH glass. No deviation
    Ball Flight Check Hit balls Observe: rises ~3ft over net, dips inside baseline. Adjust. Consistent arc
    Two-Ball Plane Two balls same height Hit both on same 45° path. Groove plane. Identical trajectory

    BRAKE - Braking¶

    Drill Setup Execution Target
    Wall Push Pulse 1" from wall Palm/edge on wall. Push wall. Feel spiral/back line fire. Release. 20 reps. Ground→hand pulse
    Brake & Freeze Shadow Swing → hard front leg brake → freeze 3s. Check: torso stopped? Instant stop
    Med Ball Slams 3kg ball Slam diagonally. Front leg braces. Torso stops. Arm whips. Brake → whip
    One-Leg Brake Single leg Shadow FH/BH on one leg. Front leg brakes. Balance held. Stable brake

    PULSE - Impulse¶

    Drill Setup Execution Target
    1-Inch Wall Push Racket butt on wall Palm pushes 1" through wall. 30ms peak. 20 reps. Sharp impulse
    Saber 3-Inch Shadow BH Edge slices 3" down-through. Freeze. 20 reps. Edge leads
    Pulse Regulation Partner feeds "Soft / Medium / Hard" → same stroke, regulate pulse. 3 distinct levels
    Eyes Closed Pulse Easy feed Close eyes at bounce. Pulse on feel only. Feel-based impulse

    JIN - Ting, Hua, Fa¶

    Drill Setup Execution Target
    Eyes Closed Shadow No ball Full shadow, eyes closed. Feel every inch of stretch. High fidelity feel
    Absorb & Redirect Partner hits hard Short takeback. Absorb pace. Redirect. No swing. Hua Jin
    Fa Jin Snap Shadow Stretch → instant snap. No windup. Like striking match. Fa Jin
    Variable Pace Rally Partner varies Fast/slow/spin/flat. Adjust takeback, pulse each ball. Ting→Hua→Fa flow

    Integrated Chain Drills¶

    The Daily 15 (Pre-Practice)¶

    1. Arch lifts + screw (10/side)          → ROOT
2. Pogo hops (20s)                       → SSC
3. Med ball rotational throw (4/side)    → 8, 45°, BRAKE
4. Band pull-apart + rotation (10/side)  → F, T
5. Saccades + VOR (30s each)             → Q, V
6. Shadow FH/BH (30s each)               → Full chain
TOTAL: 5 minutes

    The Chain Integrator (Weekly)¶

    Round 1: 5 FH Push - focus Q
Round 2: 5 BH Saber - focus V
Round 3: 5 Serve - focus ROOT
Round 4: 5 Return - focus 8
Round 5: 5 Volley - focus BRAKE
Round 6: 5 Touch - focus PULSE
Each round: full routine, film 1, diagnose.

    Pressure Simulation (Bi-Weekly)¶

    Set 1: 5 points - must execute routine perfectly (scored by partner)
Set 2: 5 points - down 0-30, must hold chain
Set 3: 5 points - up 40-15, no celebration, process only
Set 4: 5 points - tiebreak, fatigue induced (burpees between)
Track: Routine %, Chain feel 1-10, Ball outcome.

    Drill Progression Rules¶

    Level Criteria to Advance
    1. Shadow 20 reps perfect feel, no ball
    2. Slow Feed 20 balls, 80% chain feel, ball in play
    3. Live Ball Rally 20, maintain chain under variability
    4. Pressure Points with consequence, routine holds
    5. Match Tournament play, chain automatic
