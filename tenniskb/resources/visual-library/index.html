    Chapter 30 — Unified System (5 of 5)

    Figure 30.4: Fault Diagnosis Master Flowchart

    What it shows: Six symptoms (LONG, INTO THE NET, NO POWER, NO CONTROL, SHANK, LATE) mapped through five diagnostic checkpoints (FACE → CONTACT → TAKEBACK → TENSION → Q/V/Root) with specific fix recommendations per chain. A mid-match troubleshooting tool that condenses the entire fault-card library onto one page.

    Use this when: A player reports a stroke error mid-match. Walk backward from ball flight through the five checkpoints to isolate the broken link.

    Figure 30.5: Complete System Architecture

    What it shows: The finished eight-layer tower model — the meta-diagram of the entire Q-V-ROOT-8-45-Impulse-Jin system. Information/force flows UP from Rhythmic Footwork (foundation) through Expression (JIN), Impulse Timing, Dynamic Motion, Structural Alignment, Somatic Sensation, Visual-Vestibular, to Cognitive Decision (peak). Gaze/decision flows DOWN via Quiet Eye to direct execution.

    Use this when: You need the big picture. Every other diagram in the book is a smaller sketch of this exact structure.
