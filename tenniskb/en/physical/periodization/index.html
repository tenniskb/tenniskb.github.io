    Periodization¶

    Peak When It Matters.

    The Principle¶

    Periodization aligns physical, technical, and mental peaks for competition. Random training = random results.

    Annual Macrocycle¶

    Phase Months Duration Focus Volume Intensity
    Transition Oct 2-4 wks Recovery, fun, other sports Very Low Very Low
    General Prep Nov-Jan 8-12 wks Base strength, movement quality, aerobic High Low-Mod
    Specific Prep Feb-Apr 8-12 wks Tennis-specific strength, power, patterns Mod-High High
    Pre-Comp May-Jun 4-6 wks Match simulation, sharpening, taper Low-Mod Very High
    Competition Jul-Sep 12-16 wks Maintain, peak for majors, recover Low Match

    Mesocycles (4-Week Blocks)¶

    Block Structure¶

    Week 1 2 3 4
    Load 70% 80% 90% 50% (Deload)
    Focus Learn/Stabilize Develop Peak/Overreach Recover/Adapt

    Deload Week (Week 4)¶

    • 50% volume
    • Maintain intensity
    • Add: mobility, visualization, mental skills
    • Result: Supercompensation

    Periodization by System¶

    System Off-Season Pre-Season In-Season
    ROOT (Feet/Ankle) High volume barefoot, single-leg Plyometric integration Maintain stiffness, low volume
    F/P/T (Fascia/Prop/Tensegrity) Slow loaded carries, flow Dynamic elastic, reactive Pre-match activation only
    SSC/PULSE (Elastic Power) Depth jumps, bounds Med ball, sprints, reactive Match warmup intent
    8/45° (Patterns) Shadow + band, slow Full speed patterns, variability Tournament patterns
    BRAKE (Eccentric) Nordic, tempo, landings Decel drills, approach work Maintain landing quality
    Q/V (Vision/Vestibular) Daily drills, strobe Pressure simulation Pre-match routine
    JIN (Expression) Flow, variability Matchplay, competition Matchplay only

    Weekly Microcycle (In-Season Example)¶

    Day Physical Technical Mental Load
    Mon Power + Vision Serve + 1 patterns Visualization High
    Tue Strength + Chain Return + 1 patterns Pressure drills High
    Wed Mobility + Light feet Matchplay (sets) Routine practice Mod
    Thu Power + Vision Serve + Return Match sim High
    Fri Light activation Pre-tourney routine Strategy Low
    Sat MATCH MATCH MATCH MATCH
    Sun Recovery walk Visualization Review Off

    Tapering for Major Events¶

    Days Out Volume Intensity Focus
    14-10 70% 90% Sharpen patterns
    9-7 50% 95% Match sim, routine
    6-4 30% 100% (intent) Visualization, QE
    3-2 20% Match warmup Sleep, nutrition
    1 10% Light hit Routine, trust
    Match Warmup only MATCH EXECUTE

    Individualization Factors¶

    Factor Adjustment
    Age >30 More recovery, more strength maintenance
    Injury history Extra deloads, specific prehab
    Schedule density Compress blocks, prioritize sleep
    Surface change 2-week specific prep (clay→grass→hard)
    Travel/time zones Shift routine, light exposure, hydration

    Monitoring Dashboard (Weekly)¶

    Metric Target Action if Off
    HRV (morning) Stable/increasing ↓ Volume, ↑ Recovery
    Sleep (hrs + quality) 8+ hrs, >85% Nap, hygiene, ↓ evening screen
    RPE (session) Matches plan Adjust next session
    Jump height (CMJ) Stable/increasing ↓ Plyo volume
    QE % (practice) >85% Extra vision drills
    Mood/motivation Positive Mental reset, fun session

    The Periodization Mantra¶

    "Build the engine off-season. Sharpen the blade pre-season. Trust the swing in-season."
