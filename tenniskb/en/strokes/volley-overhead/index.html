    Volley & Overhead¶

    Short. Firm. Redirect.

    Volley Philosophy¶

    No swing. The volley is a block with a pulse. Racket stays in front. Face adjusts. Front foot steps and brakes.

    The Volley CORE Map¶

    Variable Volley Implementation
    Q Eyes on opponent's contact → instant jump to YOUR contact zone
    V Head perfectly still. Chin tucked. No head movement = face stable.
    ROOT Split step lands → immediate step forward. Front foot brakes.
    8 Zero 8. Racket doesn't go back. Stays in front.
    45° Face angle = target. Slight open for drop, slight close for drive.
    BRAKE Front foot lands = brake. Torso stops instantly.
    PULSE 1-inch firm push. No follow-through.
    JIN Ting feels ball on strings. Hua absorbs pace. Fa = firm pulse.

    Ready Position¶

    Element Detail
    Feet Wide, athletic, tripods active. Weight slightly forward.
    Hands Continental grip. Racket at chest height, in front. Throat supported by off-hand.
    Eyes On opponent. Soft focus.
    Head Level, chin slightly tucked.

    The Sequence¶

    Phase Action Timing
    1. Split Small hop, land wide, both tripods At opponent's contact
    2. Read See direction/spin in 50ms Ball leaving opponent
    3. Turn Shoulders turn, racket stays in front Instant
    4. Step Front foot steps TOWARD ball Before ball crosses net
    5. Face Adjust face for target/spin During step
    6. Meet Catch ball in front, firm wrist Contact
    7. Brake Front foot stomps, torso stops At contact
    8. Pulse 1-inch firm push through 30ms post-contact
    9. Hold Freeze, eyes on contact spot Until ball crosses net

    Forehand vs Backhand Volley¶

    Aspect Forehand Volley Backhand Volley
    Turn Shoulders turn, racket stays front Shoulders turn more, racket stays front
    Step Front foot (left) steps forward Front foot (right) steps forward
    Face Palm behind, pushes through Knuckles behind, pushes through
    Pulse Palm pushes 1" Knuckles push 1"

    Overhead Philosophy¶

    The overhead is a serve you don't control the toss for. Same chain. Move feet to the ball.

    The Overhead CORE Map¶

    Variable Overhead Implementation
    Q Eyes track ball up, park at contact zone
    V Head up, still. Chin tucked. No looking down early.
    ROOT Move feet to get BEHIND ball. Front foot brakes.
    8 Small 8 like serve. Racket up early.
    45° Same glass plane. Push up & through.
    BRAKE Front leg stops. Chest brakes.
    PULSE Palm pushes 1" through.
    JIN Ting tracks ball. Hua adjusts to bad toss. Fa = pulse.

    Overhead Sequence¶

    Phase Action
    1. Track Eyes on ball from opponent's contact
    2. Move Side shuffle / crossover to get BEHIND ball
    3. Load Racket up early, trophy position, back foot loaded
    4. Park Eyes jump to contact zone as ball peaks
    5. Jump/Drive Back leg drives up, front leg braces
    6. Brake Front leg/hip stops, chest brakes
    7. Pulse Palm pushes through 1"
    8. Land Front foot lands, recover instantly

    Common Errors¶

    Error Volley Fix Overhead Fix
    Swinging "No takeback. Catch and pulse." "Small 8. Racket up early."
    Late "Step forward earlier. Meet in front." "Get BEHIND ball sooner."
    Face opens "Firm wrist. Face set before contact." "Chin tucked. Don't look down."
    No power "Front foot brakes. Pulse firm." "Back leg drives. Front brakes."
    Shanks "Eyes parked. Head still." "Track with eyes. Head up."

    Quick-Scan Cards¶

    Volley¶

    SPLIT → READ → TURN → STEP → FACE → MEET → STOMP → PULSE → HOLD

    Overhead¶

    TRACK → MOVE → LOAD → PARK → DRIVE → BRAKE → PULSE → LAND

    Checklists¶

    Volley¶

    • Split step at opponent contact, wide base
    • Q: eyes jump to your contact zone instantly
    • V: head frozen, chin tucked
    • Turn: shoulders turn, racket STAYS IN FRONT
    • Step: front foot moves toward ball
    • Face: adjusted for target before ball arrives
    • Meet: catch in front, firm wrist
    • Brake: front foot stomps, torso stops
    • Pulse: 1-inch firm push
    • Hold: freeze, eyes on spot

    Overhead¶

    • Track ball from opponent's contact
    • Move feet to get BEHIND ball (side shuffle)
    • Load: trophy position early, back foot loaded
    • Park: eyes to contact zone at ball peak
    • Drive: back leg pushes up
    • Brake: front leg/hip stops, chest brakes
    • Pulse: palm pushes 1" through
    • Land: balanced, recover instantly
