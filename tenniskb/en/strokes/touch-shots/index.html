    Touch Shots: Slice, Drop Shot, Lob¶

    Hua Jin Mastery — Absorb & Redirect with Finesse.

    Touch Philosophy¶

    Touch isn't "soft hands." It's precise face control at low speed. The same CORE chain applies — just smaller 8, softer brake, gentler pulse.

    The Touch CORE Map¶

    Variable Touch Implementation
    Q Eyes park at contact zone. Track ball longer on slow shots.
    V Head still. No peeking at target.
    ROOT Light feet. Hư (empty) ready to become Thực (full).
    8 Tiny 8. Grapefruit-sized. Continuous, no pause.
    45° Face angle IS the shot. Open for slice/lob, neutral for drop.
    BRAKE Gentle brake. Torso slows, doesn't stop hard.
    PULSE Feather pulse. 1-inch guide, not push.
    JIN Hua dominant. Absorb pace, redirect with precision. Ting feels face.

    Slice (Backhand & Forehand)¶

    The Principle¶

    Cut under the ball. Face open. Path high-to-low. Racket stays on 45° glass.

    Backhand Slice (Saber Family)¶

    • Grip: Continental, slight eastern backhand
    • Load: High takeback, edge leading, non-dominant hand pulls
    • Park: Eyes to contact zone (higher, further side)
    • Execute: Chest stays side-on. Lat pulls elbow down. Edge leads. Face opens through contact.
    • Brake: Chest brakes side-on. Front foot roots.
    • Pulse: Edge guides down-through. 1-inch pulse.
    • Finish: High, elbow up, chest still side-on.

    Forehand Slice (Push Family)¶

    • Grip: Continental / slight eastern forehand
    • Load: Racket up, face open, chest coiled
    • Execute: Chest fires, palm guides under ball. Face stays open.
    • Brake: Front leg brakes gently.
    • Pulse: Palm guides under 1 inch.

    Fault Signals — Slice¶

    Symptom Cause Fix
    Floats long Face too open / no brake Close face 5°, brake torso
    Into net Contact late / face closed Meet earlier, hold face open
    No bite Wrist floppy / no edge lead Firm wrist, edge leads
    Pops up Tried to "hit" not guide Softer brake, guide pulse

    Drop Shot¶

    The Principle¶

    Disguise = same setup as drive. Tiny 8. Face open ~30°. Feather pulse. Ball dies at net.

    The Disguise¶

    • Identical ready, load, park as your drive
    • Only difference: 8 size (tiny), brake (gentle), pulse (feather)
    • Contact point: Slightly further out front than drive

    Execution¶

    Phase Detail
    Load Same coil as drive. Opponent reads "drive."
    Park Same. Eyes locked.
    8 Grapefruit-sized. Continuous.
    Face Opens to ~30° at last instant (wrist lays back slightly).
    Brake Torso slows gently. No stomp.
    Pulse Feather — fingers guide, don't push.
    Spin Backspin + sidespin = dies + curves away.

    The Rule¶

    If you can hit a winner, don't drop shot. Drop shot is for when they're back and you're in.

    Fault Signals — Drop Shot¶

    Symptom Cause Fix
    Pops up Face not open enough / pulse too firm Open face 30°, feather fingers
    Into net Contact too low / late Meet further front, higher
    No disguise Takeback different from drive Identical load & park
    Too short Too much backspin, no forward Tiny forward pulse component

    Lob¶

    The Principle¶

    Offensive lob = drive mechanics + open face + upward 45° path. Defensive lob = survival — just get it deep.

    Offensive Lob (Attack)¶

    • When: Opponent at net, you have time
    • Load: Same as drive
    • Face: Opens to 45° at contact
    • Path: 45° glass — low to high
    • Pulse: Firm 1-inch push UP through
    • Target: Deep to baseline, over backhand side

    Defensive Lob (Survival)¶

    • When: Stretched wide, on run, late
    • Face: Open 60-70°
    • Path: High arc
    • Pulse: Minimal — just get it up
    • Target: Deep, middle, buy time

    Fault Signals — Lob¶

    Symptom Cause Fix
    Short (smashed) Not enough height/depth Open face more, push up harder
    Long Face too open / too much pulse Close face 10°, softer pulse
    No time Recognized late Read opponent position earlier

    Quick-Scan Cards¶

    Slice¶

    SAME LOAD → PARK → EDGE LEADS → FACE OPEN → GENTLE BRAKE → GUIDE PULSE

    Drop Shot¶

    IDENTICAL DRIVE SETUP → TINY 8 → FACE OPENS LATE → FEATHER PULSE → DIE AT NET

    Offensive Lob¶

    DRIVE LOAD → FACE 45° → 45° PATH UP → FIRM UP PULSE → DEEP BASELINE

    Checklists¶

    Slice¶

    • Grip: continental
    • Load: high, edge leading (BH) / face open (FH)
    • Park: eyes to zone
    • Execute: edge leads (BH) / palm guides under (FH)
    • Brake: chest side-on (BH) / front leg (FH)
    • Pulse: 1-inch edge/palm guide
    • Finish: high, controlled

    Drop Shot¶

    • Disguise: identical drive setup
    • 8: tiny, continuous
    • Face: opens to 30° at last instant
    • Brake: gentle torso slow
    • Pulse: feather fingers
    • Target: just over net, dies

    Lob¶

    • Type chosen: offensive vs defensive
    • Face: 45° (offensive) / 60-70° (defensive)
    • Path: 45° glass up (offensive) / high arc (defensive)
    • Pulse: firm up (offensive) / minimal (defensive)
    • Target: deep baseline / deep middle
