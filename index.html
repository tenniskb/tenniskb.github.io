Welcome to Tennis Knowledge Base¶

This is a bilingual tennis resource — English and Vietnamese — built from the ground up for players who want more than just tips and drills. Here you'll find the why behind the techniques, the neural control theory that explains how movement actually works, and the principles that transfer across strokes.

Chào Mừng Đến Tennis Knowledge Base¶

Đây là nguồn tài liệu tennis song ngữ — Tiếng Anh và Tiếng Việt — được xây dựng từ đầu dành cho những người chơi muốn tìm hiểu sâu hơn những mẹo nhỏ và bài tập rời rạc. Tại đây bạn sẽ tìm thấy lý do tại sao đằng sau các kỹ thuật, lý thuyết điều khiển thần kinh giải thích cách chuyển động thực sự hoạt động, và các nguyên lý có thể áp dụng xuyên suốt mọi cú đánh.

How to Use This Site¶

Everything is reachable in 3 clicks or less:

  1. Click your language → English or Vietnamese
  2. Choose your section → Foundation, Advanced, Elite, Science Lab, Players, System, or Handbook
  3. Pick a topic → Drills, deep dives, or specific techniques

No dead ends. No mysterious folders. Just clear paths to what you need.

Site Structure¶

  • Foundation — strokes, grips, footwork, doubles patterns, and the core coaching guides.
  • Advanced — tactical patterns, scouting, in-match adaptation, and the neurological/biomechanical deep-dives underneath advanced play.
  • Elite — for players who've outgrown copying pro technique and are building a personalized system.
  • Science Lab — anatomy and joint-by-joint biomechanics, independent of playing level.
  • Players and System — supporting reference sections.
  • United Tennis Handbook — a complete 30-chapter unified system (Q-V-ROOT-8-45-Impulse-Jin) connecting grip, footwork, vision, and biomechanics into one working model, from first principles to match play. 🇻🇳 Tiếng Việt

About This Site¶

This knowledge base grew out of frustration with surface-level tennis instruction. Most coaching tells you what to do — "get under the ball," "hit through it," "use your legs" — but never explains why or how your nervous system actually learns these patterns.

Everything here is grounded in:

  • Raw coaching materials — no AI-generated fluff
  • Bilingual by design — not translated, but written natively in both languages
  • Neural control theory — understanding movement as the brain learns it
  • Practical application — drills and progressions you can use today

For educational purposes only. Not a substitute for professional coaching.
