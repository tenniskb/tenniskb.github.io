Tennis Knowledge Base — Your Complete Guide to Playing Better¶

Introduction¶

Welcome to the Tennis Knowledge Base — a comprehensive, bilingual library of practical tennis coaching grounded in real biomechanics and years of on-court experience. Whether you're a 3.5 player building fundamentals or a 5.0+ player refining elite mechanics, you'll find honest, detailed writing about how to play tennis and why the techniques actually work.

Thisn't AI-generated content or quick-fix advice. Every article is written from genuine coaching experience, focused on what your body needs to solve — not what a rigid stroke model demands.

Browse by Level¶

If you prefer to browse by skill level instead of topic:

Foundation → — Grip, stance, footwork, the 6 major strokes, doubles patterns, and mental fundamentals. Start here if you're building from scratch or solidifying basics.

Advanced → — Biomechanics deep-dives: embodied cognition, fascia, proprioception, reflex arcs, the kinetic chain, and X-factor anatomy. For players who want to understand why their body moves the way it does.

Elite → — Advanced frameworks: constraint-led coaching, the dream library decision system, anti-orthodox thinking, and state work (Kinh & Mushin). For players with years of practice ready for the subtle layers.
