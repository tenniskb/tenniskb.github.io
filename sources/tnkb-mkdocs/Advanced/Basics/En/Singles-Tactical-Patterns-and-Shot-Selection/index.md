# Singles Tactical Patterns & Shot Selection

*The doubles side of this site has patterns and formations. Singles deserves the same treatment.*

## Why This Matters

You can own great technique and still lose to a player with worse strokes but a clearer game
plan. Singles tennis rewards players who know which of four "phases of play" they're in on any
given ball, and who have 2-3 go-to patterns for each phase, rather than reinventing a plan point
by point.

## Core Concepts

**The four phases of play.**

- *Attack* — you have a short, high, or weak ball; the point is yours to finish.
- *Build* — a neutral rally ball; your job is to construct advantage, not force a winner.
- *Neutral* — both players are in position; patience and consistency dictate.
- *Defense* — you're out of position or under pressure; the only goal is to survive the point
  and reset to neutral.

Most rec-level errors come from playing an "attack" shot selection while actually still in
"build" or "neutral" — going for a winner too early because the ball felt hittable, not because
the phase justified it.

**Repeatable shot patterns**, not just isolated great shots. A pattern is a 2-3 shot sequence you
can execute on command: for example, deep crosscourt forehand → short ball response →
inside-out forehand winner. Recreational players who improve fastest tend to drill 3-4 patterns
until they're automatic, rather than trying to have unlimited shot options.

**Scouting-informed game plans.** Before or early in a match, identify one clear weakness (weak
second serve, poor movement to the ad-side backhand corner, discomfort at net) and build your
shot selection around exploiting it consistently, rather than playing "your game" regardless of
opponent.

## Action Steps

1. Before your next match, write down (mentally or literally) 3 patterns you'll try to execute —
   not just "hit deep" but the full 2-3 shot sequence.
2. During warm-up, identify your opponent's weaker wing and commit to directing 60%+ of rally
   balls there in neutral/build phases.
3. Practice phase recognition in drilling: have a partner or coach call out "attack / build /
   neutral / defense" mid-rally and match your shot selection to the call, not to how the ball
   feels.

## Common Mistakes

- Going for winners from neutral or defensive positions because the shot "felt good," not
  because the phase called for it.
- Playing the same pattern regardless of what the scouting/warm-up revealed about the opponent.
- Treating singles tactics as "just hit it where they're not," with no repeatable sequence behind
  it.

## Quick Reference

| Phase | Ball You Got | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Attack | Short/high/weak | Finish the point |
| Build | Neutral, slight advantage possible | Construct, don't force |
| Neutral | Even rally ball | Consistency, patience |
| Defense | Under pressure/out of position | Survive, reset |
