# Slice Cheat Sheet — Your Tennis Bag Companion

## The Fundamentals

However you're using the slice — low ball, return, approach, defense — these five things stay constant:

- **Grip:** Continental, V on bevel #2.
- **Face:** Open like a hood, 30-45°.
- **Path:** High to low (or flat-to-forward on a low ball).
- **Sound:** Listen for "fwoosh" — that's clean underspin. A "thwack" means you hit flat.
- **Grip pressure:** Soft hands, 3-4 out of 10. Firm contact, relaxed grip — that's the paradox that makes it work.

## Know Your Situation

| Situation | Your move | The cue |
|---|---|---|
| **Low ball** | Scoop & slide | Chest down to the ball — bend the knees, not the back |
| **Return of serve** | Block & redirect | No swing — use their pace, not yours |
| **Approach shot** | Slice low, advance | Cut it deep, split-step, and move forward |
| **Defense** | Just get it deep | You're buying time — don't aim for the lines |

## Your Safety Checklist

- **Protect your back.** Bend the knees for low balls. Never the back.
- **Protect your shoulder.** If it's feeling tight, lean on your two-hander for power and save the slice for everything else.
- **Control the lunge.** Small, controlled steps beat a sudden lunge every time — that's what strains the knees.
- **Keep the grip soft.** A tight grip is how you turn ball impact into injury. Relax your hand and let it absorb the pace instead of fighting it.

---

*The slice is your insurance policy. When in doubt, slice it deep and breathe.*
