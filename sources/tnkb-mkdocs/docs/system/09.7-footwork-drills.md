# Footwork Drills — The Dynamic Foundation

Five drills built around [Footwork Stances](footwork-stances.md) — training the body to manage its center of mass instead of just running, using the [GSM (Gravitational Boost)](04.1-gsm-gravitational-boost.md) and [DSI (Dynamic Stability Index)](04.2-dsi-dynamic-stability-index.md) engines directly.

## Drill 1: Sinking Split-Step

**Goal**: optimize $S_{gravity}$.

**How**: hop lightly. As you land, sink the hips 5-10cm lower than usual, then accelerate sideways immediately.

**Feel**: the ground should feel like it's "pushing" you into the first step, not the other way around.

## Drill 2: Axis-Preservation Slide

**Goal**: hold $DSI \geq 0.85$ through a slide.

**How**: balance a tennis ball on top of your head. Slide side to side to retrieve balls. If the ball falls off, your tilt angle $\Delta\theta$ went too far and your DSI collapsed.

## Drill 3: V-Step Transition

**Goal**: optimize the interception point.

**How**: from center court, step 45 degrees forward, make contact, then cross back to recover. Repeat 20 times per side.

## Drill 4: Distributed Braking

**Goal**: avoid a single hard brake.

**How**: sprint back toward the baseline. Before you arrive, lower your center of gravity and take several small decelerating steps rather than one big stopping stride. A single hard brake kills your ability to move again immediately; distributed braking doesn't.

## Drill 5: Elastic Movement (Rubber Band)

**Goal**: train tensegrity through the whole chain.

**How**: treat the legs and upper body as a single connected unit, like a stretched rubber band. Feel the centrifugal force link continuously from feet, through hips, to shoulders — never as separate, disconnected pieces.
