# The 4-Month Roadmap — From 3.0 to 3.5

A four-month periodization plan built around a different lens than the [12-Week Master Plan](10.2-12-week-master-plan.md): instead of sequencing through the engine stack (DET → NKF → DIT), it moves a player through four phases of a broader framework — foundation and awareness, perceptual reading, internal power generation, and footwork/tactical integration. Where the 12-week plan is the engine-focused route, this is the awareness-and-movement-focused route; the two can run side by side or be used as alternate templates depending on what a player needs most.

## Month 1 — Foundation and Vision

**Goal:** build the awareness base and internal strength that everything else sits on.

Weeks 1-2 center on diagnosing your own movement patterns and activating the body's center of gravity — daily work includes 5 minutes of centered breathing, 10 minutes of standing meditation (a stillness/posture hold), and 5 minutes of self-diagnosing your own movement tendencies.

Weeks 3-4 shift from unconscious, habitual practice into deliberate practice, and introduce the broader RPM framework this roadmap is built on.

**Daily practice (20 minutes):** 5 min centered breathing, 10 min standing meditation, 5 min movement self-diagnosis.

## Month 2 — Perception

**Goal:** develop the ability to read the ball and the opponent.

Weeks 5-6 combine soft-focus ball tracking with [split step](split-step.md) timing work. Weeks 7-8 move into reading opponent intent and managing cognitive load under pressure.

**Daily practice (30 minutes):** 10 min focus/two-point-focus drills, 10 min rhythm split-step work, 10 min "no-ball" cue-reading practice.

## Month 3 — Internal Power

**Goal:** build the ground-force foundation and spiral movement patterns that generate power.

Weeks 9-10 focus on using ground reaction force and sequencing it through the body (see [Ground Reaction Force](ground-reaction-force.md) and [Kinetic Chain](kinetic-chain.md)). Weeks 11-12 move into spiral rotation and releasing acceleration through a relaxed wrist (see [Wrist Lag and Release](wrist-lag-and-release.md)).

**Daily practice (30 minutes):** 10 min plyometric push-offs, 10 min medicine-ball slams, 10 min drop-and-catch work for a loose wrist.

## Month 4 — Movement and Integration

**Goal:** complete the footwork system and bring it together with tactics under match conditions.

Weeks 13-14 build the overall movement framework and crossover-step footwork. Weeks 15-16 cover stance selection (open vs. closed), the run-around forehand, and defending a deep ball. Week 17 covers winning patterns and cognitive-load management in competition. Week 18 is dedicated to building a long-term self-coaching system (see [Self-Coaching Discipline](self-coaching-discipline.md)).

## End-of-Program Benchmarks

After four months, a player following this roadmap should be able to check off:

- A stable, reliably activated center of gravity
- An automatic split step with correct timing
- Smooth ball tracking, reading 60-70% of available cues
- A forehand with visible spiral rotation and kinetic-chain sequencing
- Natural crossover-step footwork and recovery
- Situational judgment on open vs. closed stance
- Three practiced, repeatable winning patterns
- A stable pre-performance routine
