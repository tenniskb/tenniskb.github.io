# 10-Minute Volley Drill (Redirect)

A short, focused routine built around [Volley: Redirect Instead of Swing](02.5-volley-redirect.md) — the entire point is to redirect the ball's own pace rather than swing at it.

## 1. Off-Court Feel (2 minutes)

Hold the racket in a continental grip. Deliberately loosen your hold until you can wiggle the handle 2-3mm inside the palm, using only the thumb and index finger. Tap the string bed against your other palm — you should hear a dull thud, not a sharp clack.

## 2. Wall Volleys From 6 Feet (4 minutes)

Stand 6 feet from a wall. No backswing — just set the face. Say "redirect" out loud as the ball hits, 20 balls in a row.

**Goal**: the ball dies at your feet with backspin. If that happens, you're doing it correctly.

## 3. Slice Practice (2 minutes)

Same setup as the wall volleys, but cut slightly down and through — high to low, about 6 inches of travel — to create backspin that keeps the ball low after the bounce.

**Cues**: "take the pace away." "Soft hand, firm wrist, slightly open face, short slice."

## 4. Cradle Volley (2 minutes)

At the net, with no backswing: press on the handle with the off-hand, bring the shoulders in, cradle the racket close to the torso, elbows out. Pull back only 6 inches — the impulse you need is already there. This is the same shape covered in [Tensegrity and the Cradle](02.1-tensegrity-cradle.md).

**Check**: you shouldn't feel like you're "punching" the volley. The racket face stays stable because the whole upper body tenses as one piece.

## Total: 10 Minutes

| Segment | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Off-court feel | 2 min |
| Wall volleys | 4 min |
| Slice practice | 2 min |
| Cradle | 2 min |

## Common Traps

- **Squeezing on contact** — kills feel, the ball pops up.
- **Swinging from the shoulder** — breaks timing, misses the sweet spot.
- **Palm fully glued to the grip** — loses the mini-pivot that gives the racket its feel.
