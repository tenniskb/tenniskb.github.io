# Final Checklist for the Player

A run-through checklist covering the numbers from [Constants and KPIs](08.2-constants-kpis.md), organized by when you'd actually use it — before the match, during it, per stroke, and after.

## Pre-Match Checklist

| Check | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Spine stays vertical during a slide? | DSI $\geq 0.85$ |
| Hips "sink" during the split-step? | GSM check — drop roughly 10% of standing height |
| Wrist locked at impact? | $K_{wrist} \geq 0.85$ |
| Hitting into the high-probability zone? | $P_{error} < 40\%$ |
| HRV in the green zone? | At or above baseline |
| Fascia warmed up? | Dynamic stretching completed |

## During-Match Checklist

**Every point:**

- Apply the 16-second neuro-reset
- Grip pressure at 3-4/10 (loose)
- Eyes on the ball through impact and 0.3 seconds beyond

**Every three points:**

- Heart rate above 180bpm? → Reset
- $\sigma_{sync}$ feeling shaky? → Slow the next point down

## Per-Stroke Checklist

### Forehand

- Hips leading the shoulders? (the X-factor)
- Wrist neutral, not dropped?
- Butt cap pointing toward the ball on the takeback?
- Weight transferring forward, back foot dragging through?

### Backhand

- "See the watch"? (palm facing down)
- Coiled before the ball bounces?
- Thrusting through the ball, not just meeting it?

### Serve

- GRF at 2.3-2.5x body weight at the trophy pose?
- Knee bend at 100-120 degrees?
- Shoulders still turned behind the hips at contact (X-factor)?
- Contact at the highest reachable point?

### Return

- Split-step timed correctly?
- Backswing 20cm or less?
- $K_{wrist} \geq 0.95$?
- $\Delta t \leq 0.15s$?

### Volley

- V-step at 45 degrees?
- Racket staying in front of the shoulder, never behind it?
- $K_{wrist} \geq 0.95$?
- Weight loaded forward?

## Post-Match Checklist

- HRV logged?
- Video uploaded for AI analysis?
- Unforced errors categorized?
- Tomorrow's training zone set (green/yellow/red)?
