# GSM (Gravitational Boost) — Borrowing the Earth's Pull

Stop pushing with muscle; start falling into your movements. The split-step is a **gravitational drop** - by lowering your center of gravity, you receive free energy that you then convert into an explosive first step sideways.

## The Formula for Borrowing Gravity

$$S_{gravity} = 1 + \alpha \left( \frac{h_{player}}{h_{drop}} \right)$$

| Variable | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| $h_{drop}$ | How far the center of gravity falls during the split-step |
| $h_{player}$ | Body height |
| $\alpha$ | Conversion coefficient (how well the leg fascia stores and releases energy) |

**The takeaway:** hop up and let yourself drop deep, and you can gain 15-20% more speed on your first step - without spending any extra effort.

## The Stretch-Shortening Cycle (SSC)

As the center of gravity sinks, the Achilles tendon and thigh fascia stretch and store potential energy. The moment you push off the ground, that energy releases instantly. This is why elite players look like they're gliding across the court rather than running.

## The Sinking Philosophy

| Mistake | Correct |
| --- | --- |
| Rising onto the toes, pushing with the thigh muscles | Letting the center of gravity "drop," borrowing gravity |
| Fatigues muscles fast, loses balance | Sustainable, low effort |
