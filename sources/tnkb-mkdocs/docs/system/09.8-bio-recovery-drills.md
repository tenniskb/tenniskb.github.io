# Bio-Recovery Drills — Biological Recovery

Practical protocols supporting [Bio-Agentic Recovery](05.2-bio-agentic-recovery.md) and the [Bio-Engine Deep Dive](06.09-bio-engine-deep-dive.md) — training the nervous system and the fascia to recover as deliberately as you train the swing itself.

## Daily Protocol: 16-Second Neuro-Reset

**Goal**: reset $\sigma_{sync}$ between points.

| Window | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 0-4s | Look at the strings |
| 4-12s | Inhale 4s, exhale 8s |
| 12-16s | Visualize the next shot |

## Drill 1: Plyometrics ("Green" HRV Days)

**Goal**: build the stretch-shortening cycle and fascial thickness.

**How**: box jumps, depth jumps, lateral bounds. 3-4 sets of 6-8 reps.

## Drill 2: Dynamic Stretching

**Goal**: wake the fascial system up — not static holding.

**How**: leg swings, arm circles, hip openers, thoracic rotations.

## Drill 3: Fascial Self-Massage

**Goal**: keep the fascia elastic.

**Tools**: foam roller, lacrosse ball.

**Target areas**: IT band, glutes, thoracic spine, plantar fascia.

## Drill 4: Vagus Nerve Activation

**Goal**: bring the heart rate down fast.

**How**: inhale for 4 seconds, exhale for 8 (the exhale should always be longer than the inhale). Repeat for 4-6 cycles. This activates the vagus nerve and resets $\sigma_{sync}$.

## Drill 5: HRV-Based Weekly Cycle

| HRV Zone | Training Type |
| --- | --- |
| Green | Explosive (sprints, plyometrics, [VE](09.4-ve-drills.md)) |
| Yellow | Technical (shadow swings, AI review) |
| Red | Recovery (yoga, fascia work) |

## Drill 6: Hydration and Electrolytes

**How**: don't over-rely on plain water. Prioritize magnesium, potassium, and sodium electrolytes, plus collagen and vitamin C 30-60 minutes before training.
