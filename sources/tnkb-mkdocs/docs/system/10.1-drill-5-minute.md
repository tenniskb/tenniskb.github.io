# 5-Minute Shadow Drill Routine

A compressed daily routine for cleaning up the forehand takeback — six short pieces that add up to five minutes, meant to be done every day rather than occasionally. It builds directly on the fixes flagged in [My Forehand — Personal Analysis](08.1-my-forehand-analysis.md) and [Drop Wrist vs. Correct Takeback](08.2-drop-wrist-vs-correct-takeback.md).

## 1. Shadow 5:30 (2 minutes — 30 seconds per side, 10 reps)

Stand in front of a mirror. Turn the shoulders, take the racket back, and stop to check the position:

- Is the racket head higher than the wrist?
- Does the palm face slightly down toward the ground?

If both check out, that's the correct takeback.

**Cue**: "tuck it in your back pocket" — imagine pulling the butt cap of the racket toward your back right pocket.

## 2. Towel Drill (1 minute)

Tuck a small towel under the hitting-arm armpit and hit shadow swings. If the towel drops early, you're using the arm in isolation. If it drops right at the follow-through, you're rotating the chest and pulling the arm along with it — that's correct. See [Pre-Compensation](01.3-racquet-face-pre-compensation.md) for the racket-face side of this same fix.

## 3. Loose Wrist Practice (30 seconds)

Hold the racket with three fingers only (thumb, index, middle), letting the other two rest loose. Hit softly into a wall, focusing only on keeping the wrist laid back. If the racket rattles and bounces off naturally, you're doing it right. If you have to grip hard to control it, you're tensing up.

## 4. Three-Finger Grip at 70% (1 minute)

Rally at 70% pace. Focus on the sound of the feet driving into the ground and the hips rotating — you shouldn't hear any "strain" sound coming from the arm. Grip pressure should sit around 3 out of 10.

## 5. "Navel Pull" Recovery Drill (30 seconds)

Serve a light ball out wide. Land on the left foot and hold still for half a second. Use only the lower abdomen to pull the navel back toward the left post of the court — no leg involvement. If the hips rotate back into position on their own, you're using the dantian correctly.

## 6. Three-Point Freeze (30 seconds)

1. Drive the legs, stop — check that the front knee is extending.
2. Rotate the hips, stop — hips should face the net while the shoulders stay closed.
3. Contact.

Gradually link the three checkpoints into one smooth motion.

## Total: 5 Minutes

| Segment | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Shadow 5:30 | 2 min |
| Towel drill | 1 min |
| Loose wrist | 30s |
| 70% rally | 1 min |
| Navel-pull recovery | 30s |
