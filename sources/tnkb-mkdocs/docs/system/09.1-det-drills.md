# DET Drills — Energy Transfer

Four drills for training the [DET (Dynamic Energy Transfer)](02.1-det-dynamic-energy-transfer.md) engine directly — the goal in each one is to feel force arriving from the ground rather than being generated by the arm.

---

## Drill 1: Feel the Ground Reaction Force

**Goal**: connect the brain to ground force.

**How**: stand still and give a small bounce. Feel the counter-push traveling from the heels up to the crown of the head. Then hit balls slowly, focusing on "pushing" into the ground before the arm ever swings. See [Ground Reaction Force](ground-reaction-force.md) for the underlying mechanism this drill is training.

## Drill 2: Stretch the Cross-Body Fascia

**Goal**: increase the spring coefficient $k$ and the stretch distance $\Delta x$ in the body's elastic system.

**How**: rotate the upper body while keeping the hips fixed. Feel the tension build along the ribs and chest — that tension is the fascia storing energy.

## Drill 3: Overload Wrist Locking

**Goal**: reach $K_{wrist} \geq 0.85$.

**How**: use a racket with added swingweight (lead tape on the head). Hit balls against a wall at close range, focusing entirely on keeping the racket face from rattling or twisting at contact.

## Drill 4: The Bow Technique

**Goal**: build the "bow" shape used in the serve and forehand.

**How**: jump upward and let the body arch slightly, like a bent bow. Feel the extra elastic tension this bend creates through the trunk.
