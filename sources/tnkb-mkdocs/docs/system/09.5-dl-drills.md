# DL Drills — Direct Load Return

Four drills for training the [DL (Direct Load) Return](03.2-dl-direct-load-return.md) engine — cutting the backswing out of the equation entirely and loading the body directly off the incoming ball.

## Drill 1: Wall-Compression

**Goal**: eliminate the backswing.

**How**: stand close to a wall. Have a partner throw the ball hard against it. Split-step and block the rebound as it comes back at you. The racket must **never** travel back past the shoulder.

## Drill 2: Gravity-Step Sprint

**Goal**: optimize $J_{load}$, the loading impulse borrowed from the split-step's drop.

**How**: split-step, feel your center of mass "fall" as low as it can go, then accelerate sideways for 2 meters. This is the same gravitational-drop mechanism covered in [GSM](04.1-gsm-gravitational-boost.md) — you're borrowing the fall instead of pushing with muscle.

## Drill 3: High-Impact Locking

**Goal**: hold $K_{wrist} \geq 0.95$.

**How**: face a ball machine firing at 120 km/h or faster. Focus entirely on keeping the racket face still on contact — don't let the wrist shake or twist.

## Drill 4: DL Shadow (No Ball)

**Goal**: internalize the split-step and torso-compression pattern without a ball to react to.

**How**:

1. Stand in the ready position.
2. Split-step low.
3. Rotate the shoulders 30-45 degrees and load the hips.
4. Take a short swing — no backswing at all.
