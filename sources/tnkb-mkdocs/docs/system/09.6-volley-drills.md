# Volley Drills — The Reflex Wall

Five drills built around the [Volley: Redirect Instead of Swing](02.5-volley-redirect.md) philosophy — the racket face acts as a wall, not a bat, and these drills train the hands and feet to hold that wall steady under increasing pressure.

## Drill 1: Rapid Fire

**Goal**: hold $K_{wrist} \geq 0.95$ while reacting fast.

**How**: stand 2 meters from the net. Have a partner feed balls continuously to different spots. Punch volleys only, short and compact — absolutely no backswing.

## Drill 2: Target Precision

**Goal**: master the reflex angle.

**How**: set up four targets, one in each corner of the court. Volley for precision against each one.

**KPI**: better than 70% of volleys land on target.

## Drill 3: V-Step Transition

**Goal**: optimize the interception point.

**How**: ready position, then a 45-degree V-step, then contact, then recover. Repeat until the footwork and the swing timing feel like a single unit rather than two separate actions.

## Drill 4: Punch vs. Drop Decision

**Goal**: train fast switching of $K_{wrist}$.

**How**: alternate rapidly between two calls — "Punch" (lock the wrist, $K \geq 0.95$) and "Drop" (deliberately soften the wrist to kill pace). This is the same touch-vs-firm distinction covered in the [Tensegrity and the Cradle](02.1-tensegrity-cradle.md) page.

## Drill 5: Live Volley Points

**Goal**: hold the pattern under real pressure.

**How**: play out a live point ending at the net. Have AI analysis review your V-step timing and $K_{wrist}$ afterward.
