# 12-Week Master Plan — The Training Roadmap

A three-phase periodization plan that walks a player through the engine stack in order: feel the ground force first, synchronize the chain second, then tune the racket's inertia and the tactical layer last. Pairs with the [Final Checklist for the Player](10.1-player-checklist.md) and the thresholds in [Constants and KPIs](08.2-constants-kpis.md).

## The Three Phases

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Key Metric | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1-4 | Energy Flow ([DET](02.1-det-dynamic-energy-transfer.md)) | GRF | Feel ground force transfer through the core |
| 2 | 5-8 | Synchronization ([NKF](02.2-nkf-neuro-kinetic-fusion.md)) | $\sigma_{sync}$ | Master the sequence: feet → hips → shoulders |
| 3 | 9-12 | Inertia and Meta ([DIT](02.3-dit-dynamic-inertia-transfer.md)) | $P_{error}$ | Optimize racket weight and shot selection |

## Phase 1: Energy Flow (DET) — Weeks 1-4

**Week 1 — Feel the GRF.** Ground-force awareness work, foam rolling, dynamic stretching, shadow swings focused on "pushing into the ground."

**Week 2 — Basic loading.** Bend the knees to 100-120 degrees at the trophy pose, GRF jump drills, slow forehands focused on energy flow.

**Week 3 — Elastic fascia.** Light plyometrics, fascial stretching, forehands with a clearly felt pre-stretch.

**Week 4 — Integrate DET.** Forehands at 70% speed with DET as the sole focus, AI video review.

**KPI**: GRF clearly felt on 70% of strokes.

## Phase 2: Synchronization (NKF) — Weeks 5-8

**Week 5 — Basic rhythm.** Metronome counting (1-2-3-4), breath-motion sync, footwork without a racket.

**Week 6 — 4-segment slow motion.** Feet → hips → shoulders → arm, broken apart, then gradually fused into one motion.

**Week 7 — Visualization plus live reps.** Visual-motor overlay, slow rallies focused on timing.

**Week 8 — Full sync test.** Full-speed rally, AI-measured $\sigma_{sync}$.

**KPI**: $\sigma_{sync} \geq 0.92$ on 80% of strokes.

## Phase 3: Inertia and Meta (DIT + Strategy) — Weeks 9-12

**Week 9 — Swingweight tuning.** Lead-tape experiments, shadow swings with the heavier racket, sweet-spot hunting.

**Week 10 — $P_{error}$ profiling.** Log where opponents break down, heatmap analysis, Smart Index drills.

**Week 11 — Tactical integration.** Apply $P_{error}$ patterns in live rallies, "squeeze" tactics, leverage-point awareness.

**Week 12 — Match simulation.** Full sets with AI analysis, measuring every KPI, full evaluation.
