# Constants and KPIs

The target numbers behind every formula in [Master Equations Reference](08.1-master-equations.md) — what "good" actually looks like, quantified.

---

## Master Thresholds

| Constant | Target value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| DET transfer rate | $\geq 92\%$ | Energy transfer efficiency |
| $\sigma_{sync}$ | $\geq 0.92$ | Neuro-kinetic synchronization |
| $K_{wrist}$ (baseline) | $\geq 0.85$ | General wrist stiffness |
| $K_{wrist}$ (volley / DL) | $\geq 0.95$ | High wrist stiffness for reflex shots |
| GRF (serve) | $2.3$–$2.5 \times BW$ | Ground reaction force |
| Knee flexion (loading) | $100°$–$120°$ | Optimal knee bend angle |
| DSI (safe) | $\geq 0.85$ | Dynamic stability index |
| $\omega_{IR}$ | $\approx 2{,}800°/s$ | Internal rotation angular velocity |
| $\alpha_{IR}$ | $\approx 112{,}000°/s^2$ | Internal rotation angular acceleration |
| $\Delta t_{IR}$ | $\approx 25\text{ms}$ | Internal rotation time window |
| $\Delta t$ (reaction) | $\leq 0.12\text{s}$ | Neural reaction time |
| $\Delta t$ (DL) | $\leq 0.15\text{s}$ | Split-step to contact |
| $\Delta t$ (dwell) | $\approx 4$–$5\text{ms}$ | Ball-on-string dwell time |

## HRV Thresholds

| Zone | HRV change | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Increased or at/above baseline | Explosive training (VE, DET work) |
| Yellow | −10% to −20% | Technical training only |
| Red | ≤ −20% | Recovery only (yoga, fascial work) |

## P_error Thresholds

| Zone | P_error | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Green | < 15% | Play freely |
| Yellow | 15–40% | Weigh the risk |
| Red | > 40% | Avoid — a "suicide shot" |

## Axis Tolerance (Body Tilt)

| Situation | Allowed $\Delta\theta$ |
|---|---|
| Normal hit | $\leq 5°$ (DSI ≥ 0.95) |
| Wide reach | $\leq 10°$ (DSI ≥ 0.85) |
| Emergency save | $\leq 20°$ (DSI ~0.40) |

## Volley Angle Tolerance

| Type | Target |
|---|---|
| Over-swing prevention | Racket does not travel back past the shoulder plane |
| V-step | 45° angled step |
| Face angle adjustment | 5°–10° |

## Equipment Targets (DIT / Lead Tape)

| Lead tape position | Primary effect |
|---|---|
| 12 o'clock (head) | Maximum swingweight, more leverage |
| 3 / 9 o'clock | Torsional stability |
| Handle | Balance shifted toward the hand |

## Grip Pressure by Shot (0–10 scale)

| Shot | Grip pressure |
|---|---|
| Serve | 6–7 (firm) |
| Forehand | 3–4 (loose) |
| Volley | 3–4 (loose) |
| Slice | 4–5 (medium) |
