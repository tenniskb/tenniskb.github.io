Kyrgios and the Underarm Serve Dropshot

By Chris Lewit · The Prodigy Maker (2020-05-10) · Original Article ↗

Facebook Video: https://www.facebook.com/17413858957/posts/10157507875798958?s=100001145192967&v=e&sfns=mo

Kyrgios has started a new trend in the pro game with underarm serving.

I am going to start teaching this shot to my junior players. It can come in handy, especially when the returner is playing very deep.

Basically it’s a drop shot. In my opinion, perfectly legitimate.

In addition, Kyrgios now faking the underarm serve and then blasting a power overarm serve—another ingenious variation.

I appreciate Kyrgios’ creative approach to the game even when I don’t always agree with his behavior.
