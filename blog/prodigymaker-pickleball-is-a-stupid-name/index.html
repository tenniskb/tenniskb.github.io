Pickleball is a Stupid Name

By Chris Lewit · The Prodigy Maker (2019-12-02) · Original Article ↗

Pickleball is a stupid name and the sound of the ball is annoying.

The game has ugly technique and very little movement, footwork, or athleticism.

Why is the game growing quickly and what is the future of the game?

Seniors who want a good fitness workout should play real tennis.

Young people who want an athletic sport should play real tennis.

The only mini tennis game that could potentially rival traditional tennis is Touch Tennis. That’s a cool mini tennis game.

But real tennis is still better—more athletic and exciting to play—and a better workout.

Agree or disagree?
