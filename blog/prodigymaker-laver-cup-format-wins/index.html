Laver Cup Format Wins!

By Chris Lewit · The Prodigy Maker (2020-05-30) · Original Article ↗

Love the Laver Cup format.

“Both singles and doubles are best of three sets with ad scoring. In the event of split sets, the third set is a 10-point match tiebreaker.”

2 sets and a tiebreak.

In general, tennis matches are way too long.

Love the on court coaching too. Very exciting and everyone wants to tune in and listen to the advice.

Maybe the slams stay the same for tradition and the rest of the year we switch to this scoring and coaching format?
