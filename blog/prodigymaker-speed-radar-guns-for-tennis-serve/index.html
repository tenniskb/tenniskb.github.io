Why Speed and Radar Guns Matter in Tennis

Measuring serve velocity and shot speed has become a critical part of modern tennis training. In Episode 85, host Chris Lewit delves into how radar guns are transforming player development by providing accurate data to analyze performance and set measurable goals.

What You’ll Discover in This Episode

  • The Latest Technology: Get a comprehensive overview of radar gun innovations and their growing impact on the game.
  • Practical Applications: Learn how to incorporate radar technology into your tennis training to track progress and improve serve efficiency.
  • Coaching Benefits: Understand how radar tools empower coaches to deliver data-driven feedback for player improvement.

Key Takeaways

  1. Maximize Serve Potential: Use radar guns to measure and fine-tune serve speed for peak performance.
  2. Objective Metrics: Rely on precise data to guide your training and evaluate progress.
  3. Next-Level Coaching: Leverage radar technology to offer targeted, effective coaching.
