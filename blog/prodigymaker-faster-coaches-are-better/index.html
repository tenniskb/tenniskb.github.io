Faster Coaches are Better

By Chris Lewit · The Prodigy Maker (2020-05-18) · Original Article ↗

How fast a coach can get a player to their highest level reflects on the coach’s talent and ability?

If one coach can maximize a player in 8 years and the other in 10 years, which coach is better?

Which coach is saving the parent more money by working more efficiently?

We shouldn’t accept slow development as the norm.

We should look for the fastest and most efficient method to develop a kid, always regarding the safety and health of him or her.
