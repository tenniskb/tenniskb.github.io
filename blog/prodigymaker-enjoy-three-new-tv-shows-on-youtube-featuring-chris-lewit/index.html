Enjoy three new TV shows on YouTube featuring Chris Lewit!

By Chris Lewit · The Prodigy Maker (2018-11-11) · Original Article ↗

Chris is excited to announce the production of three new programs on the Chris Lewit YouTube Channel.

Chris Lewit:  Prodigy Maker is a reality show that follows the life, work, and career of Chris Lewit, leading junior development coach, as he works to build the next great American player.  The show features different segments including interviews, video diaries, and live on court lessons with Chris.

Wake Up Live with Chris! is a fun and casual live broadcast with Chris in the early morning hours, NYC time.  Tune in for a morning tennis message from Chris giving his perspective on the world of tennis,  junior tennis, parenting, or high performance coaching, and more.   The show features a popular live Q and A lightning round from viewers.  Ask Chris any tennis question on your mind!

Q and A with Chris is a longer form live question and answer program where viewers can pose all their burning tennis questions to Chris.  Typically broadcast Sunday evenings NYC time.
