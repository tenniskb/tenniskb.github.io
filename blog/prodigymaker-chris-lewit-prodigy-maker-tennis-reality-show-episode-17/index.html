Chris Lewit Prodigy Maker Tennis Reality Show Episode 17

By Chris Lewit · The Prodigy Maker (2019-01-21) · Original Article ↗

Chris Lewit, Prodigy Maker

Episode 17 Chris Lewit Prodigy Maker Tennis Reality Show

Hang out with Chris on court with his little buddies!

Chris trains two superstar brothers, Rhys and Harrison, who are hard workers but sometimes act like knuckleheads! It’s an entertaining lesson.

Check out the world-class one hander conversion on the older player, about 12, which is one and half years into the conversion. It’s a beautiful stroke and very elastic.

Chris works on the racquet speed and elasticity of the younger brother Rhys, who is 10, while Harrison does cardio.

Chris finishes the lesson with some cool acceleration and transition to volley drills with a Spanish influence.

Rhys demonstrates his very good Floss technique to close the show!

One of Chris’s favorite tennis serve drills! Toss and Check!
