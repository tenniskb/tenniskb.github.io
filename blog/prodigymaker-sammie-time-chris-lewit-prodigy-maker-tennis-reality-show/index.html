By Chris Lewit · The Prodigy Maker (2019-02-07) · Original Article ↗

Sammie the Tennis Academy Dog

Sammie is the Lewit family dog. He is a rare American Hairless Terrier. Sammie is the official dog and mascot of the Chris Lewit Tennis Academy. If you come to CLTA summer high performance sleepaway camp in the Vermont Green Mountains, you can hang out with Sammie every day! He loves meeting new friends, treats, and tummy rubs.

Sammie has fast feet and is a tennis footwork expert. He is the cohost of Chris’s Talk Tennis Sunday night FB Live tennis show and podcast, but he mostly just naps during the show!
