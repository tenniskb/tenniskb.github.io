Live Interview with S&J Lessons About High Performance Tennis

By Chris Lewit · The Prodigy Maker (2020-05-09) · Original Article ↗

Chris had an opportunity to be interviewed by S&J Lessons.

S&J Lessons provides people with world-class information on tennis. They cover technical, tactical, mental, nutrition, strength and conditioning. 

“As a coach, I believe in the Spanish way. You gotta learn to suffer to be great. That’s probably one of my buzzwords that I talk a lot about with my kids. Building character.

Tennis sometimes reflects life. 

Tennis is a game of running. Run! Chase after the ball. It sounds simple, but we have many kids in the US who don’t want to run that much.

In tennis, you need to run. You need to be willing to run. You need to have discipline.

These are some of the things from Spain that I try to impress on my players.

You have to be patient and consistent.”
