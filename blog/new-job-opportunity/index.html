New Job Opportunity

By iTPA · iTPA Blog (2014-12-29) · Original iTPA article ↗

JOB OPPORTUNITY:

Great job opportunity with the iTF for a someone with an interest in online tennis education. The iTPA works very closely with the ITF and specifically the icoach to provide high quality coach education material.

Tennis iCoach is recruiting! Do you have the skill set to manage one of the world’s leading Coach Education websites? We are looking for an enthusiastic professional with a strong tennis background and the following qualifications, skills and attributes:

  • A University degree in the Sport Sciences (or similar)
  • A qualified tennis coach with experience
  • English as your native tongue
  • Proficient Spanish a strong bonus
  • An affinity for the world of digital and social media
  • Ready to start in January 2015. Location: Valencia, Spain.

If that sounds like you, we would love to hear from you. Email your CV and cover letter to [email protected] with the e-mail subject line “Tennis iCoach Coordinator: Application” by January 11th 2015. For a full description, click here: http://cms.itftennis.com/media/194350/194350.pdf
