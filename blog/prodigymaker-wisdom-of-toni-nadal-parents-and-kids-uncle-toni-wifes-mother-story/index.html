Wisdom of Toni Nadal: Parents and Kids. Uncle Toni’ Wife’s Mother Story

By Chris Lewit · The Prodigy Maker (2020-05-06) · Original Article ↗

“Parents tend to justify the actions of their own kids but judge the actions of other parents’ kids. They are quick to criticize the behavior of other kids, but slow to criticize their own.

If you brought my children up and I brought yours up, we would probably raise them in a much better way.”

—Toni Nadal
