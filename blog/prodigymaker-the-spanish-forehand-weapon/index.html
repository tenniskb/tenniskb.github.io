The Spanish Forehand Weapon

By Chris Lewit · The Prodigy Maker (2019-04-16) · Original Article ↗

The Lewitology: The Coaching Wisdom of Chris Lewit

“Spanish tennis training systems are known for developing great movers, consistent players, and supreme defenders. However, one area of development that is common all across Spain is the big, topspin forehand. Spanish players are steady, but they all learn how to dominate their opponents with big spin and power, especially on the forehand side. This is an important–but often overlooked–aspect of the Spanish approach.”
