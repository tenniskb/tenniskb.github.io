Kenin and the Early Specialization Myth

By Chris Lewit · The Prodigy Maker (2020-02-03) · Original Article ↗

“At 7 years old, Kenin reveals she was practicing three hours per day and her favorite player was Roddick.”

Just tired of hearing from the experts that early specialization is wrong and that parents shouldn’t coach their own kids.

While that’s not the only formula to get to the top—it often is. Early tennis and passionate parent coach.

Also, much respect to Rick Macci for helping another parent build a champion. He’s got the right philosophy when it comes to working with parents.
