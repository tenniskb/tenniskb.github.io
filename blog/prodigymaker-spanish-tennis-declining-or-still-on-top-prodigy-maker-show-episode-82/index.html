Spanish Tennis Declining or Still on Top? – Prodigy Maker Show Episode 82

By Chris Lewit · The Prodigy Maker (2024-11-03) · Original Article ↗

Chris Lewit · Spanish Tennis Declining or Still on Top? Reflections on Spanish Tennis – Prodigy Maker Show Ep 82

Is Spanish tennis declining, or is it still on top? In Episode 82 of the Prodigy Maker Show, Chris Lewit reflects on the current state of Spanish tennis after a recent trip to Barcelona. Chris explores the evolution of Spanish training, discusses the rise of new methods, and shares insights on rising stars like Carlos Alcaraz, the influence of science, and the role of high-tech academies.

  • How Spain is now known as a leading training ground for foreign players

  • Alcaraz and his development
  • The negatives of traditional Spanish training
