Advantage Canada has been produced to honour that incredible effort and trace the main features of the growth of our sport as it has occurred from Cornerbrook, Nfld., to Cowichan Bay, B.C. The common thread that runs through this rich tapestry is the Canadian championships; they have clearly been our international flagship during the first hundred years - and there is no doubt that they will remain so for many years to come. The characters on our stage are so numerous and the written records so scant that it has been impossible to definitively catalogue the contributions of every individual who participates as our story unfolds. History is necessarily selective and sometimes arbitrary, so kindly forgive us if, in some instances, we have failed to render the full significance of an individual's impact onour sport over these many years. We are delighted to present Advantage Canada as the first book of its kind published in this country. It's long overdue, and we hope it will stimulate others to expand on the visual and written record that we've created. There is always a different perspective and, thus, a new story to be told: with the tradition and heritage that so many people have built in the first hundred years, we believe that the next hundred may reveal the true promise of all that has gone before. On behalf of the Board of Directors, I'd like to extend my personal gratitude to all those individuals across the country who have contributed their valuable time to the development of tennis in Canada. Without that commitment, it is unlikely that our hundredth anniversary would hold the significance that it does. We thank you. Sincerely yours, Robert H. Wright, Q.c.
