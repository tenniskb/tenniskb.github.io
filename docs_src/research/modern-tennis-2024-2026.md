## 2. ITF / ATP rule changes 2024–2026

### Serve clock / shot clock

- **25-second serve clock** introduced at the 2018 US Open. Originally started when the chair umpire called the score.
- **2024 trial:** timer started **3 seconds after the previous point ends** instead of after the umpire's score call (Halle, Queen's, Roland Garros, Shanghai).
- **ATP unanimously approved the new auto-start rule at season start 2025** (previously on trial).
- **60-second changeover clock** is the standard ATP/WTA changeover allowance.
- **Doubles shot-clock = 15 s** (vs 25 s singles) — allowed one **10-second extension** per set.

### Electronic Line Calling (ELC) / Hawk-Eye Live rollout

| Year | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| 2021 | Australian Open — first Grand Slam to use Hawk-Eye Live on all courts, all matches |
| 2022 | US Open — fully replaces line judges with Hawk-Eye Live |
| 9 Oct 2024 | All England Club announces ELC will replace human line judges at Wimbledon starting 2025 (147-year tradition ended) |
| 2025+ | All four Grand Slams use Hawk-Eye Live ELC |

### Coaching rules

- **WTA** has allowed on-court coaching (with restrictions) since 2020.
- **ATP** "coaching rule" allows brief coach-to-player communication from the player's box only at changeovers/set breaks, never during play.
- **ATP coaching-corridor pilot** since 2024 in selected events (player can talk to coach through a designated channel).

### ATP Rulebook 2024 (Ch. 6) highlights

- Shot clocks **mandatory** at all ATP Tour events (250/500/1000/United Cup/Nitto ATP Finals)
- Two panels minimum, each 2×2 ft recommended (3×2 ft max), placed back-wall left and right from umpire chair
- Electronic review (challenge) systems mandatory on all non-clay ATP Tour events; voluntary on clay in 2024

### Quotes

> "The clock never stops. After the point is finished, the clock is putting on." — Carlos Alcaraz, Queen's Club, June 2024 (Reuters)

> "Without even notifying any players, they start this new rule. It's incredible honestly. I get it. You guys want to speed up the pace of the tennis match. But at the same time we've got to know if we're starting the season this way." — Novak Djokovic, Shanghai Masters 2024

> "You cannot be serious? Wimbledon abolishes line judges after 147 years." — Tumaini Carayol, *The Guardian*, 9 Oct 2024

## 3. Wheelchair tennis — Paris 2024 Paralympics

### Rules refresher

- **Two-bounce rule** — the ball is allowed to bounce twice before being returned; only the first bounce must land within the court
- **Two sport classes:**
  - **Open Division** — players with permanent physical disability resulting in substantial loss of function in one or more lower extremities. Men and women compete in **separate draws**.
  - **Quad Division** — Open-eligible players who also have substantial loss of function in one or both upper extremities, with at least three extremities affected. Men and women compete together in one draw.
- **Serve rule:** Server must be in a stationary position before serving; one push of the wheelchair is allowed immediately before the strike.

### Theme 1 — Open-stance, rotation-driven forehand.

The unit turn + hip-shoulder separation + back-leg drive is now treated as the default for adult players, replacing the 1980s closed-stance linear pattern. Cited by Top Tennis Training's 8-step forehand (Nov 2024, ~681K views) and reinforced by Sinner/Alcaraz/Draper modelling across all channels.
2. **Semi-Western to full-Western grip on the forehand** for serious players; the two-handed backhand remains dominant, with continental grip reserved for serve, volley, slice, overhead.
3. **Short, compact modern swing paths**, especially under pace. Sinner / Alcaraz / Draper "horizontal racket whip" examples dominate 2024–2026 analysis.
4. **Toss-based serve mechanics** — "toss fixes serve" is the most-clicked coaching thesis across channels (Essential Tennis, Top Tennis Training, Intuitive Tennis).
5. **Elastic, relaxed arm** + proximal-to-distal sequencing — explicitly opposed to "stiff, classical fundamentals."
6. **Decision-based, point-construction coaching** (Talk Tennis, Reddit r/10s, Mouratoglou) framing technique in service of match patterns, not the other way around.
