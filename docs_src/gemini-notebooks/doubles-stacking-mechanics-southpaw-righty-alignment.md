## 1. The Return Game Stacking Matrix

## 2. The Serving Stacking Cross

When serving from the 'wrong' side, the net partner crouches in the alley; as soon as the server strikes the ball, the net partner slides across the 'T' to assume their permanent post.

## 3. Avoiding Stacking Collision Protocols

Net player stays low and sprints on an outward diagonal; server runs behind the partner's hip to avoid court traffic.
