## Executive Abstract

The **Talocrural (Ankle Mortise) Joint** is the initial gatekeeper of kinetic energy transfer. During the sprint-deceleration-plant cycle, **30° of Ankle Dorsiflexion** pre-stretches the Achilles tendon. If talocrural joint stiffness is deficient (< 15° dorsiflexion mobility), the knee collapses into excessive flexion and the foot over-pronates, losing **35% of Vertical Ground Propulsion**.
