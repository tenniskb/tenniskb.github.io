### 1.1. Why Amateurs Fail: The "Chopping" Error
Amateurs telegraph the drop shot by slowing down their takeback or chopping down with a stiff wrist. Carlos Alcaraz swings with **maximum racket acceleration toward the ball**, then abruptly relaxes the grip and carves underneath at the microsecond of impact.
