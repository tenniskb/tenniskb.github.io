## Executive Abstract

Elite tennis ball-striking requires dynamic grip tension modulation: **2/10 Relaxation during Lag Drop transitioning to 8/10 Clamping in 15ms at Impact**. This rapid tension spike places immense hydraulic load on the **A2 and A4 Annular Flexor Pulleys** of the middle and ring fingers as the **Flexor Digitorum Profundus (FDP)** tendon attempts to bowstring under 500N collision forces. Proper grip size selection prevents pulley micro-tears.
