## Executive Abstract

Rushing youth players into early specialized tactical point play without fundamental athletic grounding leads to technical plateaus, chronic injuries, and psychological burnout. The **5-Stage Tennis LTAD Curriculum** (Balyi / Côté model) structures progressive 10-year development: Stage 1: Active Start & FUNdamentals (ages 6–9); Stage 2: Learning to Train & Movement Literacy (ages 9–12); Stage 3: Training to Train & Biotensegrity Architecture (ages 12–15); Stage 4: Training to Compete & Tactical Warfare (ages 15–18); Stage 5: Training to Win & ATP/WTA Mastery (18+).
