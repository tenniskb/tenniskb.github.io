## Executive Abstract

When recovering from extreme lateral corners, turning the entire body to run forward blinds the player to the opponent's next shot. The **Carioca Step (alternating cross-in-front and cross-behind footwork)** allows the pelvis to rotate dynamically while the thoracic spine, shoulders, and eyes remain locked facing forward toward the net, preserving tactical awareness during high-speed repositioning.
