### 1.1. Helical vs. Linear Movement
Linear movements (pushing forward) concentrate stress at individual joints (e.g., patellar tendon or rotator cuff). **Helical spiral movement (Chan Si Jin)** distributes mechanical load evenly along the entire 3-meter myofascial spiral chain, multiplying rotational torque while reducing peak joint strain by **up to 45%**.

### 2.1. The Principle of Non-Breaking Elasticity
In Chen Style Tai Chi, if silk is pulled too fast or jerked, it breaks; if pulled with slack, it tangles. In tennis, **Silk Reeling means maintaining continuous, unbroken elastic tension** throughout the takeback, racket drop, impact, and follow-through. The racket head is in perpetual, accelerating orbital motion.
