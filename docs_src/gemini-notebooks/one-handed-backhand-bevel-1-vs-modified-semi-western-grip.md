## Executive Abstract

The choice of grip on the single-handed backhand dictates the vertical racket face angle, permissible contact window, and topspin capacity. This whitepaper provides an engineering and orthopedic comparison between the **Classical Eastern Backhand Grip (Index base knuckle on Bevel 1)** and the **Extreme Semi-Western Backhand Grip (Index base knuckle on Bevel 8 / Bevel 1 border - Kuerten / Almagro style)**.

## 1. Eastern Backhand Grip (Bevel 1 - Federer Model)

Provides optimal horizontal power transfer and natural palm support on low-to-medium balls. Allows seamless transition to slice and volley without major grip shifts.

```
       [ Deep Knee Spring Drive ] ──► [ Scapular Retraction Anchor ]
```

---

## 2. Modified Semi-Western Backhand Grip (Bevel 8 - Thiem Model)

Closes the racket face by 15°, facilitating aggressive upward brushing on high-bouncing balls (+600 RPM topspin) but requiring contact 10cm further forward.

---

## 3. Grip Selection Decision Matrix

Fast hard/grass courts → Bevel 1 Eastern; Slow clay courts with high bounce → Bevel 8 Semi-Western.
