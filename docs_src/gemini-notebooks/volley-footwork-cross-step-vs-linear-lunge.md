## Executive Abstract

Volley execution depends entirely on footwork. In the **Classical 45° Cross-Step Lunge**, the player steps across with the opposite foot, channeling **100% of forward linear momentum** into deep punch volleys. In modern high-speed exchanges, the **Open-Stance Lateral Volley** plants the outside foot to absorb wide passing shots, allowing instant rebound recovery back to the center mark.

## Volley Diagnostic & Technical Remediation Matrix

| Biomechanical Parameter | Common Technical Fault | Clinical / Tactical Risk | Prescribed Intervention Protocol |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Backswing Control** | Stepping Backward on Volleys: Falling away or stepping backward while hitting volleys | Weak floating volleys and loss of net positioning | **Step Into the Ball: Always step forward or diagonally forward with positive aggressive intent.** |
| **Lower Body Sinking** | Bending at waist while keeping legs straight | Net dumps & severe lower back strain | **Drop Rear Knee**: Sink rear knee to 5cm above court; keep chest and head upright. |
| **Grip Tension Control** | Over-squeezing handle with 9/10 tension | Popping ball up deep for opponent putaways | **Feather Soft Hands**: Soften grip to 2/10 on impact to cushion drop volleys. |
