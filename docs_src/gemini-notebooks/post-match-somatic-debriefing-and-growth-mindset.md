## Executive Abstract

Evaluating match performance purely by whether you won or lost is **Outcome Bias**—it rewards bad tactics that won against weak opponents and punishes great tactics that lost by a fraction of an inch. Elite development requires the **24-Hour Objective Clinical Review Protocol**: waiting 24 hours for emotional cooling, analyzing video telemetry (1st-serve %, 0-4 shot dominance, target depth), and creating an actionable 3-point technical homework list.
