## Executive Abstract

Watching Rafael Nadal, Carlos Alcaraz, or Roger Federer at the baseline reveals continuous, high-frequency **Micro-Hops (3 to 5 hops per second)** between shots. Biomechanically, these micro-hops maintain continuous stretch-shortening cycle (SSC) pre-activation in the calves and Achilles tendons, keeping muscle spindles sensitive and eliminating the neuromuscular delay of starting from a static stance.
