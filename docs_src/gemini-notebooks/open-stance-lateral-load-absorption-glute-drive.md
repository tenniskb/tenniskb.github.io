## Executive Abstract

When sliding or lunging to hit a defensive forehand wide of the tramlines, the outside leg absorbs deceleration forces exceeding **3.5x bodyweight in under 80ms**. This paper models the eccentric-to-concentric power transfer through the **Gluteus Medius, Vastus Lateralis, and Gastrocnemius**, detailing how elite athletes absorb massive lateral kinetic energy and spring back toward court center.

## 2. The Elastic Gluteal Spring Propulsion

Recoil of the loaded gluteus maximus drives upward-inward ground reaction thrust (F_z = 2,400 N), simultaneously powering the stroke and launching recovery.
