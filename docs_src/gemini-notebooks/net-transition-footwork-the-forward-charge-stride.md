## Executive Abstract

## 1. The Forward-Moving Split-Step Geometry

Unlike a baseline split-step which lands in place, the transition split-step lands 0.5m forward, converting forward running inertia into balanced multi-directional agility.

## 2. Establishing the 'T' First-Volley Platform

Landing at the service line 'T' bisects the opponent's passing angles, cutting available passing width from 8.2m down to 2.4m.

## 3. Transition Footwork Drills

Approach-charge-split-volley timed circuits (target: 1.4 seconds from baseline to volley contact); reaction light net rushes.
