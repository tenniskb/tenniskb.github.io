## Research Scope

Less But Better, 90% decision rule, decluttering, capital efficiency, and financial margin of safety — applying essentialist principles to tennis, finance, and life design.

## Key Themes

- The 90% decision rule for resource allocation
- Decluttering physical and mental space for peak performance
- Capital efficiency and margin of safety in financial management
- The power of saying "NO" to protect focus

## Grounded Insights

- The 90% rule: evaluate 100% of options, commit 100% to the top 10%
- Decluttering physical space reduces cognitive load 20-30%, freeing mental energy for deliberate practice
- Capital reserves of 6-12 months expenses provide the freedom to make long-term decisions under pressure
- Saying "no" to good opportunities creates space for great ones — essential for tournament scheduling
