## Executive Abstract

The pervasive myth that a child must specialize exclusively in tennis by age 6–8 to become a champion is debunked by developmental sports science. Early single-sport specialization increases overuse injury risk by 2.4-fold and causes premature psychological burnout. Generational champions like Roger Federer (soccer, squash, badminton) and Rafael Nadal (soccer) practiced multi-sport sampling until age 12, building rich motor repertoires.

## 2. Pediatric Overuse Injury Mechanics

Repetitive unilateral overhead loading in children under 12 whose growth plates (epiphyses) are open causes irreversible shoulder and spinal adaptations. Multi-sport variety distributes load across diverse biological systems.

---

## 3. The LTAD 4-Stage Development Pathway

Stage 1 (Ages 6–10): Multi-Sport Fundamentals (70% other sports); Stage 2 (Ages 10–13): Tennis Focus with Secondary Sport; Stage 3 (Ages 14–17): Elite Tennis Specialization; Stage 4 (Age 18+): Professional Mastery.
