## Executive Abstract

In professional tennis, deceleration occurs in under 0.2 seconds, generating braking forces up to **4.2x bodyweight**. Improper braking mechanics (landing stiff-legged with knee over toe) concentrates catastrophic shear force on the patellar tendon, causing chronic tendinopathy ('Jumper's Knee'). This paper outlines the **3-Joint Deceleration Cascade** (ankle dorsiflexion -> knee flexion -> hip hinge) that absorbs shock safely.
