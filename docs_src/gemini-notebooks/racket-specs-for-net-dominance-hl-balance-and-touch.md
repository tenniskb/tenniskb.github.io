## Executive Abstract

Shifting racket mass toward the handle lowers swing inertia around the hand pivot, allowing instantaneous reflex positioning at the net.

## 2. High Twistweight (*I*~zz~) for Off-Center Shock Absorption

Lead tape at 3/9 o'clock prevents frame twisting when blocking heavy topspin drives on the edge of the sweet spot.

## 3. Volley Racket Customization Protocols

Adding handle counterweight (silicone / lead in trap door); polar lead tape application; diagnostic maneuverability testing.
