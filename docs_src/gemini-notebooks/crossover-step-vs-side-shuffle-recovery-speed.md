## Executive Abstract

Choosing the wrong recovery footwork costs 0.3 to 0.5 seconds of court repositioning time. Kinematic distance-velocity profiling establishes that for **Short Displacements (< 1.5 meters)**, the side-shuffle preserves forward vision and lateral balance. However, for **Deep Corner Retrievals (> 2.5 meters)**, the Crossover Step (crossing the outside leg in front of the torso) increases recovery velocity by **38%**, covering court distance with fewer strides.
