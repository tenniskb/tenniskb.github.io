## Executive Abstract

During rapid saccadic eye movements (> 400°/s), the brain temporarily shuts down visual input processing for 20–50 milliseconds (Saccadic Suppression) to prevent motion blur disorientation. This means a tennis player is biologically blind during the very saccade that jumps to the bounce point. ## 2. Trajectory Extrapolation & Predictive Landing

Because vision is suppressed during the jump, the saccade MUST land at the bounce location 100–150ms before the ball arrives. This allows the fovea to restation and reopen visual processing exactly as the ball collides with the court.
