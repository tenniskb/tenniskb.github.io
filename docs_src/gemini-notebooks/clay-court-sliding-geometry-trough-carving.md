## Executive Abstract

Sliding on red clay is a distinct aerodynamic and frictional discipline. With a friction coefficient of μ ≈ 0.45, red clay allows sustained **Trough Carving**: the player initiates the slide 1.5 meters BEFORE reaching the ball, sliding *into* the contact zone rather than sliding to stop. Rafael Nadal's 14 Roland Garros titles were anchored by this precise timing of sliding deceleration during ball impact.
