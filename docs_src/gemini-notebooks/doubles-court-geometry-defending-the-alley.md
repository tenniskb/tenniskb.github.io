## 1. The Low Mathematical Success of Down-the-Line Passes

A down-the-line pass must clear a net that is 6 inches higher at the posts (42 vs 36 inches) over a distance that is 4 feet shorter than crosscourt. Unforced error rates on this shot exceed 38%.

## 2. The Alley Baiting Stance

Stand with the outside foot 2 feet inside the singles sideline. This optical illusion makes the alley appear wide open, baiting the opponent into attempting a low-percentage drive.

---

## 3. The Alley Interception Punch

On ball flight recognition, take one sharp lateral crossover step, meeting the ball above net tape and punching it crosscourt into the open doubles alley.
