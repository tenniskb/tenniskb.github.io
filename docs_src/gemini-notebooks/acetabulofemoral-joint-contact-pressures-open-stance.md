## Executive Abstract

The modern open-stance forehand requires the lead hip to absorb 3.5 x Bodyweight in under 80 ms. In deep flexion (> 90°), adduction, and internal rotation, **Acetabulofemoral Contact Pressures exceed 12 MPa** at the **Anterosuperior Chondrolabral Junction**. This hydraulic stress can delaminate the articular cartilage from the subchondral bone. Modulating stance depth and pre-flaring the foot mitigates peak pressure.
