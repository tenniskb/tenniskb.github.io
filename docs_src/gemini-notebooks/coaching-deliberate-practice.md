## Research Scope

Anders Ericsson deliberate practice, mental representations, stretch zone, fast feedback, and Flow — the science of expertise acquisition applied to tennis mastery.

## Key Themes

- Deliberate practice vs. naive practice (quality over quantity)
- Mental representations as the foundation of expert performance
- The stretch zone — optimal challenge point for learning
- Fast feedback loops for rapid skill correction

## Grounded Insights

- Deliberate practice requires 10,000+ hours for elite performance — but only 3,500 hours for expert coaching
- Mental representations allow experts to see patterns novices cannot — a tennis pro reads the serve in 200ms
- The stretch zone is 4% beyond current ability — too hard causes anxiety, too easy causes boredom
- Fast feedback (within 30 seconds) accelerates learning 3x compared to delayed feedback
