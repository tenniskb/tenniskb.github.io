## Executive Abstract

Attacking short balls inside the baseline requires converting horizontal sprinting speed into forward ball propulsion without jamming the strike. The **Closed-to-Neutral Stance Transition** (exemplified by Roger Federer and Pete Sampras) utilizes a 45-degree forward step-in that channels linear running momentum directly through the contact plane, allowing the player to flow smoothly into the net.
