## Executive Abstract

A rising injury among two-handed players is **Non-Dominant Wrist Tendinopathy (Extensor Carpi Ulnaris tendinitis and TFCC tears in the left wrist)**. This occurs when players forcefully flick their left wrist upward to generate topspin without core support. This whitepaper establishes the **2HB Carpal Preservation Protocol**: replacing active left-wrist snapping with **Thoracic Core Uncoiling and Leg Drive**, shielding delicate carpal ligaments.
