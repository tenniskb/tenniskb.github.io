## Executive Abstract

Novice players take large, heavy strides that lock their feet in concrete before impact. Elite ball-strikers maintain a perpetual high-frequency **Hop-Step Rhythm** (3–5 micro-steps per second) between the split-step and final plant. This paper analyzes how micro-hops continuously recalibrate the striking envelope against erratic wind, spin, and bounce variables.

## 2. Distance Calibration via Micro-Hops

Micro-adjustments of 5–10 cm allow the player to position their center of mass at the exact ideal sweet-spot distance (60–75 cm from hip) regardless of ball skid.
