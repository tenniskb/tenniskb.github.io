## Executive Abstract

Wasting emotional energy on bad line calls, gusty winds, or lucky net cords guarantees match defeat. Applying **Stoic Equanimity & Epictetus's Dichotomy of Control**: divide every event into **Internals (Effort, Attitude, Stance, Breath)** and **Externals (Referee calls, Wind, Opponent luck)**. Surrendering emotional attachment to externals shields the player from frustration, maintaining an unshakeable psychological baseline.
