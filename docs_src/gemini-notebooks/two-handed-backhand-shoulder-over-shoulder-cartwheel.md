## Executive Abstract

Amateur two-handed backhands rotate horizontally like a baseball swing, causing balls to fly flat into the net or long over the baseline. Elite modern two-handers execute a **Shoulder-Over-Shoulder Vertical Cartwheel Inversion**: dropping the rear shoulder steeply during the drop and driving it high above the lead shoulder during the finish, creating a steep vertical swing plane that lifts low balls effortlessly.
