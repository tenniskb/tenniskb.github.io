## Executive Abstract

Single-handed backhand players face extreme vulnerability when facing 130 mph first serves to their backhand wing. Attempting a full topspin drive results in catastrophic late contact. Roger Federer and Stan Wawrinka solved this via the **One-Handed Chip-Block**: gripping with a strict **Continental Grip (Bevel 2)**, pinching the right scapula isometrically, and presenting a firm, immovable racket blade to chip the ball deep into the center court.
