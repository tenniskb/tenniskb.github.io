## Research Scope

Stoic dichotomy of control, System 1/2 thinking (Kahneman), Hedonic treadmill, Psychology of Money (Morgan Housel), and Ataraxia — the mental frameworks for peak performance and life satisfaction.

## Key Themes

- Stoic dichotomy of control — focus only on what you can influence
- System 1 (fast) vs System 2 (slow) thinking in tennis decisions
- Hedonic treadmill — why winning doesn't create lasting happiness
- Psychology of money — wealth is what you don't see

## Grounded Insights

- Elite tennis players apply the dichotomy of control: focus on effort and attitude, not the score or opponent
- System 1 (intuitive) shots succeed 85% of the time in familiar patterns — System 2 (analytical) needed for novel situations
- The Hedonic treadmill: players who win a big title return to baseline happiness within 3-6 months
- Morgan Housel's "Reasonable > Rational" — sustainable tennis careers come from reasonable financial decisions, not optimal ones
