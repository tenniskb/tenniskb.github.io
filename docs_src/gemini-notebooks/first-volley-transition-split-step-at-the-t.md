## Executive Abstract

The transition from baseline to net is won or lost on the **First Volley Split-Step**. When advancing behind an approach shot or serve, the player must execute a **Forward Moving Split-Step exactly as the opponent strikes the ball**, landing balanced at the service line 'T'.
