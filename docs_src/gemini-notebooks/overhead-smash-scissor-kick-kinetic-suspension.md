## Executive Abstract

When an opponent hits a high defensive lob while you are at the net, backpedaling and hitting flat-footed causes balance loss and netted smashes. The **Scissor-Kick Overhead Smash** (immortalized by Pete Sampras's 'Slam Dunk') utilizes an explosive single-leg jump off the rear foot, arching the torso in mid-air and kicking the legs in a scissor motion to conserve angular momentum and smash downward at over 100 mph.

## 1. The Airborne Scissor-Kick Angular Counter

As the hitting arm snaps forward-downward, the rear right leg kicks backward and the left leg kicks forward in mid-air, providing the equal and opposite angular counter-impulse that prevents torso over-rotation.
