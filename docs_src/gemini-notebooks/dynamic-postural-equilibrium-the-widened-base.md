## Executive Abstract

Classical tennis taught players to stand with feet shoulder-width apart. Modern hyper-speed tennis mandates the **Widened Base-of-Support Law**: setting the feet at **1.4 to 1.6x Biacromial (shoulder) Width**. This widened triangle dramatically lowers the center of gravity, expanding the stability margin to withstand 85 mph baseline impacts without tipping.
