## Executive Abstract

Trying to suppress anxiety (*'Don't be nervous'*) creates paradoxical thought rebound (**Wegner's Ironic Process Theory**), worsening performance. **Acceptance and Commitment Training (ACT)** teaches athletes **Cognitive Defusion**: accepting butterflies and tension as normal evolutionary readiness (*'My body is preparing to fight'*), while maintaining 100% committed action toward tactical values regardless of internal emotions.
