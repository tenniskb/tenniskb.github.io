## Executive Abstract

When a deep lob is drifting past the baseline and there is no time to execute a full scissor jump, Jimmy Connors pioneered the **Skyhook Overhead Smash**. Reaching straight upward with a fully extended arm and Continental blade, the player executes a **Lateral Pronation Hook**, deflecting the ball on a high-skidding diagonal drive that neutralizes the lob and keeps the player in the point.
