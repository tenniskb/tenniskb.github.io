## Executive Abstract

Neurobiologist Wolfgang Schöllhorn's **Differential Learning** model proves the opposite: the brain learns faster when exposed to **continuous, stochastic movement variations** (hitting forehands with one eye closed, on one leg, with knees locked, with different rackets).
