## Executive Abstract

Aimless serving without target clustering produces low-percentage aces and easy returns. Strategic serve placement utilizes the **3 Target Cluster Matrix**: (1) **The Center 'T'** (attacks the opponent's backhand and eliminates return angles), (2) **The Jamming Body** (targets the dominant hip to prevent arm extension), and (3) **The Wide Slider** (pulls the defender 3m off court to open the opposite court for a Serve+1 winner).

## 1. Center 'T' Angle Compression (< 14°)

Serving down the 'T' gives the returner no angle to work with, forcing returns back down the middle for easy forehand putaways.

## 2. Jamming Body Geometry

Aiming at the returner's dominant hip catches them between forehand and backhand, producing weak floating returns.
