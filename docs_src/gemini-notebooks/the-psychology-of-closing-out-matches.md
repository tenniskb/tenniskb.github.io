## Executive Abstract

Serving for the match at 5-4 in the final set is the most perilous psychological moment in tennis. As the brain visualizes victory, heart rate spikes and players succumb to **'The Hope Trap'**—playing passively and hoping the opponent misses. Closing out matches requires **Proactive Finishing Aggression**: attacking with 1st-serve placement, stepping inside the baseline on ball 2, and driving through the finish line with complete authority.
