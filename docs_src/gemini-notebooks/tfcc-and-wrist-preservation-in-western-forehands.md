## Executive Abstract

Extreme Western and Semi-Western forehands place heavy mechanical stress on the **Triangular Fibrocartilage Complex (TFCC)** and Extensor Carpi Ulnaris (ECU) tendon during high-speed wrist lag and pronation. This whitepaper establishes the **TFCC Preservation Protocol**: replacing active carpal ulnar snapping with **Isometric Forearm Co-Contraction** and forward leg drive, eliminating wrist shear forces.

## 1. Pathomechanics of TFCC Overload

Striking the ball late with an excessively extended, ulnar-deviated wrist concentrates 350N impact shock directly onto the fragile TFCC disc.

## 2. Forward Contact & Isometric Wrist Locking

Hitting 40cm in front of the hip aligns the forearm bones parallel to the impact force, dissipating shock safely through the elbow and shoulder.

---

## 3. TFCC Rehabilitation & Conditioning Protocols

Tyler Twist FlexBar resistance; isometric wrist flexion/extension holds; forward contact reach calibration.

## Forehand Diagnostic & Technical Remediation Matrix

| Biomechanical Parameter | Common Technical Fault | Clinical / Tactical Risk | Prescribed Intervention Protocol |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wrist Lag Execution** | Actively forcing wrist extension | Extensor tendinopathy & TFCC tear | **Passive Inertial Lag**: Relax grip to 2/10; let racket drop under gravity. |
| **Pelvic Sequencing** | Spinning hips continuously through swing | Weak 'slapping' arm contact | **Lead Hip Braking**: Abruptly halt pelvic rotation at 45° to whip arm. |
| **Contact Geometry** | Hitting ball late (behind hip line) | Shoulder strain & ball sailing long | **45cm Forward Contact**: Step into open/neutral stance 40cm ahead of hip. |
