## 1. Kinetic Yielding & Impulse Dissipation

## 2. Carving the Outside Hemisphere for Dead Spin

Brushing underneath and around the side of the ball imparts 2,800 RPM of combined backspin and sidespin, causing the ball to hook sideways toward the doubles net post upon bouncing.

## 3. Drop Volley Disguise & Execution Gate
