## Executive Abstract

The **Change-of-Direction (COD) Deficit** measures the time difference between linear sprinting and 180-degree directional reversals. Elite defenders (Djokovic, Alcaraz, Medvedev) minimize this deficit by employing a **Penultimate Braking Stride**: the second-to-last foot plant absorbs 60% of forward momentum, allowing the final plant foot to hit at a clean 45° angle with instant torso inversion.

## 2. 45° Plant Angle & Center of Mass Lowering

The final plant foot angles 45° inward; the torso leans 30° back toward court center, redirecting momentum before the stroke is even completed.
