## Executive Abstract

Hitting overhead smashes aimlessly into the deep court allows fast defenders to guess and retrieve them. Elite smash execution uses **2 High-Probability Target Clusters**: (1) **The Downward Sneaker Strike** (aiming steeply at the opponent's feet where reaction time is < 120 ms), and (2) **The Angled Grandstand Bounce** (cracking 35° crosscourt to bounce the ball high over the side fencing).
