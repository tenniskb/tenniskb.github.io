## Executive Abstract

Tennis footwear is a specialized traction interface. On hard courts, **Modified Herringbone with Dense Rubber Compounds (Durometer 65–70A)** provides durable multi-directional grip while allowing controlled slides. On clay, **Full Deep Herringbone (100% 120° grooves)** expels crushed brick to prevent clumping. On grass, **3mm Pimpled Studs** penetrate turf blades to guarantee anti-slip mechanical interlocking.

## 2. Clay-Court Herringbone Expulsion Mechanics

Deep 120-degree zig-zag grooves channel crushed brick outward during foot compression, preventing clay build-up that turns shoes into slick skates.

---

## 3. Outsole Selection & Maintenance Guide

Matching tread patterns to court CPI; monitoring tread wear depth (replace when groove depth < 1.0mm).
