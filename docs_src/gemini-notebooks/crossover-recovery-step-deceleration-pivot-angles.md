## Executive Abstract

When recovering from three meters outside the doubles alley, taking side-shuffle steps is mechanically too slow to cover the open court. The Crossover Step—crossing the outside leg aggressively over the lead leg—harvests ground reaction braking forces and converts rotational angular momentum into linear sprinting speed back to the center hashmark.

## 2. Crossover vs. Shuffle Decision Boundary

Displacement > 2.5 meters → Immediate Crossover Step. Displacement < 1.5 meters → Side-Shuffle Step to maintain chest orientation to opponent.
