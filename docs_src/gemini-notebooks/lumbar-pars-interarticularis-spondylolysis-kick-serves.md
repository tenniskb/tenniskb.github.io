## Executive Abstract

Lumbar spondylolysis (stress fracture of the **Pars Interarticularis at L4-L5**) is the most common cause of structural back pain in tennis. Executing the **Kick Serve** by hyperextending and laterally flexing the lumbar spine compresses the posterior facet columns under **> 4,000 N of axial shear load**. Remediation requires replacing spinal hyperextension with **115° Knee Flexion Spring** and conditioning the **McGill Big 3 Core Stability Engine**.
