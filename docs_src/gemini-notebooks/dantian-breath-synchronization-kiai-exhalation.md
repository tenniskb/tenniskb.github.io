### 1.1. Lowering the Center of Gravity
When an athlete is nervous, breathing shifts into the upper chest, elevating the center of mass (CoM) and creating an unstable, top-heavy base. By anchoring breathing into the **Lower Dantian**, the CoM drops closer to the court, maximizing ground reaction force transmission and rooting the split-step.

### 2.1. Dynamic Core Stiffening Without Limb Rigidity
Acoustic vocalization forces the vocal cords and diaphragm to contract for a fraction of a second, locking the lumbar spine in perfect stability while allowing the peripheral hitting arm to remain loose and elastic.
