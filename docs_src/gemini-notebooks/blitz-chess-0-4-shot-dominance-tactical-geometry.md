## 2. The Serve + 1 & Return + 1 Execution Blueprints


### 2.1. The Serve + 1 Playbook
1. **Target A**: Serve out wide on Deuce court → Inside-in Forehand to open backhand corner.
2. **Target B**: Body-T serve on Ad court → Inside-out Forehand to opponent's backhand.
3. **The Rule**: The server must hunt the forehand on Ball 3 regardless of court position.

### 2.2. The Return + 1 Playbook
- **Neutralizing Serve Power**: Block/chip return deep through the middle 2 meters (eliminates sharp server angles).
- **Attacking Second Serves**: Step 1 meter inside baseline, strike deep crosscourt, and immediately advance inside the baseline for Ball 4.

### 3.1. The Law of the Crosscourt Channel
- The diagonal dimension of the court is **4.5 feet longer** than down-the-line, and passes over the lowest part of the net (36 inches at the strap).
