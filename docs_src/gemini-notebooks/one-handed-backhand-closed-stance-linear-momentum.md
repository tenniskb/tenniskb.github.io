## Executive Abstract

Unlike the two-handed backhand which thrives in wide open stances, the **Single-Handed Backhand requires a Closed or Neutral Stance on 90% of topspin drives**. Stepping across with the front right foot creates the necessary pelvic lock that enables the straight-arm lever to accelerate freely down the target line without premature chest opening.

## 1. The 45-Degree Closed Stance Step-In

The lead right foot steps across the body at a 45° angle, loading the front quadriceps and locking the hips perpendicular to the baseline.

## 2. Linear Momentum Transfer Along Shot Axis

As the racket swings forward, body mass transfers 100% onto the front foot (*m* *v*~body~), adding linear plowing power to the stroke.

---

## 3. The Scissor Recovery Hop

Immediately following contact, the rear left leg swings backward in a dynamic scissor motion to absorb forward momentum and initiate recovery.
