## Executive Abstract

Lateral epicondylalgia is not an acute inflammatory condition, but **Angiofibroblastic Hyperplasia (Failed Healing Tendinosis)** of the **Extensor Carpi Radialis Brevis (ECRB)** origin. Biomechanical faults (off-center hitting, bent-elbow one-handed backhands, and rigid polys) create eccentric overload. The gold-standard rehabilitation is the **Tyler Twist with a TheraBand FlexBar (3 sets of 15 eccentric repetitions daily)**, stimulating collagen type I cross-linking and tenocyte remodeling.
