## Executive Abstract

Judging the depth and velocity of a 90 mph dipping drive at the net requires **Stereoscopic Binocular Disparity**: the brain calculates the angular disparity between the images projected onto the left and right retinas. Operating in tandem with David Lee's **Tau Theory (rate of optical expansion on the retina)**, the visual cortex calculates time-to-collision with sub-millisecond precision.
