## Executive Abstract

Pioneered by Novak Djokovic and perfected by Carlos Alcaraz and Jannik Sinner, the **Hard-Court Slide** has revolutionized baseline defense. By entering the lateral plant at an angle of 45° with specialized outsoles, the player forces the shoe-court interface to transition from static friction (μ_s ≈ 0.85) to kinetic sliding friction (μ_k ≈ 0.58), gliding 0.8 to 1.4 meters to strike the ball before recovering instantly.

## 2. Ankle Inversion Injury Prevention (45° Rule)

The lead foot must flare outwards at 45° with the medial edge bearing load; landing with a 90° perpendicular foot causes catastrophic subtalar inversion sprains.

## 3. Hard-Court Slide Progression Drills

Friction mat sliding transitions; low-angle resistance band lateral lunges; progressive deceleration slide-to-hit drills.
