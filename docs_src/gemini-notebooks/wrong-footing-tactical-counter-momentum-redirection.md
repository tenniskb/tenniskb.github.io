## Executive Abstract

When a fast defender sprints to retrieve a wide shot, they immediately initiate an aggressive crossover recovery sprint back toward the center mark. If you hit into the open court, the defender runs straight onto the ball. The master tactic is **The 'Wrong-Footing' Principle**: striking the ball directly behind the defender back into the corner they just vacated. Newton's First Law makes it physically impossible for them to halt their momentum and reverse direction in time.
