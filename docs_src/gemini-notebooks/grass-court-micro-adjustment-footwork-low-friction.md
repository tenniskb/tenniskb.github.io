## Executive Abstract

Grass is the fastest and lowest-bouncing tennis surface (CPI > 45, rebound angle < 12°). Sliding on grass results in catastrophic groin and hamstring tears. Grass court mastery requires **High-Frequency Chop Steps (4–6 steps/meter)** and maintaining a Center of Mass lower than 65cm off the turf, distributing bodyweight across multi-studded outsoles to prevent slipping.

## 1. The High-Frequency Micro-Chop Step

Replacing long lunges with rapid, short chop steps keeps the center of mass within the base of support at all times, preventing unexpected turf slips.

## 2. Lowering Center of Mass (< 65cm)

Bending both knees to 110° places the pelvis in a continuous low crouch, enabling instant adjustment to irregular turf bounces.

---

## 3. Grass Court Agility Drills

Low-crouch agility cone zig-zags; rapid chop-step deceleration ladders; short-bound recovery hops.
