## Executive Abstract

When facing a break point, amateur players perceive a 'Threat State' (fear of losing, defensive push, high cortisol). ## 2. Proactive Aggression on Break Points

Championship data shows that players who hit with positive forward racket speed on break points win 64% of those points, compared to only 28% for players who play passively to avoid mistakes.
