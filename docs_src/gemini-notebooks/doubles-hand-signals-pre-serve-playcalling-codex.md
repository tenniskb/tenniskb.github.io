## 1. The 4 Fundamental Movement Signals

• **Index Finger (1)**: Poach aggressively across the center.
• **Closed Fist (0)**: Stay on your own side.
• **Thumb Out / Shaka**: Fake poach (feint across and return).
• **Open Hand (5)**: Pinch the middle seam together.

## 2. The Secondary Target Codex

Target is signaled simultaneously: Pointing left → Serve out Wide; Pointing down → Serve to the 'T'; Pointing right → Jam the Body.

## 3. Signal Security & Anti-Decoding Measures

Hiding hands beneath shorts hemline; using double-tap confirmations; changing signals after every broken service game.
