## Executive Abstract

In singles, overhead smashes are often directed into open corners. In doubles, hitting toward the open corners gives fast defenders time to retrieve the ball off the bounce. The mathematically lethal doubles smash targets are: (1) **The Opposing Net Player's Feet** (zero reaction time), and (2) **The Middle Seam** (inducing partner collisions).
