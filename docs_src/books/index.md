## How the PDF reader works

The reader uses the browser's built-in PDF viewer (`<embed type="application/pdf">` wrapped in `<iframe>`). The toolbar at the top lets you:

- **Zoom in/out** and **fit-to-width**
- **Navigate** with the page list on the left
- **Search** within the book (Ctrl+F)
- **Print** or **download**

No external PDF.js dependency, no separate window, no plugin. Works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari. The page renders at **100 % width** of your browser viewport — for larger text, use the browser's zoom (Ctrl++) or the PDF viewer's own zoom.
