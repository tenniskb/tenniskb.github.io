IMG Tennis Academy (Nick Bollettieri Coaching Series)

Total Videos: 97

IMG Academy Tennis Program — founded by legendary Hall of Fame coach Nick Bollettieri — is the world’s premier tennis training institution, having developed champions including Andre Agassi, Monica Seles, Jim Courier, Maria Sharapova, and Kei Nishikori. Visit official YouTube channel for more.

About the Channel

This authentic instructional collection features Nick Bollettieri’s world-renowned High Performance Teaching Series, How to Play Tennis stroke fundamentals, Court Positioning mastery, Offensive Attacking, Defensive Tennis, and Tennis Power Training.
