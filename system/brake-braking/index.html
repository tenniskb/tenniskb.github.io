    BRAKE - Braking¶

    The 1-Inch Pulse.

    The Principle¶

    Power was never about how far you swing. It's impulse — a short time carrying massive force. Braking IS the power.

    The Physics¶

    Impulse = Force × Time

    To maximize impulse with minimal time: 1. Accelerate the body (build momentum) 2. BRAKE suddenly (stop the chassis) 3. Energy transfers to the free end (racket)

    Like cracking a whip: handle stops → tip explodes.

    The Two Brakes¶

    Stroke Brake Point What Stops
    Forehand Front leg + front hip Torso rotation
    Backhand Front leg + chest (side-on) Chest rotation

    The Sequence¶

    Phase Action Feeling
    Load Small takeback, coil Stretch in spiral/back line
    Accelerate Hips/legs initiate Body moves as unit
    BRAKE Front leg STOMPS, hip/cheat stops Sudden hardness
    PULSE Arm/racket whips through One-inch push

    The Two Errors¶

    Error What Happens Fix
    No brake Long swing, arm-only, no pop Front leg stops HARD
    Early brake Push, no whip Brake AT contact, not before

    The Pulse¶

    Stroke Pulse Direction Distance Peak Force
    Forehand Palm pushes THROUGH 1 inch 30ms peak
    Backhand Edge slices THROUGH 3 inches 30ms peak

    Never a long follow-through. The racket finishes high, but YOU stopped the motion.

    The Drill¶

    "Wall Push Pulse"

    1. Stand 1 inch from wall, racket butt touching
    2. Palm behind racket, no takeback
    3. Push wall — feel spiral line fire from foot to palm
    4. Release instantly
    5. No swing. Just push.

    Then: Shadow swing → brake → pulse. Feel the stop → snap.

    The Cue¶

    "Brake hard. Pulse one inch. The racket does the rest."
