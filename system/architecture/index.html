The Eight Layers

Layer 8 (Peak): COGNITIVE DECISION

  • Label: Decision
  • Concept: Hư Thức, Strategy, Play Plan
  • Action: Formulate tactical decisions, create internal plan
  • Flow direction: Bidirectional (top of tower)

Layer 7: VISUAL-VESTIBULAR

  • Label: Eye Control
  • Concept: Q-Focus, Q-Intensity, Quiet Eye
  • Action: Filter visual info, stabilize gaze (Hold +0.1s)

Layer 6: SOMATIC SENSATION

  • Label: Body Feel
  • Concept: Root Centering, Body Awareness
  • Action: Establish internal balance, manage physical presence

Layer 5: STRUCTURAL ALIGNMENT

  • Label: Core Stability
  • Concept: 45°-Alignment, Rotational Axis
  • Action: Ensure biomechanical integrity, manage posture

Layer 4: DYNAMIC MOTION

  • Label: Force Generation
  • Concept: 8-Rotation, Loop Scale
  • Action: Convert physical force to shot power
  • Icon: Circular rotation

Layer 3: IMPULSE TIMING

  • Label: Decisive Moment
  • Concept: Brake Intensity, Pulse Timing
  • Action: Regulate loop scale and speed, master contact (0ms)
  • Icon: Lightning bolt

Layer 2: EXPRESSION (JIN)

  • Label: Internal Power
  • Concept: Jin Internal Power, Release
  • Action: Project force into shot, complete loop (Release +0.25s)
  • Icon: Flame

Layer 1 (Base): RHYTHMIC FOOTWORK

  • Label: Foundation
  • Concept: Unit Turn, Timing, Step Patterns
  • Action: Step and synchronize prep (Unit Turn -0.2s)
  • Icon: Braided rope base

Flow Directions

↑ Information / Force (UP)

Force originates at the foundation (footwork) and propagates upward through the layers:

FOOTWORK → JIN → IMPULSE → MOTION → ALIGNMENT → SENSATION → VISION → DECISION

↓ Gaze / Decision (DOWN)

Conscious decision and gaze direction (Quiet Eye) cascade downward to direct each lower layer of execution:

DECISION → VISION (Quiet Eye) → SENSATION → ALIGNMENT → MOTION → IMPULSE → JIN → FOOTWORK

Why This Architecture Matters

Most tennis instruction treats strokes as isolated technique. This tower shows that every stroke is the simultaneous expression of all eight layers. A forehand isn't just an arm motion — it's the visible output of an integrated mind-body system.

Break one layer, break all strokes. That's the diagnostic principle behind the CORE Diagnostic and the Fault Diagnosis Master Flowchart.
