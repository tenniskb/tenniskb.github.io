Elevate Your Tennis Game — Learn from Champion Andre Agassi

Andre Agassi

Topic: Overview

The flagship lesson from the Elevate Your Tennis Game series with Andre Agassi. A brief, high-level overview designed to set the tone and introduce the coaching philosophy that runs through every drill in this library.

Andre Agassi is one of the greatest tennis players of all time — eight Grand Slam singles titles, six ATP Masters titles, an Olympic gold medal, and a career Golden Slam. Known for his return of serve and one of the most consistent groundstrokes in the history of the game, Agassi played professional tennis for more than two decades (1986–2006) and was inducted into the International Tennis Hall of Fame in 2011. After retiring, he has stayed involved in the game as a coach and mentor, with these lessons capturing some of the most important lessons Agassi wants to share with the next generation of players.
