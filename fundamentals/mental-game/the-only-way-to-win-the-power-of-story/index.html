The Only Way to Win: The Power of Story

Jim Loehr

There is the real world. Then there is our version of the real world. And that version is our story. Jim Loehr has spent many years listening to players and helping them realign and reshape their stories. Time and time again, he has found that creating new mental architecture inspires players and creates pathways to reach their goals. Learn to write your current story and then write your new story in your own language to develop the forehand you want, or to overcome choking, or become a great wind player.
